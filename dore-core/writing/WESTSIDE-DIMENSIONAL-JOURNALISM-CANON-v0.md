# Westside Dimensional Journalism / 西望立體新聞寫作 Canon v0

Status: FOUNDATION CANDIDATE
Capability ID: `writing.westside-dimensional-journalism`
Owner: Westside Watch / Doré Core

## Purpose

This capability preserves and grows Westside's writing method as a versioned, testable editorial capability rather than chat memory or surface imitation. It serves Journal, The Gate, Doré Folio, book publishing and long-form biblical/theological nonfiction.

It does not replace the author's thesis. The author remains authority over thesis, discovery and intended meaning. Doré deepens, researches, edits, tests and proposes; it must not silently normalize the author into a generic house voice.

## Governing rule

Westside does not use "less" as a style ideology. Fewer adverbs, connectors or structural particles are useful only when they remove needless mediation. Adjectives, repetition, long sentences, rhetorical density and poetic force may be abundant when they arise from material, add dimension, carry perception or create earned tension.

Literary force may intensify evidence. It may never purchase permission to invent evidence.

## Nine foundation layers

### GROUND / 落地
Enter through a real scene, person, text, place, object or event rather than an abstract conclusion. Scene construction must remain evidence-grounded.

### DEPTH / 下潛
Do not rush from a concrete thing to an analogy. Write the thing accurately and deeply enough for larger meaning to emerge. Reality is not an illustration pasted onto Scripture; Scripture and present reality may meet only where a defensible structural relation exists.

### POSITION / 位置
The author may be present. The author must know where they stand, what they witnessed, what the sources permit them to know, and where they were not present. First person is a viewing position, not automatic authority.

### MOVEMENT / 運動
A paragraph is a movement of thought, not a fixed sentence count. Narrative, fact, tension, context and judgment may move within one paragraph. Judgment should often grow from narration rather than arrive as a detached slogan.

### ALTITUDE / 高度
Writing may move among detail, person, text, history, social structure and theology. The shift in altitude should be earned and need not be announced. A person may become an aperture through which a larger structure becomes visible.

### LANGUAGE / 語言
Prefer working nouns and verbs over explanatory padding. Audit `的 / 地 / 得`, adverbs and connectors by function, never by quota. Time should normally be carried by events, causality by relations, turns by meaning and progression by accumulated material. Use connectors when they add precision rather than merely administer prose.

Adjectives are not to be minimized. Their force depends on dimension, distribution, evidence and semantic pressure. A strong adjective may add time, space, body, class, memory, history, ethics or theological position. Do not stack adjectives merely to simulate literary atmosphere.

### PROSODY / 韻律
Poetry enters prose beneath the surface. Rhythm must carry perception, not merely produce punch. Sentence length can alter experienced time; punctuation follows thought and breath; repetition must accumulate meaning; motifs may return as a bass line. Prose remains prose: do not expose poetic machinery through habitual one-sentence paragraphs.

A useful rhythm may be rejected when it contains no new seeing. `第八天，約伯開口。` is technically effective but insufficient by itself as a Westside solution: it closes rhythmically without necessarily carrying perception.

Semantic pressure matters more than ornamental intensity. A plain word may become charged because the preceding prose has prepared it.

### EMERGENCE / 生長
Deep writing discovers ore veins. A vein may return to the mother article, become an independent article, or surface through sound, image, map, manuscript, video or another publication surface. Do not pre-split a mother article into a predetermined content grid. Derived work emerges from actual depth.

### TRUTH / 真實
Evidence roles remain distinct: author judgment, biblical text, original language, historical evidence, scholarship, tradition, editorial inference and sensory extension must not impersonate one another. Literary scene construction cannot add weather, gesture, psychology, dialogue or sensory facts that the evidence does not provide.

## Chinese prose constraints

1. Do not treat short sentences as depth. Avoid habitual one-sentence-per-line cadence.
2. Reduce administrative connectors such as `然後 / 所以 / 但是 / 然而 / 此外 / 與此同時 / 因此 / 值得注意的是 / 由此可見` when the relation already exists in event order, syntax or meaning.
3. Preserve connectors when removing them would reduce logical, temporal or evidentiary precision.
4. Remove an adverb when the verb already carries its work; strengthen or reorganize the verb before searching for a stronger adverb.
5. `的 / 地 / 得` are not banned. Audit modifier compression, unnecessary adverb marking and over-explained degree.
6. Do not use a global adjective-reduction rule. Prefer dimensional and consequential description; distribute description across the sentence/paragraph rather than hanging every property before one noun.
7. Repetition is not automatically redundancy. A repeated word must return changed, carrying accumulated meaning or rhythm.
8. Poetic rhythm may use recurrence, semantic rhyme, motif, parallelism, breath, silence and kinetic arcs, but should remain embedded in normal prose.
9. High-density rhetoric is permitted when the source material provides an ignition point. Restraint governs unearned density, not density itself.
10. Prefer low-temperature surface with high-tension interior when the material calls for it: do not announce emotion that action, detail, relation and semantic pressure can make present.

## Acceptance philosophy

Do not score prose by keyword counts alone. A passage may contain many adjectives or repetitions and pass; another may contain none and fail. Evaluation asks what each choice does.

Required axes:
- author thesis preserved;
- evidence boundary preserved;
- perception present, not rhythm alone;
- paragraph movement intelligible;
- language mediation justified;
- adjectives add dimension or necessary perception;
- repetition accumulates meaning;
- connectors perform necessary relational work;
- poetic rhythm remains internal to prose;
- no fabricated scene detail;
- derived ore veins are recorded without forcing premature structure.

## Foundation research lineage

v0 is informed by continuing study of Western literary journalism / narrative nonfiction (scene, action, detail, point of view and factual boundary), Huang Renyu / Ray Huang's `1587, A Year of No Significance` (small aperture to large structure, person-system relation and narrative judgment), and modern Chinese prose/poetics research concerning paragraph movement, sound, repetition, adjective pressure and prose rhythm.

Research remains open. This canon is a foundation checkpoint, not a declaration that Westside style research is complete.
