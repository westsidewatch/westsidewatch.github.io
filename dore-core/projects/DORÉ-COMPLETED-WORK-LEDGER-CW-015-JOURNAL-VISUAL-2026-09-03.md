## CW-015 — August 2026 Journal / Vol.00 visual milestone

**Current classification:** `COMPLETED_REVISIT_CANDIDATE` for the bounded August 2026 Journal / Vol.00 visual milestone. `MAIN` remains `MAINTENANCE`; `VIS-GRAMMAR` remains `ACTIVE_PARALLEL / BUILDING`.

**Original objective**
Establish a recognizable Westside Journal publication object and continuous night-to-dawn visual narrative across Vol.00, using a stable bilingual editorial hierarchy, temple-stone / city-wall material language, First Light Gold, publication ratios and a staged opening-to-Contents light journey rather than a generic church-site treatment.

**Current quality judgment**
The milestone remains coherent and production-worthy as a historical baseline. Its strongest durable traits are the night→dawn→great-light narrative, temple-stone / city / wall symbolism, restrained sequencing of the major light transition, the 5:8 editorial image-window language and the formal bilingual publication hierarchy. However, by current standards it still relies too heavily on large dark fields, gradients, stone photography/texture and geometric light treatment. It predates the explicit requirement for a purpose-built Doré-derived website asset family, so it should remain a control rather than the final shared ecosystem grammar.

**What Doré learned / retained**
- stage the strongest visual transformation instead of repeating it on every section;
- treat stone, city/gate/wall, light and publication ratios as semantic brand primitives rather than decoration;
- remove contrast devices when they break a continuous material composition;
- preserve bilingual editorial hierarchy and mobile readability as first-class constraints;
- distinguish product-specific success from cross-product brand law;
- use the live Journal as a real-content control when testing later visual grammar.

**Weaknesses / debt**
- the system under-expresses Doré as an active visual-production capability because it lacks a coherent freshly drawn Doré-derived asset family;
- several successful Journal devices are product-specific and should not be propagated automatically to Search, Library, ONE, Main or Join;
- the current visual grammar has not yet proved purpose-built `LIGHT`, `STAR`, `WATER`, `SKY/CLOUD`, `STONE/WALL/CITY`, `PAPER/SURFACE` and `DIVIDER/EDGE/SECTION` assets across responsive digital and print contexts;
- real same-content A/B proof against the production Journal remains open.

**Revisit trigger**
Reopen only when `VIS-GRAMMAR` has production-ready evidence: purpose-built Doré-derived assets, same-real-content comparison against the current Journal, rendered mobile inspection, reading-comfort/accessibility/performance checks, and at least one credible print/ministry transfer proof. Do not reopen merely to replace the Journal with conceptual placeholders, direct Doré-image collage or generic engraving filters.

**Disposition**
Keep the August Journal milestone historically closed and production-stable. Retain it as the required control for `RQ-004`. Future refit should use purpose-built Doré-derived website materials while original Doré works remain curated reader content/exhibition cards rather than default interface decoration.
