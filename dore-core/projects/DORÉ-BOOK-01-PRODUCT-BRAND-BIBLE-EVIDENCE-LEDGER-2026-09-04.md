## Durable learned principles

- Major learning loops should be capable of producing durable cultural products, not only internal capability.
- A product-learning loop is complete per iteration only when learning evidence, original transfer and manuscript/publication output all exist.
- External brand systems teach architecture, pedagogy and governance; they do not become Westside identity or visual authority.
- Book 01 and the New Westside system share one research lineage, but production-site mutation remains a separate user-approved gate.
- Interactive publication behavior must have a print-equivalent editorial logic; HTML effects may not destroy reading order or source portability.
- AI may preserve and analyze recorded spiritual/design provenance but may not certify an aesthetic decision as divine revelation.
