## What this corpus adds

The 2026-09-09 corpus is materially stronger than a bookmark collection. It requires every reference to be reduced into mechanism, art-direction principle, information-architecture principle, transferable primitive, constraint, failure risk, Westside application and an experiment. It currently contains twenty award-calibre or historically significant digital precedents spanning narrative scroll, spatial chronology, explorable semantic systems, horizontal navigation, multi-current structures, craft-native controls and browser-native storytelling.

The most material new hypotheses are architectural rather than stylistic:

1. New Westside may be tested as a Scripture/salvation-history spatial skeleton rather than only a conventional publication shell containing Scripture as one section.
2. Doré engraving should generate its own motion physics from line/hatch density, light emerging from darkness, paper/plate behavior, depth planes, crop/continuation, ink saturation and engraved architecture rather than inheriting generic parallax or cinematic presets.
3. Horizontal interaction may need to be plural: several controlled currents can carry chronology, editorial material, themes, places/persons, Journal and ONE entry points rather than one irreversible strip.
4. `river of history / living water` is a serious interface-metaphor candidate because it can potentially unite time, movement, Living Water identity, light/reflection, depth and multiple currents; it remains a hypothesis until prototype critique proves semantic usefulness rather than watery decoration.
5. Deep reading likely remains stable editorial/vertical after deliberate entry from the spatial layer, protecting Scripture readability, accessibility and comprehension.

## Evidence boundary

This corpus is design intelligence and a prototype agenda, not a completed visual system and not Brand V1 authority. Its own language repeatedly marks the new hypotheses as active, competing and critique-dependent. The internal `Red Dot-level or reject` phrase is an aspirational quality bar, not evidence of award affiliation or award-calibre output.

The corpus therefore does not supersede the existing purpose-built Doré asset contract. It extends it: fresh LIGHT / STAR / WATER / SKY / STONE-WALL / CITY / PAPER elements remain the visual grammar substrate, while the new corpus proposes how those elements might participate in spatial and motion systems.

The corpus also does not justify forcing horizontal/spatial interaction into ONE or article-depth reading. Several cited precedents are explicitly used as warnings that one spatial grammar should not dominate every depth level.
