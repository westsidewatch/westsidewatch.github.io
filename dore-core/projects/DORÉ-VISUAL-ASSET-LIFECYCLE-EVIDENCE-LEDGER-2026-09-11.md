## Reconciliation judgment

1. The newly clarified purpose-built Doré Website Asset Suite is fully compatible with the older Brand Operating Architecture and R2/Asset Registry architecture. The suite should therefore be treated as a governed reusable asset family, not as ad-hoc page decoration or one-off generated imagery.
2. Asset acceptance and asset storage are separate gates. A visual asset does not become production-worthy merely because it can be generated, uploaded, registered, or delivered. `LIGHT-01` still must first pass the visual-design gate: rendered inspection, critique/revision, transparent reuse, Living Paper + true-night survival, and human editorial acceptance.
3. Once an asset passes design acceptance, its lifecycle should follow the existing hybrid placement policy rather than a new storage rule:
   - code-coupled fixed UI marks/textures/icons that must version atomically with the interface may remain in GitHub;
   - growing editorial/generated masters and cross-product reusable media should normally use R2;
   - each production asset should have one authoritative binary backend, stable `asset_id`, provenance/rights metadata, preservation class, reuse relationships and supersession history.
4. The website asset suite should be registered semantically by family and use, not only by filename. At minimum records should distinguish `LIGHT`, `STAR`, `WATER`, `SKY/CLOUD`, `STONE/WALL`, `CITY/CITY-EDGE`, `PAPER/MATERIAL`, and `DIVIDER/EDGE/SECTION MARK`, with intended products and approved contexts.
5. Historical Doré originals remain a separate curated content class under `DORE-EXHIBITION`. Their provenance/rights records may use the same asset infrastructure, but they must not collapse into the interface-grammar class.
6. The phrase `Doré = Asset Creator + Librarian` remains a current architectural principle for this work: Doré should retain not just the binary but why the asset exists, what it depicts, its rights state, where it is used, whether it can be reused, and what supersedes it.
7. No evidence in this batch proves that the purpose-built Website Asset Suite has yet entered the Asset Registry/R2 lifecycle. This is not a new blocker; it is a downstream proof gate after visual acceptance.
8. No workstream status changes are justified. `VIS-GRAMMAR` remains `ACTIVE_PARALLEL / BUILDING`, `DORE-EXHIBITION` remains `READY`, `BRAND-V1` remains gated, and `NERVOUS-SYSTEM` remains incomplete beyond its bounded shared-substrate foundation.
9. No P01 subtitle state, deployment, audio/transcription dependency, credential, ordering or blocker condition was modified.
