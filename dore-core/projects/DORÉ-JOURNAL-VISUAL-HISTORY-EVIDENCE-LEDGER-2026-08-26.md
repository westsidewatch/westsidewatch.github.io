## Historical completion judgment

The August Journal/Vol.00 visual sequence is a legitimate **historically completed bounded design milestone**, not an abandoned mockup.

The evidence shows an intentional, iterated editorial system rather than a one-shot styling pass:

1. the Journal established a twelve-column preview edition and one continuous night-to-light narrative through Vol.00;
2. the opening was deliberately kept fully illuminated while the diagonal light transition began later in the Contents journey;
3. the masthead was moved to the formal external artwork rather than remaining embedded markup;
4. section names and editorial labels were refined as the publication structure stabilized;
5. rectangular contrast backplates were subsequently removed to preserve an uninterrupted temple-stone/night composition;
6. current CSS still retains the resulting vocabulary: Watch Night, First Light Gold, temple-stone materiality, issue-door framing, 5:8 image windows, bilingual serif editorial hierarchy and the publication-ratio system.

Classification for the bounded August Journal visual milestone: `COMPLETED_REVISIT_CANDIDATE`.

This does **not** change `MAIN` from `MAINTENANCE`, and it does not make `VIS-GRAMMAR` complete.

## Why the revisit is now justified

The newer engraving-brand-system project materially changes the quality standard under which the August treatment should be judged.

The current governing visual direction says:

- Doré engraving is source grammar, not a universal page template;
- original Doré works belong mainly in a curatorial/exhibition relationship rather than generic interface decoration;
- the preferred brand outcome is often purpose-built Westside visual vocabulary freshly drawn from Doré logic;
- clean paper/stone surfaces, localized hatching, subtle engraved fields and rare immersive dark fields should be governed by hierarchy rather than applied uniformly;
- Dawn Gold, stone, typography, city/wall, light, maps, photography, video and motion must form one restrained system;
- real page/mobile/print proof is required before Brand V1 propagation.

Therefore the August Journal should remain a production baseline and historical source of successful primitives, but it should no longer be treated as the final shared visual grammar.

## Current quality judgment

### What remains strong

- The **night → dawn → great light** narrative is semantically strong and deeply aligned with Westside Watch.
- Temple stone, city/gate/wall, First Light Gold and watch-night symbolism are durable brand primitives.
- The opening-versus-Contents sequencing shows useful restraint: the strongest transition is staged rather than repeated everywhere.
- The 5:8 editorial image-window language and formal masthead created a recognizable publication object rather than a generic church webpage.
- Removing contrast backplates was a valid correction toward a more continuous material composition.

### What is now visibly limited

- The visual identity still relies heavily on large dark fields, gradients, temple-stone photography/texture and geometric light treatment rather than a reusable Doré-derived mark/texture/stroke vocabulary.
- Several successful Journal devices are product-specific and should not automatically become cross-ecosystem Brand V1 rules.
- The current system predates the explicit rule that purpose-built Doré-derived website assets should be designed as a family and proved across digital + print/ministry surfaces.
- The old Journal can therefore look coherent while still under-expressing Doré as a living visual-production capability.

## Revisit rule

Do **not** replace the live Journal with conceptual placeholders or direct Doré-image collage.

When `VIS-GRAMMAR` has production-ready evidence, use the current Journal as the control and test the same real content with only evidence-backed additions such as:

- purpose-drawn Doré-style light fields / engraved light textures;
- a purpose-drawn Bethlehem-star family where the editorial context warrants it;
- engraved water, cloud/sky, stone/wall and city-edge elements derived from Doré logic but newly made for Westside;
- restrained paper/stone texture behavior;
- reusable divider/edge/section marks and responsive equivalents;
- the existing First Light Gold, Cormorant Garamond + Noto Serif TC and publication ratios retained where they continue to serve hierarchy.

Original Doré works should remain separately available as curated content/exhibition cards in the reading flow rather than being used as the default source of interface decoration.

## Desired future comparison

The future comparison must be a **real-content control test**:

`current Journal production baseline` vs `same content + proven purpose-built Doré-derived grammar`

Evaluate at minimum:

- immediate Westside recognition without logo dependence;
