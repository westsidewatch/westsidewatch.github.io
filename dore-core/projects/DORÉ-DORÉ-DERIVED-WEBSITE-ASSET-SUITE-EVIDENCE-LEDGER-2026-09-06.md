## Consolidated finding

The website should not use original Doré works as its default decorative material. Original Doré works are curated content and should appear in a dedicated exhibition/curation card inside the reader's scroll flow, with context, Scripture linkage, provenance and rights.

The website visual language should instead be a coherent suite of fresh assets drawn by Doré specifically for the current Westside Watch principles. The requested asset families are:

- `LIGHT`: directional engraved light fields, broken/occluded beams, edge illumination, pale paper-light textures;
- `STAR`: Bethlehem-star and watch-star variants for hero, section and navigation scales;
- `WATER`: restrained ripple/current/reflection fields with crop and repeat behavior;
- `SKY/CLOUD`: cloud mass, dawn break, night-sky and atmospheric-depth textures;
- `STONE/WALL/CITY`: temple-stone material, battlement edges, distant city silhouettes and first-light contact edges;
- `PAPER/SURFACE`: restrained papyric/stone-derived surface behavior supporting Living Paper rather than replacing it;
- `DIVIDER/EDGE/SECTION`: structural marks for navigation, cards and editorial pacing;
- responsive digital and print equivalents.

## Evaluation

Original objective: create a recognizable Westside Watch visual language that carries Doré's spatial/atmospheric/engraved logic without copying or pasting original plates into every layout.

Completion evidence: the asset-family contract is explicit in the governing brand-system document and has been reinforced by the current design decision. No production-ready suite has yet been evidenced.

Current quality: the concept is now correctly separated into (1) curated original works and (2) purpose-built website grammar. The prior placeholder comparison was invalid because it used labels and abstract lines rather than real assets.

What was learned: the meaningful control is a real current production surface; the treatment variable must be a real asset suite applied to the same content and geometry. A/B comparison is invalid when the “new” side is only a description or placeholder.

Weakness/debt: no persisted asset files, generated source prompts, semantic roles, crop rules, density scales, mobile/print proofs or human-judged specimen currently establish the suite as production-ready. Ink Black must not be used as a blanket background; dark immersive fields are exceptional and should use the defined Midnight Blue treatment.

Revisit trigger: reopen as a build/evaluation milestone when real generated assets exist for at least LIGHT, STAR, and one of WATER/SKY/STONE, and can be applied to one real Journal/Search surface plus one print/ministry specimen.

Current disposition: `ACTIVE_PARALLEL / BUILDING`; do not promote to Brand V1 or replace current Journal/Search surfaces yet.
