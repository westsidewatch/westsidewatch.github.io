## Engineering decision

The next phase is **not chapter-by-chapter linear polishing** and is not uniform expansion.

The manuscript must first be treated as one book-sized system. The latter roughly two-thirds of the current manuscript remains substantially first-draft material, while the earlier portion is comparatively more developed. Therefore the first priority is to complete and rebalance the whole-book architecture before spending disproportionate effort polishing already mature early chapters.

**There is no target or maximum manuscript length.** Previous references to `~100k`, `50k → 100k`, an expansion budget, or a `gap-to-100k` ledger are deprecated as construction authority. The book expands only while new writing opens a real world, advances the inner action, resolves a structural need, adds non-redundant knowledge/evidence, or reveals a genuine cross-book connection. The book stops when the book itself is complete.

## Governing construction principle

Before substantial rewriting, Doré must determine at whole-book scale:

- what carries the central theological load;
- what is orientation or connective tissue;
- where narrative time should slow or accelerate;
- which scenes deserve magnification and which should remain compressed;
- where evidence must be visible in the prose and where it should remain in the research layer;
- where original-language material actually changes understanding;
- which historical / archaeological / geographical material is necessary;
- where the current manuscript jumps across a missing logical or narrative bridge;
- where long-range motifs first appear, disappear, return and change meaning;
- what each chapter's compressed inner action is;
- which apparently separate motifs are actually one redemptive movement;
- how much reader attention each section is allowed to consume relative to the whole book.

Local writing decisions are subordinate to this whole-book composition.

## Chapter 2 authority — 嘆息

Chapter 2 is not governed merely by `驕傲`. Its compressed dramatic theme is **嘆息**.

The chapter should allow human strength to become genuinely impressive before its limit becomes tragic: Nimrod, city, brick, kingship, ancient hero traditions, Gilgamesh, mortality, the desire to leave a name, the search for immortality, and the human need to keep acting even while knowing that human strength cannot finally save.

The emotional engine is not contempt for human civilization. It is the tragic tension:

`人這麼有能力，為什麼還是不行？`

This chapter should not resolve its own sigh too quickly. Salvation must not be appended as an explanatory moral at the end of every digression. The sigh is allowed to remain unresolved until later movements earn its answer.

### Nimrod expansion node

Nimrod is a major candidate for character/world expansion:

- investigate Hebrew `gibbōr` and `ṣayid` without assuming modern positive `hero` semantics;
- distinguish the lexical text of Genesis 10 from later Jewish interpretation;
- research the ancient Jewish interpretive line in which Nimrod hunts/ensnares people through speech or brings people into rebellion;
- research ancient Near Eastern royal hunting, kingship, lion-hunt imagery and hero traditions;
- compare, without identifying, Nimrod and Gilgamesh / Mesopotamian heroic traditions;
- follow Gilgamesh through Enkidu, death, mortality and the search for immortality;
- allow the research to widen into the human philosophical problem of strength, finitude, death, name and self-grounding;
- return only when the chapter's `嘆息` has deepened.

No equation `Nimrod = Gilgamesh` is permitted without evidence. Unknowns remain unknown.

## Underground-waterway / echo architecture

The book is developing an **emergent echo structure**. Doré must discover genuine long-distance connections rather than manufacture callbacks. An element may appear locally, disappear, then return with changed meaning; a later return may retroactively alter how the reader understands its first appearance.

For every candidate waterway record:

`first appearance → transformations → crossings with other waterways → later return → final theological/emotional resolution → evidence status`

Do not force recurrence merely for symmetry.

### 1. 風 / 翅膀 / 等候 / 上騰

Working movement:

`迦南帳篷外不知道從哪裡吹來又吹去的風 → ruach / rachaph → 神的靈振翅 → 等候 → 五旬節大風 → 歷史中的聖靈工作 → 今日仍吹的風 → 人如鷹展翅迎風 → 上騰`

Important inversion: early in the book the Spirit `hovers / flutters`; at the ending the renewed human opens wings to the wind. Human life is not defined by frantic self-propulsion but by receiving life/energy from God.

Ending candidate source-authority prose supplied by the author:

> 不知道從哪裡吹來又吹去的風，直到今日今時，還是吹，那是聖靈的風。此刻，這風有些大了。老鷹用盡一生的力氣做了一件事，張開翅膀，為的是迎接風。而麻雀用了一生的力氣搧動翅膀憑藉自己。如鷹展翅上騰，是耶和華給我們的生活和能量，那股吹了三千七百年吹進現實的風沒有停息，因為生命的風不會停息。

Preserve as ending candidate; do not silently rewrite it.

### 2. 甚好 / 失落 / 新造

`創造 → 人被稱為甚好 → 甚好懸在歷史上空 → 失落 → 基督 → 聖靈 → 新造的人 → 那個本來應該成為的你浮現`

New creation is not a late devotional application. It begins in Genesis 1 and is a final theological gravity of the book.

### 3. 說話 / 聽見 / 聽錯 / 聽懂

`以撒辨認聲音失敗 → 人類語言歧義 → 神豈是真說 → 亞當聽見神的聲音而害怕 → 巴別彼此聽不懂 → 神仍說 → 道來到人中間仍有人認不出 → 五旬節各人聽見自己的鄉談 → 哥林多再次出現聽不懂 / 翻譯 / 造就問題 → 人重新學習聽神的話`

Underlying question: `誰的話是真的？`

### 4. 名字

`亞當命名 → 神賜/改名 → 人在巴別要傳揚自己的名 → 英雄/王權/碑銘以名字抵抗死亡 → 耶穌之名 → 神所賜身份 / 新造`

Do not use the popular `Saul was renamed Paul by God` simplification; research actual textual evidence.

### 5. 網羅 / 獵人 / 得人 / 釋放

This is now a major first-class candidate waterway.

Working movement:

`蛇以話欺騙 → Nimrod as hunter → Jewish interpretive tradition: 用口獵人 / 網羅人的思想 → 巴別「來吧」的集體動員 → 舊約網羅意象 / 嘴唇與言語成為網羅 → satan as adversary/obstructor → 魔鬼的欺騙與控告 → 耶穌「來跟從我」 → 漁夫 / 得人如得魚 → 復活主一句話使門徒再次下網 → 基督權柄 / 釋放 → 人的口重新歸屬神的真實`

Core inversion:

`寧錄得人，是把人收入自己的國；耶穌得人，是把人帶回神的國。`

`人的口說謊 / 網羅 → 神借人的口說話。`

The image of capture is not merely abolished; it may be **redeemed/reoriented**. This must be researched carefully rather than asserted by wordplay alone.

### 6. satan / 擋路 / 誰擋了誰

Research node, not yet settled prose:

- investigate Hebrew `śāṭān` as common noun `adversary / opponent / obstruction` before/alongside its development as a personal designation;
- Numbers 22:22 contains the messenger of YHWH standing in Balaam's road `לְשָׂטָן לוֹ`;
- verify corpus chronology/occurrence before claiming this is the absolute first biblical appearance of the word;
- exploit only after verification the narrative reversal: the apparent `obstructor` may be God's messenger because Balaam is the one travelling the wrong road;
- connect cautiously to `路`, `偏離`, `網羅`, deception and eventual liberation in Christ.

### 7. 舌頭 / 人的口 / 天國的語言

`蛇的口 → 寧錄之口 → 巴別之口 → 先知之口 → 耶穌之口 → 雅各的舌頭如火 / 能祝福也能咒詛 → glōssa as tongue/language → 五旬節人的口被打開 → 哥林多的方言 / 預言 / 翻譯 / 造就 → 新造之人的口`

The answer is not to seal the dangerous human mouth forever. The mouth/tongue is redeemed and returned to truthful, life-giving service.

### 8. 「來」 / 向上與向下

`巴別：「來吧，我們建造」 / 人要上去 → 耶穌：「來跟從我」 → 神不是等人爬到天上，而是道成肉身下來 → 聖靈降臨 → 神住進人裡面`

This is a major spatial inversion and should be researched at textual level before direct lexical equivalence is claimed.

### 9. 沉默 / 等待

`以撒祝福後的沉默 → 創造前的等待 → 巴別後漫長歷史 → 先知 / 以利亞 / 約伯 / 以西結 → 伯利恆 → 十字架 → 神看似沉默時仍在工作`

Silence must not automatically mean divine absence. `rachaph` supplies a possible image of life being prepared before visible action.

### 10. 嘆息 → 說不出的嘆息 → 拯救

Chapter 2's human sigh is allowed to exist before it is theologically named.

`英雄面對死亡的嘆息 → 人類文明知道自己有限仍必須用力 → 等待 / 痛苦 → later Pauline「說不出的嘆息」 / Spirit intercession → hope / redemption`

Writing rule: **我們不寫聖靈，但我們用聖靈寫。** In Chapter 2, do not prematurely explain the sigh as a Holy-Spirit motif. Let the emotional reality exist first; later Scripture can disclose another depth.

### 11. 光 / 火

`要有光 → 腳前的燈 → 歷史黑夜中的光 → 道成肉身 → 舌頭如火的 danger → 五旬節如火焰的舌頭 → truthful/life-giving speech`

Research and distinguish image, lexical connection and theological interpretation; do not collapse them merely because they share visual similarity.

### 12. 手

Candidate waterway requiring textual discipline:

`以撒伸手摸到偽裝 → 夏娃伸手取 → God clothing humans (literary embodiment must not be misreported as explicit "God's hand" wording) → 道成肉身後耶穌真正以人的手觸摸 / 掰餅 / 洗腳 → 十字架`

The Incarnation may transform an earlier literary hand motif into embodied divine-human touch, but this must remain literature/theology rather than false quotation.

### 13. 路 / 偏離 / 阻擋

`人的偏離 → 罪 / 過犯 / 悖逆 semantic field → Balaam's road and the adversarial messenger → apparent open road vs actual danger → Jesus' call to follow → liberation / truthful direction`

Do not reduce biblical hamartiology to the etymological slogan `sin = missing the mark`; research Hebrew and Greek semantic fields and distinguish lexical history from full theology.

### 14. 洪水 / 宣告 / 權柄

Research node:

`洪水前世界 → later 1 Peter 3:18–22 returns to Noah-era disobedient spirits/persons → Christ's proclamation → resurrection / ascension / powers subjected to Christ`

Do not state as uncontested that `Jesus died and then evangelized pre-Flood humans`. Distinguish 1 Peter 3 `ἐκήρυξεν` proclamation, 1 Peter 4 gospel-to-the-dead language, and major interpretive traditions. The structural value is that an apparently closed ancient age returns inside the New Testament's Christological horizon.

## Whole-book title resolution

The title's final theological resolution is now explicit:

**神很遠嗎？神一直都很近。**

The book must not imply a simple chronology `God was far → God later came near`. Its deeper spatial movement is:

`人偏離 → 人覺得神遠 → 人向外/向上尋找 → 建塔 / 建城 / 造英雄 / 留名 / 尋不死 → 神一直說話 → 神降臨 / 道成肉身 → 聖靈住進人裡面 → 人終於發現神一直都很近`

Chapter 9 QA is therefore not merely a controversy appendix. Its compressed theme is **神一直都很近**. Reader questions about tongues, absence, silence, authenticity, psychology, cessation/continuation and experience should be allowed to deepen toward the underlying question `神在哪裡？`, while the preceding eight chapters return as the answer.

The final wind resolves the title without requiring a didactic summary.

## Five-axis whole-book map

Every major section should be evaluated on five independent axes rather than by word count:

1. **篇幅重量 / textual weight** — how much manuscript space the section deserves as an outcome, never as a quota.
2. **敘事速度 / narrative speed** — whether years pass quickly or seconds are decompressed.
3. **場景密度 / scene density** — how much concrete human/world presence is required.
4. **資料顯露度 / evidence visibility** — how much research appears explicitly in reader-facing prose versus remaining below the surface.
5. **神學壓力 / theological pressure** — how much of the book's central argument the section must carry.

These axes do not have to rise together. Research volume does not automatically earn reader-facing word count.

## Construction sequence

### Phase A — Whole-book census and maturity map

Read the complete canonical manuscript as one object. Record maturity, function, scenes, claims, evidence, unresolved claims, transitions, motif state and source-authority status. Length may be measured descriptively but must not govern admission or expansion.

### Phase B — Inner-action / weight / rhythm map

Test the nine chapter inner actions against the manuscript. Map relative weight, speed, scene density, evidence visibility, theological pressure and emotional temperature.

### Phase C — Emergence ledger

Replace the deprecated `gap-to-100k` concept with an **Expansion / Emergence Ledger**. For every candidate expansion record:

`origin passage → chapter inner action → curiosity exit / problem → research need → world opened → waterway/echo connection → evidence class → expected reader-facing form → destination → admission status`

Allowed admission reasons include:

- missing canonical scene;
- underbuilt scene;
- historical world/context;
- textual/original-language bridge;
- unsupported or under-supported claim;
- missing theological step;
- human consequence;
- chronology/geography;
- genuine motif/echo connection;
- character expansion;
- problem-chain expansion;
- free digression that opens a meaningful world;
- historical extension into the present;
- visual evidence/breathing entrance.

No candidate is admitted merely because the manuscript is below a desired size.

### Phase D — Research queue derived from emergence

Research may begin from a structural gap **or from a genuine curiosity exit** discovered in writing. For every task record:

`writing/curiosity need → question → source/evidence class → expected use → reader-facing visibility → destination → uncertainty status`

A research path may legitimately end in `不知道`; uncertainty is an admissible scholarly result.

### Phase E — Build load-bearing and high-emergence sections

Do not require linear Chapter 1 → Chapter 9 expansion. Build where structural pressure and genuine emergence are strongest, while protecting mature source-authority prose.

Current high-value nodes include:

- Chapter 1 Isaac / blessing / human-language ambiguity;
- Chapter 2 Nimrod / hunter / speech-as-snare / ancient hero world / Gilgamesh / sigh;
- Chapter 3 OT Spirit / prophecy / `舊約有方言嗎？`;
- Chapter 4 Incarnation / Jesus' world / Logos / Ephesus / downward movement;
- Chapter 5 Pentecost as ignition / Spirit history / Azusa / present;
- Chapter 6 Corinth life-record / Paul as character attractor;
- Chapter 7 tongues problem-chain / tongue-language redemption;
- Chapter 8 new creation as Genesis-resolution;
- Chapter 9 QA resolving toward `神一直都很近` and the final wind.

### Phase F — Whole-book second pass

Reread as one continuous book and rebalance weight, repetition, research exposure, scene frequency, emotional heat, echo recurrence, theological climb, reader fatigue/recovery and opening-to-ending correspondence.

### Phase G — Sentence-level refinement and publishing gates

Only after whole-book structure is stable should final prose refinement dominate. Then run Editorial Gate, Theology Gate, Publishing QA and BookBuild.

## Research / prose separation

The project maintains two densities:

**Research layer:** extensive, technical, source-heavy, allowed to preserve competing interpretations and failed hypotheses.

**Reader-facing book:** admits evidence that changes understanding, supports a necessary claim, gives the world material reality, advances the narrative/theological movement, or creates a genuinely worthwhile curiosity path.

Do not display research merely to prove research was done.

## Theological / historical evidence discipline

For every strong connection distinguish:

1. **text says**;
2. **lexical / historical evidence suggests**;
3. **later interpretive tradition says**;
4. **author theological reading**;
5. **literary echo / construction choice**;
6. **confessional expectation**.

Do not collapse these layers.

Current explicit caution nodes include:

- `Nimrod hunts humans / uses speech to hunt` belongs to interpretive tradition unless stronger textual evidence is established;
- `satan` in Numbers 22 requires lexical/corpus verification before any `first occurrence` claim;
- `Nimrod = Gilgamesh` is not established;
- `sin = missing the mark` must not replace full biblical hamartiology;
- 1 Peter 3 must not be flattened into one uncontested descent-to-the-dead interpretation;
- literary echo does not prove lexical identity.

## Scene allocation rule

Canonical scenes are scarce structural resources, but curiosity-driven expansion is not capped by a fixed scene budget. Magnify when the chapter inner action, world, problem chain or emergent echo earns the reading time. Compress when it does not.

## Efficiency rule

Before editing a section, Doré should be able to answer:

1. What chapter inner action this work serves.
2. What new world, problem, evidence or echo it opens.
3. What must be researched first.
4. What must remain untouched from canonical author source.
5. Which underground waterways enter/leave this section.
6. What evidence status each important connection has.
7. Why the reader should be allowed to spend time here.

If the only answer is `to increase length`, do not expand.
