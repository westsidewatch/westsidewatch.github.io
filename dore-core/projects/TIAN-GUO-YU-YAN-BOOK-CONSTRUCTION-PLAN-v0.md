# 《神很遠，神很近｜天國語言極簡史》全書構建計畫 v0

Status: ACTIVE / ENGINEERING CONSTRUCTION PLAN
Project ID: `book.tian-guo-yu-yan`
Role: whole-book construction authority for the 50k → ~100k development phase
Scope: structure, sequence, weight, evidence allocation, research order, expansion order and acceptance
Not role: prose-style canon

## Engineering decision

The next phase is **not chapter-by-chapter linear polishing** and is not uniform 2× expansion.

The manuscript must first be treated as one book-sized system. The latter roughly two-thirds of the current manuscript remains substantially first-draft material, while the earlier portion is comparatively more developed. Therefore the first priority is to complete and rebalance the whole-book architecture before spending disproportionate effort polishing already mature early chapters.

The target ~100,000 characters is a consequence of completing the book's real structure, not a quota to be filled evenly.

## Governing construction principle

Before substantial rewriting, Doré must determine at whole-book scale:

- what carries the central theological load;
- what is orientation or connective tissue;
- where narrative time should slow or accelerate;
- which scenes deserve magnification and which should remain compressed;
- where evidence must be visible in the prose and where it should remain in the research layer;
- where original-language material actually changes understanding;
- which historical / archaeological / geographical material is necessary;
- where the current manuscript jumps across a missing logical or narrative bridge;
- where long-range motifs first appear, disappear, return and change meaning;
- how much reader attention each section is allowed to consume relative to the whole book.

Local writing decisions are subordinate to this whole-book composition.

## Five-axis whole-book map

Every major section should be evaluated on five independent axes rather than by word count alone:

1. **篇幅重量 / textual weight** — how much manuscript space the section deserves.
2. **敘事速度 / narrative speed** — whether years pass quickly or seconds are decompressed.
3. **場景密度 / scene density** — how much concrete human/world presence is required.
4. **資料顯露度 / evidence visibility** — how much research appears explicitly in reader-facing prose versus remaining below the surface.
5. **神學壓力 / theological pressure** — how much of the book's central argument the section must carry.

These axes do not have to rise together. A short section may be slow, scene-dense and theologically heavy. A long section may move quickly while carrying historical orientation. Research volume does not automatically earn reader-facing word count.

## Current maturity asymmetry

Working assumption to be verified against the canonical manuscript census and section map:

- **Earlier ~1/3:** comparatively mature authorial substrate. Primary work = preserve, diagnose, selectively deepen, use as author-syntax and narrative training evidence.
- **Later ~2/3:** substantially first-draft. Primary work = structural completion before sentence-level polishing.

This distinction is operational. Doré must not spend the majority of the expansion budget perfecting the already stronger opening while the later book remains structurally thin.

## Construction sequence

### Phase A — Whole-book census and maturity map

Read the complete canonical manuscript as one object. For every section record:

- current length;
- maturity level;
- current narrative function;
- current theological function;
- scenes already present;
- claims already present;
- evidence already present;
- unresolved claims;
- missing transitions;
- motif state;
- whether the section is source-authority prose, notes, sketch, or near-finished prose.

Output: a whole-book maturity and function map. No bulk expansion before this exists.

### Phase B — Weight / rhythm / proportion score

Lay out the whole book before local rewriting. Assign intended relative weight, speed, scene density, evidence visibility and theological pressure to each major movement.

The map must make visible where the book should be light and where it should be heavy. It must prevent accidental flattening in which every chapter receives equal length, equal research density or equal dramatic treatment.

Key structural load-bearing movements currently include, subject to manuscript evidence:

`創造 / 神說話 → 祝福與話語重量 → 巴別斷裂 → 漫長等候 → 道成肉身 → 五旬節 → 哥林多與恩賜誤解 → 聖靈與新造 → 語言歸還`

The central hinge around **道成肉身 → 五旬節** must be tested for sufficient structural mass before early chapters receive final polish.

Output: whole-book editorial score / weight map.

### Phase C — Structural gap ledger

For each later first-draft section, identify what is actually missing. Gaps must be typed rather than expressed as generic requests for “more detail”.

Allowed gap types include:

- missing canonical scene;
- underbuilt scene;
- missing historical world/context;
- missing textual bridge;
- missing original-language explanation;
- unsupported or under-supported claim;
- missing theological step;
- missing human consequence;
- reader-orientation gap;
- chronology/geography gap;
- motif opportunity or broken motif continuity;
- transition gap between centuries/books/chapters;
- conclusion present without sufficient narrative path to earn it.

Output: gap-to-100k construction ledger.

### Phase D — Research queue derived from gaps

Research is pulled only by a named construction need. Do not perform broad research merely because a topic is interesting.

For every research task record:

`writing need → question → source/evidence class → expected use → reader-facing visibility → destination section`

Research may be much larger than the prose that survives. A section can consume twenty sources and expose one decisive detail. Evidence quantity does not dictate prose quantity.

Priority research first serves the load-bearing later two-thirds, especially gaps that block the central theological/narrative movement.

Output: ordered research queue with provenance requirements.

### Phase E — Build load-bearing later sections first

Do not require linear Chapter 1 → Chapter 9 expansion. Build the structural hinges first where the manuscript is currently weakest and the book depends on them.

Initial priority order, to be revised by the full manuscript map:

1. **道成肉身** — must carry the book's central reversal rather than merely explain Logos.
2. **五旬節** — must answer Babel and become one of the book's principal narrative/theological climaxes.
3. **巴別 → 等候 → 道成肉身** bridge — must make the centuries feel like one unfolding problem rather than detached chapters.
4. **哥林多** — must show what happens when the returned gift is possessed but misunderstood.
5. **聖靈 / 新造 / 語言歸還** — must earn the book's ending rather than summarize earlier claims.
6. Other later first-draft sections according to the gap ledger.

Only after these load-bearing areas stand should the project return to systematic refinement of the earlier mature third.

### Phase F — Whole-book second pass

After structural completion and major expansion, reread the manuscript as one continuous book. Rebalance:

- total weight;
- chapter proportions;
- repetition;
- research exposure;
- scene frequency;
- motif recurrence;
- theological climb;
- reader fatigue and recovery;
- opening-to-ending correspondence.

Some existing passages may become shorter even while the total manuscript grows. Expansion and compression are both valid construction operations.

### Phase G — Sentence-level refinement and publishing gates

Only after whole-book structure is stable should final prose refinement become the dominant activity. Then run the established Editorial Gate, Theology Gate, Publishing QA and BookBuild pipeline.

## Expansion budget

Do not allocate a fixed equal multiplier by chapter.

Until exact census is materialized, use only a planning range. If the canonical manuscript is approximately 50–54k Chinese characters, the additional ~46–50k should be weighted toward structural completion of the later first-draft two-thirds.

Working allocation:

- later ~2/3 structural completion and new writing: approximately **30–35k** additional characters;
- earlier ~1/3 selective scene/evidence deepening and necessary recomposition: approximately **10–15k** additional characters;
- final balancing, transitions and motif continuity: remaining amount as required by the whole-book score.

These are planning envelopes, not quotas. Exact allocation follows the census and gap ledger.

## Research / prose separation

The project must maintain two different densities:

**Research layer:** may be extensive, technical, source-heavy and redundant for verification.

**Reader-facing book:** admits only evidence that changes understanding, supports a necessary claim, gives the world material reality, or advances the narrative/theological movement.

Do not display research merely to prove research was done.

## Scene allocation rule at book scale

Canonical scenes are scarce structural resources. Not every biblical episode receives cinematic treatment. Select scenes according to whole-book function so later climaxes retain room to become larger than earlier ones.

Scene magnification decisions therefore belong first to this construction plan; prose realization of an approved scene belongs to the writing capability.

This distinction prevents confusing **what the book needs to magnify** with **how Doré writes a magnified scene**.

## Motif orchestration

Motifs are scheduled across the whole book rather than inserted opportunistically during local writing.

For each major motif — currently `風`, `聲音/說話/沉默`, `重量`, `光`, `門`, `歸還` — map:

- first appearance;
- quiet recurrence;
- deliberate absence;
- return;
- changed meaning;
- final resolution or deliberate openness.

Example: the early Canaan wind can exist lightly before `風 / Spirit` acquires greater theological pressure later. The motif should not be over-explained on first appearance.

## Efficiency rule

Before editing a section, Doré should be able to answer:

1. Why this section is being worked on now.
2. What structural gap it resolves.
3. What evidence or research must be gathered first.
4. What must remain untouched from the canonical author source.
5. What whole-book weight/speed role this section has.
6. What the next dependent construction step is after this work passes.

If these cannot be answered, the section is not ready for substantial expansion.

## Immediate execution order

1. Materialize / read the complete canonical manuscript and exact census when the native source bridge permits.
2. Build the whole-book maturity/function map.
3. Build the five-axis weight/rhythm/proportion score.
4. Produce the typed structural gap ledger, with special attention to the later two-thirds.
5. Derive and run the research queue only for blocking gaps.
6. Expand the highest-load, weakest structural hinges first.
7. Re-evaluate the whole book before returning to early-chapter polish.
8. Run final whole-book recomposition and publishing gates.
