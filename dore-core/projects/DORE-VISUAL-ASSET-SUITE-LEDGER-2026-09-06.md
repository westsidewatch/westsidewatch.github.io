## Governing interpretation

Original Dore works are curated content. They belong in exhibition or editorial cards in the reader flow. They are not the default source of interface decoration.

The website language must be a freshly drawn, purpose-built asset suite generated from current New Westside visual principles. This is a design-language production task, not an image-gallery task and not a generic engraving-filter task.

## Required asset families

1. Light: engraved light fields, breaking light, halo or edge-light, dawn transitions, and restrained light textures for page backgrounds, section transitions, title fields and print equivalents.
2. Bethlehem star: multiple scale and density variants, including paper-light, night-watch and small metadata-mark versions.
3. Water: wave, ripple, reflection and crossing motifs for rules, cards, separators and subtle surface texture.
4. Sky: cloud masses, open sky, storm-to-light transitions and horizon textures with controlled contrast.
5. Stone, wall, city edge: wall faces, masonry rhythm, parapet and gate silhouettes, distant city-edge forms and light-catching edges.
6. Surface system: restrained papyrus, paper and stone behavior that supports ink without becoming a decorative background block.
7. Digital and print equivalents: responsive low-contrast digital variants and print-safe equivalents.

## Minimum valid proof

The smallest useful proof is one complete purpose-drawn asset family, recommended first as a light-texture set plus Bethlehem-star variants, with source brief, generation provenance, original files, browser readback in the same real Journal or Search specimen as the current control, desktop and mobile behavior, print-equivalent proof, accessibility result, critique and revision record, and a cross-product transfer decision for ONE, Join and Liming Library.

## Invalid proof

A named motif list, generic engraving filter, direct reuse of original Dore plates as interface decoration, placeholder lines or labels, a moodboard without reusable source assets, or a single static mockup without browser and print evidence do not prove completion.
