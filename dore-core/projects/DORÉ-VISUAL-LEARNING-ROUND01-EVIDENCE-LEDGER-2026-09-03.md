## Evidence judgment

### What is real

Round 01 is a legitimate structured learning program rather than a moodboard. It defines a repeatable observation method covering eye path, hierarchy, grid, typography, information architecture, responsive behavior, transfer, and refusal-to-copy. Its five cases were deliberately selected to expose conflicting successful strategies rather than converge prematurely on one aesthetic.

The existing H01–H07 are useful hypotheses, especially:

- archive/database structure and first-entry experience should be separable;
- search, curated discovery and editorial interpretation can coexist;
- a strong motif should vary in intensity rather than become wallpaper;
- cards belong mainly at discovery/choice layers while deep reading should reduce fragmentation;
- one corpus may need multiple entry models;
- curation must remain linked to underlying source/provenance;
- visual pace should differ among fast utility, exploratory discovery and slow immersive reading while preserving one family identity.

### What is not yet proven

The source file itself sets a stronger completion gate than the current evidence satisfies. No durable evidence was found in this bounded pass for all five cases having completed structured observations, desktop/mobile comparison, full typography inspection, explicit contradiction analysis, ten or more transferable hypotheses, and prototype verification.

Therefore:

- `VIS-LEARN` must remain `ACTIVE_PARALLEL`;
- H01–H07 must remain hypotheses, not Brand V1 rules;
- current purpose-built Doré-derived asset work under `VIS-GRAMMAR` may serve as prototype material, but asset generation by itself is not evidence that Round 01 has graduated;
- one Journal specimen cannot prove H07’s cross-surface pacing claim; proof must include at least one fast utility surface, one exploratory surface, and one slow editorial/reading surface.

## Canonical design-learning boundary

The useful relationship between `VIS-LEARN` and `VIS-GRAMMAR` is:

`case observation → explicit hypothesis → deliberately different real-surface prototype → rendered/mobile/typographic inspection → contradiction/failure analysis → retain/reject/revise → only then visual-grammar promotion`

For the current Doré-derived website-material direction, this means purpose-built light, star, water, sky/cloud, stone/wall, city-edge, paper/surface, divider/edge and related assets should be evaluated as **design hypotheses in use**, not accepted merely because they look Doré-like in isolation.

Original Doré works remain a separate curated-content/exhibition path. They are not required as raw interface decoration and do not substitute for purpose-built website assets.
