Until that convergence is implemented and verified, both existing paths remain production/implementation reality and must not be deleted merely because a more ambitious target architecture exists.

## Current quality judgment

The intake is strong as an engineering-learning contract because it prevents two common failures: copying open-source code without understanding/licensing it, and declaring capability from source accumulation. It also improves the Search evaluation target from keyword matching toward natural Chinese biblical reasoning and traceable connection.

Its main weakness is the gap between breadth of intake and proven transfer. A large registry can become research debt if individual sources are not converted into bounded trials and measurable Search deltas. The highest-leverage next proof is therefore one narrow end-to-end adoption experiment, not another expansion wave.
