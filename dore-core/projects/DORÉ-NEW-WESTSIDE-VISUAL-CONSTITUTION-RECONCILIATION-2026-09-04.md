## Reconciliation findings

1. **The visual constitution supersedes narrower style-memory interpretations.** The governing method is `Expand first. Converge later.` Any old interpretation that reduces New Westside to white/Living Paper + gold rules + large serif type is non-canonical.
2. **Permanent DNA is now explicit:** `LIGHT · LINE · PAPER · ENGRAVING · ARCHITECTURE / 光 · 線 · 紙 · 刻 · 築`; permanent information/motion grammar is `BRICK · BATTLEMENT · FLOW / 磚 · 垛 · 流`, with 5:8 as the highest editorial-gravity mother proportion and Huarongdao-style yielding/recomposition.
3. **Surface/material distinction is canonical:** Living Paper is paper color; Papyric Texture/Sacred Surface is paper character derived from Temple Stone source material + pale-gold treatment. Scripture must not collapse into fake-antique paper, and Temple Stone is not a universal background.
4. **Doré has two separate roles:**
   - Doré as **design language**: line, atmosphere, light, scale and material grammar, expressed through `LIGHT / SKY / STAR / WATER / CITY / EARTH / TRACE` asset families at Full / Fragment / Trace strengths;
   - Doré as **curated artist/editorial content**: original works belong in explicit editorial/exhibition treatment and must not become anonymous UI texture.
5. This separation strengthens the already recorded purpose-built fresh-redrawing direction: interface assets should inherit Doré grammar rather than paste/crop original artwork as the default surface treatment.
6. **Color architecture is more mature than the earlier Sacred Palette-only interpretation.** Sacred Palette values are provenance/brand primitives; the target implementation is primitives → perceptual families (OKLCH preferred where supported) → semantic tokens → modes → component tokens → accessibility/gamut gates → portable DTCG-compatible authority. Color is metadata, not architecture; gold is reveal/trace, not generic primary fill.
7. **Typography is a practical classical/free-license foundation, not a permanent font lock.** Cormorant Garamond + Noto Serif TC are current practical choices; future replacements may be tested if they materially improve bilingual coherence, reading, responsiveness and licensing.
8. **Website vs Journal boundary is canonical:** the website is the city; each Journal issue is an exhibition within the city. Journal art direction must not silently become the permanent brand system.
9. **Current visual state is explicitly non-final.** `NEW-WESTSIDE-CURRENT.md` says the process is in EXPANSION and candidate directions remain experimental references only.
10. Doré Design's accepted structured-workspace foundation supports future design production, but Build/technical acceptance does not satisfy D4 rendered aesthetic readback/correction, full mature-template proof, or New Westside style acceptance.

## Durable learned principles

- Inherit grammar, not material.
- Original cultural works can be content while a derived visual language becomes interface grammar; the two must remain visibly separate.
- Paper color and paper character are different design primitives.
- Visual identity is carried by spatial/editorial grammar before color accents.
- Expansion breadth and maturity depth are independent axes; premature convergence weakens the system.
- Technical design-tool PASS is not aesthetic maturity.
