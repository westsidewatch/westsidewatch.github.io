## Durable capability already earned

Even without Round 01 completion, the corpus establishes reusable evaluation habits:

- separate entrance/discovery/reading modes instead of forcing one UI density everywhere;
- distinguish search, curated discovery and editorial interpretation;
- treat visual pace as a product-specific variable;
- keep curation connected to source objects/provenance;
- treat strong visual motifs as variable-intensity grammar rather than wallpaper;
- compare conflicting successful systems instead of converging prematurely on one aesthetic;
- require typography/mobile inspection and prototype proof before promoting observations into brand rules.

These are retained as learning principles, not completion claims.
