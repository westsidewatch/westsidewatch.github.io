## Canonical interpretation

`dore-core/benchmarks/RESEARCHER-GRADUATION.md` is a benchmark specification, not proof that Doré has globally graduated as Researcher.

The specification requires a research response envelope preserving answer, canonical context, evidence, sources, alternative views, confidence, uncertainty, Westside relevance, provenance and human-review need. It defines ten benchmark families spanning biblical recognition, entities, language, geography, interpretation comparison, source criticism, Westside provenance, subtitle correction, visual research and librarianship.

Its graduation rule explicitly states that no numeric threshold exists yet because the first real benchmark dataset has not been established. Graduation requires both accuracy and epistemic discipline reviewed by human authority.
