## Findings and classifications

1. **`VIS-LEARN` remains a genuine `ACTIVE_PARALLEL` learning workstream, not a redesign project disguised as research.** Round 01 explicitly requires case studies to identify problem, hierarchy, editorial rhythm, typography/grid, image behavior, index-to-depth transition, digital/print relationship, transferable principles and non-transferable elements before Westside prototypes. This is consistent with the Master Register's “modern design/editorial/spatial design education” line.

2. **The visual case-study corpus is not completion evidence for `VIS-GRAMMAR` or `BRAND-V1`.** Its strongest current outputs are hypotheses: visual order before textual overload; data structure is not entrance structure; simple index + rich interior; recurring DNA plus permission for individual works to become distinct worlds; Doré engraving as source grammar rather than wallpaper; print as its own medium rather than a PDF export. These remain discovery inputs until tested in real Westside prototypes.

3. **The “record-cover / city-window” concept is `DISCOVERY`, not a governing design standard.** The source itself says to explore rather than prematurely standardize square/5:8/mixed ratios, battlement/window geometry, mobile behavior and literal-vs-compositional use. It must not be resurrected later as if already approved system grammar.

4. **`JOURNAL-PRINT`, `DORE-DISTRIBUTION`, and `VIS-LEARN` are adjacent but not duplicate workstreams.** `VIS-LEARN` develops visual/editorial judgment; `JOURNAL-PRINT` develops the physical independent-magazine destination and production/readiness model; `DORE-DISTRIBUTION` develops ethical discovery/return/depth systems across products. They converge in Journal prototypes but solve different durable problems.

5. **The editorial-publishing research strengthens the canonical rule that Journal is an amplification of Westside grammar, not an unrelated sub-brand.** The main ecosystem should contain the shared grammar; Journal may intensify typography, pacing, materiality, issue identity and art direction. This is directionally compatible with `VIS-GRAMMAR` and `BRAND-V1`, but does not complete either.

6. **Christian publishing is explicitly a content/operating corpus before it is a visual model.** The research warns against copying conventional ministry-magazine appearance and instead prioritizes editorial architecture, theological scope, recurring departments, audience, distribution, funding and longevity. Christian Culture's use of historical woodcuts is a comparison case, not permission to copy its treatment.

7. **Chinese/Asian editorial research is a first-class requirement, not a decorative diversity add-on.** Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan, Canada and wider Chinese-language publishing are named as evidence families for bilingual/CJK typography, editorial identity, material production and survival models. This supports the canonical `GLOBAL-CHINESE` workstream and prevents an Anglo-American-only visual benchmark corpus.

8. **`DORE-DISTRIBUTION` has a durable ethical metric hierarchy already articulated:** reach → useful engagement → depth → voluntary return/relationship → gospel opportunity. The source explicitly forbids inferring conversion from analytics and rejects spiritual pressure, outrage, addiction, deceptive click-through and manufactured controversy. This is an operating principle to retain as later instrumentation is implemented.

9. **The shared distribution architecture remains a research proposal, not an implemented platform capability.** Search, Journal, Library, ONE, Devotional, Stories and future Conversation are intended to share discovery/relationship logic, but no reviewed evidence in this batch proves a cross-product analytics/ranking/attribution system is live. `DORE-DISTRIBUTION` therefore remains `DISCOVERY / ACTIVE_PARALLEL`.

10. **No completed milestone was discovered in this bounded batch.** The research documents deliberately use hypotheses and required outputs, so no `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` or `COMPLETED_REVISIT_CANDIDATE` promotion is warranted.

11. **No superseded/retired work item was discovered.** The main anti-resurrection boundary is conceptual: the record-cover/city-window model and specific benchmark examples must remain hypotheses/cases until prototype evidence exists; they are not retired, but they are also not canonical visual law.

12. **No new human/environment blocker was discovered.** This research family can continue independently and does not alter P01's `ENVIRONMENT_BLOCKED` condition or subtitle critical-path ordering.
