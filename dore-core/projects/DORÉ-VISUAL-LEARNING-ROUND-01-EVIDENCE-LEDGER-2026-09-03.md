## Current judgment

Round 01 is a real learning corpus and study contract, not a completed design milestone and not Westside brand law.

The source deliberately studies five materially different systems—Emergence Magazine, Rijksmuseum Collection Online, Curationist, The Creative Independent, and It's Nice That—to avoid premature convergence on one aesthetic. The durable method is stronger than simple reference gathering: each case must be evaluated for eye path, structural ordering, transferability, refusal-to-copy, typography, mobile recomposition, information architecture, and cross-product applicability.

Seven first-synthesis hypotheses (`H01–H07`) exist, but the source explicitly labels them as hypotheses rather than rules. The most durable current hypotheses are:

- collection/database structure and entrance/discovery experience should not be identical;
- Search, curated discovery, and editorial interpretation can coexist as different modes over one resource graph;
- strong identity does not require maximal use of the strongest motif;
- card density belongs primarily to discovery/choice layers, while deep reading should reduce fragmentation;
- one archive can expose multiple mental entry models without duplicating the underlying data;
- editorial curation should stay connected to source objects and provenance;
- visual pace should differ by product role while preserving a recognizable shared grammar.

These hypotheses are useful learning outputs, but none independently qualifies as `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` capability evidence or as a canonical visual rule.

## Completion boundary

The source itself defines a strict Round 01 completion gate. The round is not complete until all of the following are evidenced:

1. structured observations for all five cases;
2. desktop/mobile differences inspected where accessible;
3. typography and information-architecture observations recorded;
4. contradictions between cases explicitly compared;
5. at least ten transferable hypotheses formed;
6. no hypothesis promoted into brand law without prototype evidence;
7. the Master Work Register updated with demonstrated learning.

Current persisted evidence contains five case briefs and seven synthesis hypotheses, but does not contain the full structured-observation set, explicit desktop/mobile and typography inspection records for every case, the contradiction comparison, ten hypotheses, or prototype-verification receipts. Therefore `VIS-LEARN` remains `ACTIVE_PARALLEL` and Round 01 remains incomplete.

## Relationship to VIS-GRAMMAR

This learning corpus should inform `VIS-GRAMMAR` but cannot directly legislate it. The governing visual doctrine already established elsewhere remains the stronger boundary:

- original Doré works are curated content;
- Doré-derived interface materials are purpose-built assets;
- production Journal is the control surface;
- visual hypotheses become grammar only after real prototype evidence.

Round 01 should therefore feed prototype questions rather than supply style templates.
