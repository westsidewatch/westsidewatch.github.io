## Guardrails

- Do not infer commercial readiness from a canonical product-direction document.
- Do not treat provisional `Dore / 多寫` naming as trademark/market validated.
- Do not allow AI to silently overwrite protected human authorship.
- Do not fork web/book/Journal into disconnected canonical content systems when the shared studio can support them.
- Do not let commercial work destabilize current editor engineering or P01.
- `VIS-GRAMMAR` remains independently evidence-gated; publishing-editor integration does not complete the Westside visual grammar.
- Book 01 / Brand Bible research sources teach method, architecture and pedagogy; they do not supersede the New Westside Visual Constitution or authorize surface copying.
- Book 01/Storybook exploration must not mutate the production homepage/site without separate user aesthetic approval.
- Book 01 interactive publication behavior must preserve a coherent print/static equivalent and reading order.
- Significant New Westside user-visible project dialogue is primary provenance evidence; summaries may index it but must not silently replace the preserved dialogue when provenance matters. Hidden chain-of-thought, tool payloads, secrets, private runtime internals and unrelated personal data are explicitly outside that archive rule.
- New Westside source selection is governed by `SOURCE-BOUNDARIES.md`: use appropriate official/neutral design-history sources; do not import disallowed religious hosts, ritual/sacred-symbol systems or unrelated devotional authority into Westside design research. If a useful secular artifact is found only through a disallowed host, replace the host rather than discarding the artifact automatically.
- Christian motifs such as light, water, city, gate, watchman, stone and related symbols are semantic/theological material, not free decorative texture. Their design use must be grounded in Scripture, documented project intent or actual editorial content; AI aesthetic output must not be treated as divine revelation.
- The previously rejected `buddhistuniversity.net` mirror of the NYCTA Graphics Standards Manual is `SUPERSEDED / DO_NOT_SURFACE` as a project source URL; NYCTA design study itself may continue through an appropriate official or neutral source.
- Book 02 narrative vividness must not manufacture biblical/historical evidence.
- Book 02's academic appendix remains a separate scholarly proof layer; a hypothesis or rhetorical continuity may not substitute for textual/lexical/historical evidence.
- Method references such as `萬曆十五年`, `耶路撒冷三千年`, and `時間簡史` are used for transferable structural methods, not recognizable stylistic imitation.
