## Durable learning

The strongest reusable result is the separation of film construction into an explicit authored motion grammar before final artwork is added:

`narrative beats → camera spine data → executable motion lab → visual/art asset integration → rendered acceptance`

This keeps camera decisions inspectable, testable and revisable instead of hiding them inside a finished video render. It also creates a possible future bridge from Doré's structured design/visual systems into film without claiming that bridge is already proven.

## Revisit / completion trigger

Do not promote the experiment beyond active experimental status until one bounded scene proves all of the following from the same authored spine: purpose-built Doré visual assets, stable camera playback, deliberate light/scene transitions, Scripture/text treatment where applicable, mobile/desktop render acceptance, and a persisted visual review showing that the resulting motion communicates the intended biblical time/space transition without relying on conventional cuts.
