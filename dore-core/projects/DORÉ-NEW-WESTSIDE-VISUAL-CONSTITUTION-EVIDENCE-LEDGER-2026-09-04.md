## Reconciliation findings

1. **The constitution resolves an important ambiguity in the Doré asset direction.** The governing rule is `Inherit the grammar, not the material.` Original Doré works are Editorial Content; Doré-derived interface/design language is a separate purpose-built asset system. This is consistent with the earlier Sweep finding that curated Doré originals belong in exhibition/content cards while website grammar should be freshly drawn from Westside principles.
2. **The purpose-built Doré family is now explicitly canonical:** `LIGHT / SKY / STAR / WATER / CITY / EARTH / TRACE`, each able to exist at `Full / Fragment / Trace` strength. This is stronger than a generic engraving-filter concept and provides a concrete asset taxonomy for the active build.
3. **Papyric Texture is not fake antique paper.** The constitution defines Living Paper as color and Papyric Texture/Sacred Surface as character derived from Temple Stone source material plus restrained pale-gold treatment. Temple Stone is material/provenance, not a universal background or brand color.
4. **The permanent grammar is architectural and editorial, not decorative:** `Brick · Battlement · Flow`, 5:8 Editorial Gravity, Huarongdao-style yielding/recomposition and the city/wall horizon are information/motion rules. Mobile must recompose rather than shrink the desktop wall.
5. **Gold usage is semantically constrained:** `Living Paper carries. Ink Black prints. Watch Night watches. First Light Gold reveals.` Gold is highlight/trace rather than generic primary accent; color is metadata, not architecture.
6. **Typography is a licensed-practical classical foundation, not a font lock.** Cormorant Garamond + Noto Serif TC are current free working choices; replacements are allowed only when they materially improve bilingual reading/display/responsive behavior and licensing remains acceptable.
7. **Expansion and convergence are separate states.** `White + gold + large serif` is explicitly rejected as a sufficient Westside style. A mature template must prove complete content rhythm, real material strategy, interaction, reduced motion, bilingual stress, desktop/tablet/mobile transformation, accessibility/keyboard behavior and real browser evidence.
8. **Technical design-tool PASS is not aesthetic acceptance.** Doré Design 0.9 remains a bounded verified structured-design foundation, but visual/style acceptance remains user-authority gated. The constitution explicitly forbids Doré self-judgment from silently becoming brand approval.
9. **Audience context is separated from visual locality.** Toronto Chinese-speaking readership is an audience/communication context, not a requirement for skyline/map/civic motifs; Vaughan is explicitly outside the permanent visual core.
10. The current Vol.00 numbered sequence must come from `data/volumes/vol-00.yaml`; historical structural labels that conflict with it are superseded as implementation inputs.

## Current quality judgment

The constitution is substantially stronger than earlier loose style notes because it converts recurring visual intuitions into an explicit system of semantic primitives, material rules, editorial geometry, motion grammar, asset taxonomy, color architecture, maturity gates and authority boundaries. It also protects against the specific failure mode already observed in visual experiments: reducing Westside to paper/white + gold rules + large serif typography.

Its main weakness is not conceptual but evidential. The written contract is ahead of implementation. The purpose-built Doré asset family, token system, mature responsive templates and real cross-product/print proof are not yet demonstrated as one coherent accepted system.

## Durable capability / principle retention

- inherit visual grammar rather than copy source material;
- keep curated artist content separate from derived interface grammar;
- treat Christian motifs as semantic/editorial systems, not decorative texture;
- separate paper color from paper character;
- use editorial weight to drive responsive layout/motion rather than card equality;
- make color subordinate to spatial/editorial hierarchy;
- require real bilingual/responsive/accessibility/browser evidence before calling a visual concept mature;
- preserve explicit human aesthetic authority for convergence.
