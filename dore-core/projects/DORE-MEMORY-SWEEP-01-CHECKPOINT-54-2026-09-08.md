## Reconciliation findings

1. The September 7 Doré Exploration documents are substantive architecture/resource-selection evidence, but they are not implementation proof. The mature-resource baseline and Build/Borrow/Adapt/Reject audit are `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` only as bounded exploration/audit artifacts; operational local multicore remains `DISCOVERY / READY_FOR_POC`.
2. Doré sovereignty is preserved: external projects are candidate organs, algorithms, adapters and reference implementations. None may take over Knowledge Authority, Context semantics, Reflex, product semantics, project authority or evolution policy.
3. A useful supersession is now explicit. The earlier intuition that multi-core requires many simultaneously resident models is superseded by a lighter architecture: multiple **virtual capabilities** remain available while physical models are selected by minimum sufficient capability and managed independently by residency policy.
4. The strongest new engineering distinction is `capability routing` versus `physical residency routing`. Products should request virtual capabilities only; deterministic/no-model paths should be preferred; a tiny frequent core may stay hot; specialists should hot-swap only when required; LRU/TTL/memory guard should release idle pressure.
5. The resource audit strengthens a deletion-first rule: do not continue bespoke implementations of generic model serving, protocol, gateway, router, semantic document search, coding-agent loop, scheduler, loop recovery, memory framework, graph platform, STT or Mac sandbox until mature candidate source/POCs demonstrate a real gap.
6. Candidate priority is now durably separated: QMD, wake, oMLX / mlx-serve / macMLX and Osaurus are POC/source-audit candidates; vLLM Semantic Router, mnemos/localmem, Graphiti/LongMemory/grit/MOOSEDev/ASKS are primarily borrow/absorb/reference layers unless later evidence justifies direct dependency adoption.
7. LongMemory remains a restored first-tier historical candidate, but its repository identity/details still require re-verification before implementation decisions. The exploration baseline correctly preserves this uncertainty rather than inventing confidence.
8. No current Master Register status needs promotion/demotion from this batch. `EVOLUTION` already provides the canonical home for transferable capability acquisition, while `RUNTIME`, `NERVOUS-SYSTEM`, `SEARCH` and `CORE` retain their current operational classifications. The new ledger is the durable evidence boundary until a real local multicore POC earns a stronger register entry.
9. No new `HUMAN_DECISION_BLOCKED` or `ENVIRONMENT_BLOCKED` condition was created. The previously known P01 production audio/transcription environment dependency remains unchanged and was not touched.
