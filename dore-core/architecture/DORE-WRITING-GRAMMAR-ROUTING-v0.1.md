# Doré Writing Grammar Routing v0.1

Status: ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT / NON-INVASIVE ADMISSION
Scope: Doré writing and publishing capability family
Origin consumer: 《神很遠，神很近｜天國語言極簡史》

## 1. Why this exists

Real writing projects generate reusable writing judgment. Doré must be able to learn from those projects without turning every surface into one house style and without allowing a project-specific grammar to become Core authority by accident.

This contract adds a routing layer around writing grammar. It does **not** replace the current Core, capability registry, inference boundary, Theology Authority / Theology Gate, Source Capability Envelope, Book Intelligence, publishing pipeline, or surface-specific runtimes. It introduces no provider dependency and claims no identity authority.

The routing action is:

`Universal Grammar → Reference Proven Project Grammars → Relevance Judge → Transferable Capability Extraction → Project Contamination Block → Current Project Grammar → Surface Grammar → writing / revision`

The reference step is part of Universal Grammar invocation. It is not an optional second workflow left to model discretion.

## 2. Authority order

Writing grammar never outranks source or author authority.

Required order:

1. Author / canonical source authority
2. Project thesis / editorial intent authority
3. Theology Authority and Theology Gate where applicable
4. Evidence / source admission boundaries
5. Doré Universal Writing Grammar
6. Relevant proven Project Grammars as reference-only experience
7. Current Project Grammar
8. Surface Grammar / publication constraints
9. Generated prose candidate

A grammar may shape expression, research movement, scene magnification, rhythm and editorial construction. It may not silently rewrite the author's thesis, alter canonical source identity, invent evidence, or promote an interpretation into authority.

## 3. Universal Grammar invocation contract

Every substantial Doré writing/revision invocation should perform one compound action:

### A. LOAD UNIVERSAL
Apply stable cross-project writing capabilities and diagnostics.

Initial capabilities admitted from the Tian Guo Yu Yan experiments include:
- material carries thought;
- earned compression rather than scheduled aphorism;
- selective scene magnification;
- scale movement without performative signposting;
- submerged recurrence / delayed naming of echoes;
- curiosity before immediate utility when the excursion remains inside the work's attractor;
- re-entry rather than automatic mini-conclusion;
- narrator pressure treated as scarce and earned;
- human irregularity in paragraph and sentence pressure;
- reader discovery without congratulatory narration.

Initial cross-project diagnostics include:
- performative discovery;
- dense landing sentences;
- automatic quotable lines;
- premature explanation of echoes;
- fake suspense;
- mechanical `not A but B` reversal;
- synopsis where scene magnification is required;
- essay-like closure on every digression.

These are capabilities/diagnostics, not a mandatory prose voice.

### B. REFERENCE PROVEN PROJECT GRAMMARS
Search the Grammar Library for project grammars that have been validated by real writing.

Reference is mandatory as an action; borrowing is optional and relevance-gated.

A candidate grammar may be relevant by:
- narrative problem;
- genre;
- evidence density;
- character handling;
- time-scale movement;
- dialogue;
- theological exposition;
- historical reconstruction;
- motif recurrence;
- research digression;
- publication surface;
- other demonstrated writing problem.

Similarity of topic alone is insufficient.

### C. RELEVANCE JUDGE
For each candidate experience, determine whether it solves a real problem in the current manuscript. Record the reason for admission or rejection when provenance is retained.

### D. EXTRACT TRANSFERABLE CAPABILITY
Borrow the operation, not the voice.

Examples:
- `slow scene when emotional direction changes` may transfer;
- a specific cadence from another author's book does not automatically transfer;
- `underground-waterway recurrence` may transfer as a structural technique;
- the actual motifs `wind / Babel / brick / name` do not transfer from Tian Guo Yu Yan;
- a dialogue project may contribute interruption / silence handling without exporting its characters or tone.

### E. PROJECT CONTAMINATION BLOCK
Reject material that belongs to the source project's identity rather than its reusable craft.

Blocked by default:
- project-specific thesis;
- project-specific motifs and symbolic vocabulary;
- character voice;
- chapter inner actions;
- proprietary sentence templates;
- repeated signature phrases;
- project-specific theological conclusions;
- surface-specific visual/editorial conventions unless explicitly requested.

### F. CURRENT PROJECT GRAMMAR
The current work then establishes or updates its own grammar. It may accept, modify or reject referenced techniques. Its own author/source authority remains superior.

### G. SURFACE GRAMMAR
Finally adapt to the publication surface: Book, Journal, ONE, Multiwrite/成書, Dawn-linked publication or other admitted surfaces. Surface grammar may alter density, navigation, visual breathing, excerpt behavior and editorial packaging; it must not overwrite the project's voice.

## 4. Grammar Library

Doré should maintain a library of proven Project Grammars rather than a style-template catalogue.

A Project Grammar becomes referenceable only after real-text validation. A speculative prompt or untested style note is not `proven`.

Recommended metadata:

- `grammarId`
- `projectId`
- `status`: candidate | proven | deprecated
- `validatedOn`: passages / chapters / artifacts
- `problemsSolved`
- `transferableCapabilities`
- `nonTransferableIdentity`
- `surfaceContexts`
- `evidenceRefs`
- `promotionCandidates`
- `version`

The first reference source is the Tian Guo Yu Yan writing experiment chain:
- Pre-Huang v1;
- Huang study;
- Post-Huang v2;
- v1/v2 style anatomy;
- House-Grammar v3.

Its project-specific material remains isolated. Only demonstrated transferable capabilities are candidates for Universal admission.

## 5. Promotion loop

The learning loop is:

`real writing → Project Grammar → validation → reusable capability candidate → cross-project validation → Universal promotion`

A rule does not enter Universal merely because it worked once.

Promotion requires evidence that the capability remains useful outside the originating project's identity. Until then it remains available through `Reference Proven Project Grammars`.

This creates asymmetric safety:
- new projects can learn from mature projects immediately through reference;
- Core changes more slowly through promotion.

## 6. Isolation and conflict resolution

If grammars conflict:

1. author/source authority wins;
2. current Project Grammar wins over referenced Project Grammar;
3. explicit current editorial intent wins over generic Universal preference unless it violates a higher authority/gate;
4. referenced grammar is advisory only;
5. Surface Grammar cannot force project-identity convergence.

No Project Grammar may mutate Universal Grammar at runtime.
No reference call may modify another project's grammar.
No writing surface may become a second canonical source of grammar authority.

## 7. Compatibility with current Core upgrades

This contract is intentionally non-invasive.

It does not:
- create a second Core;
- replace capability routing;
- alter current inference/provider boundaries;
- bypass A2A or local inference architecture;
- alter Source Capability Envelope admission;
- alter Book Intelligence schemas;
- alter Theology Authority / Theology Gate ordering;
- alter canonical Work/Edition identity;
- introduce runtime dependency on Tian Guo Yu Yan;
- require every consumer to load every project grammar;
- make a writing project an authority over Core.

Implementation should attach this routing behavior to the existing writing/publishing capability family and registry rather than establish a parallel pipeline.

If an active Core consolidation changes registry paths or capability names, this contract follows the consolidated registry. It must not pin or fork an obsolete path.

## 8. First acceptance test

Use a new manuscript that is not Tian Guo Yu Yan.

PASS requires:
1. Universal Grammar is loaded;
2. the system performs `Reference Proven Project Grammars`;
3. Tian Guo Yu Yan may be retrieved as relevant experience;
4. at least one transferable technique can be admitted when useful;
5. Tian Guo Yu Yan-specific motifs, thesis, chapter actions and voice do not leak;
6. the new manuscript forms its own Project Grammar;
7. author/source authority remains unchanged;
8. the routing uses the existing Core capability path rather than a parallel writing pipeline.

A strong first heterogeneous consumer would be a real Westside Watch manuscript such as 《十字架的影子》, because similarity is sufficient to test transfer while identity is different enough to expose contamination.

## 9. Core principle

> Doré should remember how a writing problem was solved without forcing the next work to sound like the previous one.

Every completed work may make Doré a more experienced writer. No completed work gets to become the voice of every work that follows.
