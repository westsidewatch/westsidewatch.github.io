# Researcher 01 — ONE Lab

Status: ACTIVE FIRST PASS
Established: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

The first lesson of Doré's Researcher education is to run through ONE as a real Bible-study work environment.

ONE is not an authority corpus. It is an internship surface containing real study structure, editorial synthesis, source links, maps, timelines, cross-references, questions, visual assets and product assumptions. Doré must learn how to extract research tasks from it without importing ONE prose as Core truth.

## First-pass source surface

The first pass covers the current ONE study system, including:
- the 66-book shell and study-book registry;
- Genesis chapter studies;
- Matthew chapter studies;
- 1–2 Thessalonians chapter studies;
- Samuel study structures currently present;
- chapter story / position / route;
- map source + route legends;
- chronology/timeline;
- background notes;
- scout observations;
- cross references and full-text connections;
- Gospel/book comparison structures;
- study questions and preparation tasks;
- local study-group progress as product workflow, not research evidence.

## What Doré is learning

### 1. Research-question extraction

A ONE chapter exposes multiple researchable prompts. Doré should be able to convert fields such as `questions`, `scout`, `background`, `map`, `timeline`, `connections` and `harmony/comparison` into research tasks without assuming their embedded answers are true.

### 2. Evidence-layer discrimination

Every ONE element must be routed into one of four layers:

- `SCRIPTURE_EVIDENCE` — explicit biblical text/reference.
- `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_POINTER` — a map, chronology, image, lexicon or other linked source that can be independently consulted.
- `EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS` — ONE's story, position, background explanation, thematic label or comparison prose.
- `DIAGNOSTIC_PROMPT` — a question, scout item, preparation instruction or product failure that should stimulate study.

Only the first layer is Scripture evidence by itself. External pointers require source evaluation. Editorial synthesis can generate hypotheses but cannot enter Core merely because ONE says it. Diagnostic prompts are stimuli, not answers.

### 3. Research decomposition

For each chapter Doré should be able to ask separately:
- What does the passage explicitly say?
- Which people, places, institutions and chronology claims need external support?
- Which original-language questions are implied?
- Which cross-text connections are explicit quotation/allusion versus editorial comparison?
- Which claims are uncertain, traditional or reconstructed?
- What evidence would falsify an editorial synthesis?

### 4. Product-to-education feedback

ONE may reveal recurring gaps in Doré. A recurring gap may become a learning signal, but product wording must not become training truth.

Example:
`ONE question → Doré cannot resolve → capability diagnosis → study through admitted evidence → transfer exam → possible Core/Reflex consolidation → improved ONE/Search behavior`

## First-pass observations from current ONE

The current ONE corpus already presents useful research pressure:

- Genesis explicitly distinguishes chapters where a historical route cannot honestly be mapped from chapters with route evidence.
- Genesis map registry keeps source-map route legends distinct from chapter story routes.
- Matthew combines Gospel harmony, maps, chronology, background and cross-references around chapter questions.
- Thessalonians connects epistolary interpretation to Acts, geography, chronology and Pauline comparison.
- Samuel has detailed map-route provenance and fallback/source handling.
- The public ONE application also contains local progress/group behavior; this is user workflow state and must never be treated as biblical evidence.

These are valuable research habits, not merely content to memorize.

## Self Exam

Doré must inspect current ONE structures and identify whether each sampled field is Scripture evidence, an external source pointer, editorial synthesis or a diagnostic prompt.

It must also identify at least one research task from each of these families:
- textual / passage structure;
- entity;
- geography;
- chronology;
- cross-reference;
- comparative reading;
- background/history;
- uncertainty/evidence boundary.

## Transfer Exam

Doré must apply the same evidence discrimination to a chapter/book not used as the teaching example. Passing Matthew examples does not prove transfer to Genesis, Thessalonians or Samuel.

## Blind Exam

Blind checks must include assertions such as:
- a ONE `story` statement is not Scripture evidence;
- a Holy Light map link is a source pointer, not proof until evaluated;
- a map route must not be invented when Scripture/source does not support one;
- a study `question` cannot be treated as its own answer;
- localStorage study progress is product state, never research evidence;
- a cross-reference relationship may be editorial and needs classification before being asserted as quotation/allusion.

## Completion condition

Reserved milestone: `RESEARCHER_01_ONE_LAB_COMPLETE`

The first pass is complete only when Doré can demonstrate evidence-layer discrimination and research-question extraction across multiple ONE book families without promoting ONE editorial prose into Core.

ONE is then retained as a continuing internship environment throughout Researcher education, not marked "finished forever".
