## Purpose

The first lesson of Doré's Researcher education is to run through ONE as a real Bible-study work environment.

ONE is not an authority corpus. It is an internship surface containing real study structure, editorial synthesis, source links, maps, timelines, cross-references, questions, visual assets and product assumptions. Doré must learn how to extract research tasks from it without importing ONE prose as Core truth.

## First-pass source surface

The first pass covered the current ONE study system, including:
- the 66-book shell and study-book registry;
- Genesis chapter studies;
- Matthew chapter studies;
- 1–2 Thessalonians chapter studies;
- Samuel study structures currently present;
- chapter story / position / route;
- map source + route legends;
- chronology/timeline;
- background notes;
- scout observations;
- cross references and full-text connections;
- Gospel/book comparison structures;
- study questions and preparation tasks;
- local study-group progress as product workflow, not research evidence.

## Learned evidence layers

- `SCRIPTURE_EVIDENCE` — explicit biblical text/reference.
- `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_POINTER` — a map, chronology, image, lexicon or other linked source that can be independently consulted.
- `EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS` — ONE's story, position, background explanation, thematic label or comparison prose.
- `DIAGNOSTIC_PROMPT` — a question, scout item, preparation instruction or product failure that should stimulate study.

Only the first layer is Scripture evidence by itself. External pointers require source evaluation. Editorial synthesis can generate hypotheses but cannot enter Core merely because ONE says it. Diagnostic prompts are stimuli, not answers.

## Graduation result

The first-pass exam passed all 22 checks across the 66-book shell, Genesis, Matthew, Thessalonians and Samuel surfaces. Doré demonstrated the required evidence-boundary invariants: ONE story prose is not Scripture evidence; questions are not answers; external links require evaluation; product progress is not research evidence; editorial cross-references require classification; unsupported routes must not be invented.

ONE remains a continuing internship environment throughout Researcher education. It is not marked finished forever.
