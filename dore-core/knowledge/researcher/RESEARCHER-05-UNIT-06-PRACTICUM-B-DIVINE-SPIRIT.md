## Direct Second Temple evidence assembled
### Jubilees 1
James VanderKam's translation of Jubilees 1:19–23 preserves a restoration prayer/promise in which God is asked to create a just/pure spirit and a holy spirit for Israel, followed by the promise that God will create a holy spirit for them and purify them so they will no longer turn away. This is strong evidence that eschatological inward renewal/obedience Spirit language continued and developed in a Second Temple Jewish corpus. It is not by itself evidence for a later Trinitarian ontology.

### 1QS / Community Rule
1QS 3:13–4:26 contains a two-spirit moral/cosmic framework (truth versus perversity) and 1QS 4:20–21 uses spirit-of-holiness / spirit-of-truth language in an eschatological purification setting. This overlaps lexically/thematically with biblical cleansing/renewal traditions, but the Qumran configuration is not interchangeable with either Jubilees or NT pneumatology. Its dualistic anthropology/cosmology must remain corpus-local.

## Counter-checks
1. Shared phrase `holy spirit` does not prove identical ontology across Isaiah/Jubilees/1QS/NT — PASS.
2. Qumran `spirit of truth` language cannot be equated automatically with John's Paraclete solely by verbal similarity — PASS.
3. Jubilees' promised created holy spirit for restored Israel can be treated as evidence of Second Temple reception/development of restoration-Spirit motifs, but not as proof that Jubilees teaches Nicene personhood — PASS.
4. Diversity between Jubilees and 1QS is evidence against a single homogeneous Second Temple pneumatology — PASS.
