## Question
How should Doré model divine/Holy Spirit language across Hebrew Bible → diverse Second Temple witnesses → New Testament without flattening corpora or falsely claiming either identity or rupture?

## Evidence gate

### Prior layer retained
- Hebrew-Bible baseline: divine רוח language includes divine presence/activity, empowerment, prophetic agency, creation/life, and promised restoration; later claims must not be retrojected wholesale into every earlier occurrence.
- Second Temple gate already passed: Jubilees 1 uses eschatological holy-spirit/restoration language; 1QS 3–4 uses a distinctive two-spirit/purification scheme. Shared vocabulary does not establish identical ontology, and the witnesses are not one uniform system.

### Synoptic / Lukan layer
- Luke 4:1 presents Jesus as full of the Holy Spirit and led by the Spirit; Luke 4:18 frames Jesus' mission through Isaiah's Spirit/anointing language.
- Acts 2:4 depicts the gathered disciples filled with the Holy Spirit; Acts 2:16–18 explicitly interprets Pentecost through Joel's promise of divine Spirit poured broadly; Acts 2:33, 38–39 links the poured-out Spirit with exalted Jesus, promise and communal reception.
- Development claim: Luke–Acts strongly retains prophetic/anointing/promise continuity while narratively expanding the Spirit's distribution and ecclesial-mission role.

### Pauline layer
- Representative Pauline Spirit discourse (e.g. Romans 8; 1 Corinthians 12) integrates life, belonging to Christ, divine indwelling, prayer/agency and differentiated gifts into sustained community and eschatological reasoning.
- This is not safely reducible to a single Hebrew-Bible empowerment episode, but it also does not require a claim of conceptual rupture: inherited divine-Spirit vocabulary is being used in a denser Christological, communal and eschatological configuration.

### Johannine layer
- John 14:16–17 calls the Spirit of truth 'another Advocate' who will remain with/in disciples; the farewell discourse differentiates Father, Jesus and Advocate while tightly coordinating their presence and mission.
- This configuration should be recorded as Johannine development, not read backward as though every earlier רוח occurrence already expressed the full Johannine discourse.

## Adversarial examination

1. **Flattening test:** 'Jubilees, 1QS, Luke, Paul and John all teach the same Spirit doctrine.' — FAIL as a research claim. Corpora differ in genre, configuration and argumentative role.
2. **Rupture test:** 'Because NT Spirit discourse is denser, it is unrelated to Hebrew-Bible/Second Temple Spirit language.' — FAIL. Luke–Acts explicitly activates prophetic promise/anointing traditions, most visibly Isaiah/Joel.
3. **Retrojection test:** 'John 14 proves every Hebrew-Bible use of רוח already means the complete later Paraclete formulation.' — FAIL. Later canonical development cannot erase earlier textual horizons.
4. **Single-line evolution test:** 'Second Temple Judaism supplies one intermediate Spirit doctrine from which the NT simply descends.' — FAIL. Jubilees and 1QS already demonstrate materially different configurations.
5. **Bounded synthesis test:** 'The evidence supports continuity of divine-Spirit vocabulary and functions alongside real corpus-specific development, with NT texts intensifying communal, eschatological, Christological and enduring-presence patterns.' — PASS, with the qualification that each sub-corpus must retain its own claims and chronology.
