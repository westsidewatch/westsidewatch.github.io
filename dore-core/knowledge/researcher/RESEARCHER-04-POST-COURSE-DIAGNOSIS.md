Classification: `DOMAIN_GAP + SKILL_GAP + CURRICULUM_GAP`.

## Required competencies

The course must train Doré to:
1. stratify evidence by corpus/date/provenance before synthesis;
2. distinguish lexical continuity from conceptual continuity;
3. distinguish development from simple identity and from total discontinuity;
4. use Second Temple Jewish texts as historical witnesses without treating them as one homogeneous theology;
5. compare Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek terminology without assuming translation identity;
6. distinguish historical reconstruction from Christian canonical/theological synthesis;
7. trace a concept through multiple textual stages while preserving competing trajectories;
8. avoid retrojection and “later = original meaning” reasoning;
9. recognize when dating/authorship uncertainty materially changes a developmental argument;
10. transfer the method to unfamiliar concepts rather than memorize Satan/Spirit/canon trajectories.

## Required material families

Before study, independently evaluate:
- primary Hebrew-Bible texts;
- selected Second Temple primary texts with edition/translation provenance (e.g. Dead Sea Scrolls, 1 Enoch, Jubilees, Wisdom/Sirach where relevant);
- New Testament primary texts;
- historically accountable Second Temple Judaism scholarship;
- concept-development/history-of-religions methodology;
- canon/intertextuality scholarship as a separate synthesis layer;
- counterpoint material against linear-evolution and harmonizing-retrojection shortcuts.

No popular apologetic, anti-apologetic, denominational explainer or AI summary may function as the course authority.

## Proposed minimum sequence

1. Evidence stratification, dating and corpus boundaries.
2. Continuity/development/discontinuity method.
3. Second Temple source families and non-homogeneity.
4. Translation-language mediation: Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek continuity limits.
5. Practicum A: `שָׂטָן` / adversary → Second Temple → NT.
6. Practicum B: divine Spirit → Second Temple → NT.
7. Practicum C: Scripture authority / collection / canon trajectory.
8. Blind transfer on an unseen concept (angelology, resurrection, wisdom/Logos, messianism or another evidence-led choice).
9. Historical-vs-canonical synthesis exam.
10. Retention/reopen gate.
