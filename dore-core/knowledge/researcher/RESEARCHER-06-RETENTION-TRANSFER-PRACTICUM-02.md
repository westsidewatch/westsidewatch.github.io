Date: 2026-08-24

## Why this practicum exists
Phase B's frozen classifier transferred 6/6 on its first sealed held-out run, but both dev and held-out cases supplied candidate evidence channels as fixture inputs. That tests evidence classification, not retrieval.

The remaining question is therefore narrower than a new course: can the existing Scripture corpus and graduated Researcher 06 capability generate the right candidates independently, especially when a quotation crosses verse boundaries or when the speaker paraphrases rather than misquotes?

## Probe A — contiguous quotation windows
Construct one-, two-, and when justified three-verse contiguous candidates directly from the actual Scripture corpus. Do not use expected references to build a candidate list. Measure whether the correct window is retrieved within a frozen candidate budget.

Required boundaries:
- windows may not cross book/chapter boundaries unless a future corpus contract explicitly authorizes it;
- preserve verse provenance for every window;
- score the window as one retrieval candidate while retaining component verses;
- compare against current verse-by-verse retrieval;
- preserve first failures.

### Corpus-location result — PASS
The authoritative browser corpus is now identified as `static/dore/search-index.json`, generated by `scripts/build_dore_browser_search_index.py` under schema `dore.browser-search-core.v1`.

The generator pins and records its witnesses/snapshots, including CUV Traditional 1919 and WEBU, and emits canonical verse refs plus Chinese/English verse text. The Bible Search workflow independently asserts a corpus of at least 31,000 verses and canonical control references.

Inspection of `static/dore/dore-search.js` also confirms the implementation gap: current free-text retrieval scores verses independently even though chapter grouping already exists. Explicit reference ranges are supported, but free-text adjacent-window candidate generation is not.
