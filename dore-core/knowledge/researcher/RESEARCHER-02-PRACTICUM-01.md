# Researcher 02 — Practicum 01

Status: ACTIVE
Course: `RESEARCHER-02-RESEARCH-METHOD-I`
Started: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

Turn Research Method I from doctrine into repeated research behavior. Doré must not be rewarded for merely possessing the answer. The assessed object is the path from uncertainty to a bounded, sourced conclusion.

## Practicum set

### A — Unfamiliar theological/textual question
Stimulus: `舊約有聖靈嗎？`

Required decomposition before answering:
- define what the question could mean;
- identify relevant Hebrew vocabulary and distinguish lexical occurrence from theological identity;
- collect explicit OT passages in context;
- classify actions attributed to the Spirit;
- distinguish textual observation from later canonical/theological synthesis;
- seek counter-evidence and alternative readings;
- state what can and cannot be concluded from OT evidence alone.

### B — Geography / chronology transfer
Select a ONE question involving a route, place, or chronological claim from a book family other than the primary example. Require primary-text anchors, external-source provenance, uncertainty, and refusal to invent precision.

### C — Intertext transfer
Select an apparent quotation, allusion, or cross-reference from ONE. Determine whether the relationship is explicit quotation, probable allusion, thematic parallel, or editorial association. Do not inherit ONE's classification automatically.

### D — Blind unseen question
Select an unseen biblical question from a different genre. Doré must generate the research plan without a supplied decomposition template.

## Required research record

For each task record:
1. initial uncertainty map;
2. decomposed subquestions;
3. evidence requirements before searching;
4. sources considered and source-quality judgment;
5. claim/evidence ledger;
6. competing hypotheses where warranted;
7. counter-evidence/falsification attempt;
8. bounded conclusion with calibrated confidence;
9. unresolved questions;
10. next autonomous action;
11. consolidation decision: `REJECT`, `WORKING`, or `CANDIDATE_FOR_EXAM`.

## Pass gate

A practicum cannot pass because its final answer happens to be correct. It passes only if the research path is inspectable and obeys evidence boundaries.

Failure conditions include:
- exact-phrase search treated as research;
- ONE editorial prose promoted to evidence;
- source collected without claim relevance;
- unsupported certainty;
- no counter-check;
- invented geography/chronology;
- immediate Core consolidation without transfer/blind examination.
