# Doré Product–Education Loop

Recorded: 2026-08-22
Status: governing development principle

## Principle

Doré must not be educated in isolation from useful work.

At every genuine Doré milestone, the project must stop and ask:

1. What reliable capability has Doré actually earned?
2. What bounded external job can safely expose that capability now?
3. Which Westside product(s) should consume that capability through Doré Core rather than reimplement it?
4. What does the next desired job reveal about Doré's next educational prerequisites?
5. Is Doré ready to begin learning Westside-specific knowledge, vocabulary, standards and editorial practice, or would doing so prematurely distort the Core?

The loop is:

**Education → Milestone → External Work Node → Real-world feedback → Next job prerequisites → Next education**

Education and product development therefore remain coupled, but neither is allowed to corrupt the other. Product pressure may identify what Doré should learn next; it must not cause unsupported knowledge, hidden inference, or product-specific assumptions to be written into Core as fact.

## Milestone rule

A milestone is not a small capability gate. A milestone closes a meaningful educational stage after end-to-end evidence and testing.

After every milestone, Doré must receive a product/work review before the next long educational stage begins.

## First application after SCRIPTURE_READING_COMPLETE

Milestone: `SCRIPTURE_READING_COMPLETE`

Earned external work node: **Doré Bible Search**.

Bible Search is not a separate intelligence system. It is an external access node into Doré Core. As Doré's education improves, this same search node should become more capable without being rebuilt as an unrelated product.

Initial bounded search capabilities:
- canonical reference lookup;
- Chinese/English textual keyword search where licensed/open witness data permits;
- original-language surface and lemma search;
- morphology-aware search;
- cross-witness/reference correspondence;
- fuzzy-recall candidate retrieval with explicit confidence and provenance.

Consumers may include ONE, Westside Stories, the Westside Watch site, subtitle checking, and future products.

## Forward job: subtitle proofreader

The desired future job **subtitle proofreader** should actively shape the next education plan.

It should begin before full Researcher graduation only as a bounded assistant when its prerequisites are met; it must not pretend to be a mature theological/research proofreader prematurely.

Minimum prerequisites to evaluate:
- robust Chinese and English Scripture phrase retrieval, including fuzzy/partial quotation matching;
- biblical names, places, books, abbreviations and transliteration variants;
- church/Bible vocabulary and common homophones/ASR confusions;
- quotation/allusion confidence handling;
- context-sensitive candidate ranking;
- provenance and uncertainty output;
- a safe correction contract: suggest, cite evidence, never silently rewrite uncertain speech.

Researcher graduation may be required for higher-order claims (historical, theological, interpretive, source-critical), but should not be made an arbitrary blocker for simple Scripture quotation correction if the bounded prerequisite tests already pass.

## Brand familiarization

Doré should not wait until the very end to encounter Westside, but Westside-specific learning must be layered and provenance-marked.

Recommended progression:
1. Core remains product-independent and evidence-first.
2. Once Scripture Reading is complete, begin a bounded **Westside vocabulary/editorial corpus** for retrieval and recognition, clearly separated from universal biblical facts.
3. During later Foundation/Researcher education, expand Westside-specific people, programs, terminology, teaching archive and editorial standards.
4. Only after appropriate benchmarks may Doré use Westside knowledge for interpretation, editorial judgment or autonomous product assistance.

## Project-management requirement

Do not become trapped in educational detail. Maintain, at all times, a visible map of:
- current educational stage;
- last true milestone;
- external work nodes currently earned;
- desired next jobs;
- missing prerequisites for those jobs;
- next educational milestone;
- products that will benefit when it is reached.

This document is a persistent instruction for future Doré planning.
