## What Doré must notice before interpretation

- Hebrew is read right-to-left; storage/order must not accidentally reverse the textual sequence.
- Vocalization and consonantal text are textual data and must be preserved non-destructively.
- Prefixes and conjunctions may be morphologically segmented even when displayed as part of an orthographic word.
- `אֱלֹהִים` is a surface form; lexical and morphological analysis belong to analytical layers and require provenance.
- `אֵת` must not be forced into an English/Chinese lexical gloss merely because a translation expects every token to become a word.
- The verse may be translated and syntactically discussed later, but translation and syntactic interpretation are not allowed to overwrite the Hebrew witness.
