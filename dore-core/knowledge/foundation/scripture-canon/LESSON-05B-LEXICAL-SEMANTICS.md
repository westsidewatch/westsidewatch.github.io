# Doré Lesson 05B — Lexical Semantics

Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Doré has completed full Hebrew lexical-source ingestion. The next task is not merely to know dictionary headwords, but to study how a lemma is actually used across Scripture.

## Core principle

A lexeme is not a single gloss. Meaning is constrained by attested usage, morphology, syntax, collocation, discourse context, textual tradition, and genre.

## First semantic family

Genesis 15:6 provides the initial bridge:

- אמן ↔ πιστεύω
- חשב ↔ λογίζομαι
- צדקה ↔ δικαιοσύνη

These are research correspondences to investigate, not declarations of one-to-one semantic identity.

## Required evidence layers

For each lemma Doré must be able to retain and distinguish:

1. lexical-source entry;
2. every corpus occurrence and canonical reference;
3. surface forms and morphology;
4. immediate lexical collocations;
5. book / genre distribution;
6. Hebrew Bible usage;
7. Septuagint translation evidence when available;
8. New Testament Greek usage;
9. explicit intertextual links;
10. scholarly semantic claims with provenance.

## Guardrails

- Never reduce a lemma to its most familiar English or Chinese gloss.
- Frequency does not by itself establish meaning.
- Shared gloss does not establish Hebrew–Greek equivalence.
- A New Testament quotation may reuse an Old Testament text while developing its argument in a new discourse context.
- LXX evidence must be represented as its own textual witness, not silently substituted for the Hebrew text.
- Theology may use lexical evidence; theology must not be smuggled into lexical data as though it were morphology.

## First practicum

Build a corpus concordance for אמן (OSHB 539): collect every attested occurrence from the pinned Hebrew corpus, preserving reference, surface form, morphology, and provenance. Then summarize distributions without assigning a theological conclusion.

## Milestone

Lesson 05B first stage passes when Doré can answer: “How is אמן used in the Hebrew Bible?” from corpus evidence rather than from a single dictionary gloss, and can show the evidence behind the answer.
