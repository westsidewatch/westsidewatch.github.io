# Doré Multi-Version Witness Policy

Status: ACTIVE

Doré must not learn Chinese or English from a single Bible version. A single witness is only a baseline ingestion test, never completion of a language curriculum.

## Principle

Language knowledge and translation-version knowledge are separate layers.

For each modern language Doré should learn multiple textual witnesses representing different translation philosophies, historical periods, vocabulary traditions, and ecclesial usage. Each version remains an independent witness with its own provenance, edition, license, and canonical alignment.

## English witness set

Baseline/open witnesses to ingest where legally and technically available:
- WEBU / World English Bible Updated
- KJV / King James Version
- ASV / American Standard Version
- additional public-domain or openly licensed witnesses

Important contemporary copyrighted witnesses to support through licensed/authorized access when available:
- NIV
- ESV
- NRSV / NRSVue
- NASB
- NLT

Doré must know the existence, translation history, textual base, style, and characteristic translation choices of major versions even when full copyrighted text cannot be stored locally without permission.

## Chinese witness set

Baseline/open witnesses to ingest where legally and technically available:
- CUV Traditional / 和合本繁體
- CUV Simplified / 和合本簡體
- other clearly public-domain or openly licensed historical Chinese Bible witnesses

Important modern witnesses to support through licensed/authorized access when available:
- 新標點和合本 / Revised punctuation editions as rights permit
- 和合本修訂版 RCUV
- 中文新譯本 CNV
- 新漢語譯本 and other major scholarly/church-use translations as rights permit

Traditional and Simplified Chinese are not separate languages, but distinct orthographic witnesses may differ in punctuation, lexical modernization, names, and editorial choices; they must not be silently merged.

## Comparative learning targets

Doré should learn across witnesses at verse and phrase level:
- translation choices for the same source-language lemma or construction
- formal vs dynamic equivalence tendencies
- archaic vs modern vocabulary
- divine-name conventions
- proper-name conventions
- punctuation and segmentation differences
- textual-base differences where relevant
- denominational or historical usage context without treating popularity as truth

## Copyright rule

Open/public-domain witnesses may be ingested corpus-wide. Copyrighted modern versions require lawful licensed or authorized access before full-text local ingestion. Without such access Doré may retain bibliographic metadata, documented translation principles, and user-provided excerpts within applicable limits, but must not build an unauthorized local full-text corpus.

## Completion criterion

`English learned` and `Chinese learned` may never be declared on the basis of one witness. Completion requires a multi-witness coverage benchmark plus cross-version alignment and comparison tests.
