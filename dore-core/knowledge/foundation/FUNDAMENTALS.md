# DORÉ Foundation Education — 0.1

Status: **CURRICULUM BEFORE MINISTRY WORK**

Doré is not intended to enter Westside Watch as an ignorant assistant that gradually learns basic Scripture. Exceptional breadth of verifiable knowledge is the minimum entry qualification.

## Graduation domains

### A. Scripture Canon
- complete canonical structure and reference system;
- persons, places, peoples, events, genealogies and relationships;
- quotations, allusions, parallel passages and cross-canonical relationships;
- textual/version metadata;
- Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek lexical/morphological research capability;
- ability to distinguish explicit text from inference.

### B. Biblical Worlds
- Ancient Near East;
- Israel/Judah and surrounding peoples;
- Assyrian, Babylonian, Persian periods;
- Second Temple Judaism;
- Hellenistic and Roman worlds;
- historical geography, archaeology and material culture;
- chronology with explicit uncertainty where disputed.

### C. Interpretation and Theology
- hermeneutics and exegetical method;
- biblical theology;
- major systematic-theological categories;
- historical theology and church history;
- major Christian traditions and their differences;
- history of interpretation without flattening traditions into one voice.

### D. Research Method
- source identification and bibliography;
- primary vs secondary source distinction;
- source authority and evidence grading;
- textual/lexical comparison;
- competing-view representation;
- citation and provenance;
- contradiction detection;
- explicit uncertainty and ability to return `insufficient evidence`.

### E. Westside Foundation Corpus
Doré must know Westside Watch before Companion Mode begins. Existing brand work is part of its inherited pre-service education, including approved/current material from:
- ONE;
- Doré Visual research and decisions;
- Westside Watch main site;
- Journal/editorial work;
- Liming Library;
- Westside Stories where available;
- brand vision, design language and terminology;
- approved church/public ministry information;
- architecture memos and dated working records, with status/provenance preserved;
- relevant Git history where it clarifies supersession or decision development.

The corpus must distinguish production truth, approved specification, research evidence, working conversation, proposal, superseded material and unreviewed model output.

## Graduation philosophy

Importing documents is not graduation. Doré must demonstrate:

- very high knowledge coverage;
- reliable retrieval;
- cross-domain linking;
- research competence;
- evidence discipline;
- provenance;
- Westside familiarity;
- calibrated uncertainty.

The expected status transition is:

`DORÉ / Foundation -> benchmarked -> DORÉ / Researcher`

Only after Researcher graduation should Doré begin normal ministry/product assignments and Companion Learning.
