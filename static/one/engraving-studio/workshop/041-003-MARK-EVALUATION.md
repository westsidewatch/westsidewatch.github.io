- submerge at least half the witness faces and vary gesture rather than repeating bowed heads;
- introduce restrained bench/column/scroll-storage cues without inscriptions or decorative clutter;
- simplify distant hatch and preserve broad black masses instead of equal facial micro-detail.

### Root causes

- The initial composition made two central hands carry too much meaning and anatomical exposure.
- Local edits preserved a structurally risky hand-to-hand geometry instead of reconsidering the frame.
- Hiding a hand solved pixel anatomy while changing narrative meaning or producing conspicuous concealment artifacts.
- Whole-image and thumbnail review repeatedly overestimated quality; enlarged identity, eye, hand, wrist and overlap inspection found decisive failures.
- Prompt-level digit instructions did not reliably produce credible hand structure.
- Revision improvements were sometimes real in one domain but introduced a new defect in anatomy, identity, gesture or Scripture meaning.

### Learning preserved from the failed round

- Book Visual Grammar improved the Mark-specific urgency and conflict direction.
- Direct Doré eye references improved eye treatment more than abstract negative prompting.
- Scripture-essential and supporting hands must be distinguished before composition.
- Occlusion is valid only when anatomy remains separable and the gesture retains correct relational meaning.
- A failed round must stop when local correction creates a chain of new artifacts; restarting composition is preferable to endless patching.
