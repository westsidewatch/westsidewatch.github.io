## Scripture reading

- Narrative unit: a single prophetic oracle against Edom that moves from the pride of its cliff dwellings, through violence against brother Jacob and the Day of the LORD, to deliverance on Mount Zion and the LORD's kingdom.
- Theological center: height, security and national pride cannot resist the LORD's judgment; Edom's violence returns upon its own head, while holiness and deliverance belong to Zion and the kingdom belongs to the LORD.
- What happens before: Edom trusts its high rock habitation and says inwardly that none can bring it down.
- What happens after: Edom is stripped, its allies fail, its violence is judged measure for measure, survivors possess Mount Zion, and deliverers ascend to govern Esau's mountain.
- Key textual details: clefts of the rock; lofty dwelling; nest among the stars; violence against Jacob; standing aloof at Jerusalem's calamity; the Day of the LORD; Mount Zion as holy refuge; the house of Esau as stubble; deliverers ascending Mount Zion; the kingdom belonging to the LORD.
- Biblical cross-references: Jeremiah 49:7–22 for Edom's high dwelling and downfall; Amos 1:11–12 for violence against a brother; Isaiah 13 for the visual grammar of a proud city brought low; Psalm 137:7 for Edom at Jerusalem's fall.

## Scene choice

- Exact instant to depict: the final stillness before reversal—two very small Edomite watchmen stand at a rock-cut threshold high in the cleft, confident above the abyss, while the great descending shadow and the broken upward path reveal that their height has already become a trap; far below and beyond, a restrained opening of light marks Mount Zion and the line of approaching deliverers.
- Why this instant: it joins the book's opening pride to its closing reversal without illustrating unrelated warfare, literalizing God, or turning judgment into an explosion.
- Viewer position: below the Edomite height, looking upward through the ravine; the viewer shares the low position of those whom Edom despised.
- Viewing distance: monumental landscape with humans kept small and structurally clear.
- First visual read: an almost inaccessible rock dwelling occupying the high dark mass.
- Second visual read: the human figures are tiny and falsely secure; the cliff geometry drives the eye downward rather than upward.
- Late discoveries: far-off Mount Zion, the thin ascending procession, and abandoned objects near the threshold that quietly imply Edom's participation in plunder.
- Deliberately omitted textual details: no literal divine figure, no stars-as-special-effects, no eagle emblem, no modern army, no burning Jerusalem panorama, no literal personification of nations, no written prophecy.

## Subject grammar

- Principal figure grammar: no heroic protagonist; two small, weight-bearing watchmen in ancient Levantine garments, one upright and one leaning toward the abyss, silhouettes readable before faces.
- Hands / face / drapery: hands rest naturally on a stone threshold or plain staff; faces remain subordinate; cloth follows gravity and ravine wind.
- Animal / creature grammar: none.
- Secondary figure roles: a very small, orderly ascending group on the distant Zion path signifies deliverers without becoming an army.
- Architecture / landscape: Edom is not a European castle. The dwelling is cut into stratified sandstone and built from restrained ancient Near Eastern masonry; terrain, threshold, ravine and path carry the interpretation.

## Engraving strategy

- Hatch systems: curved contour hatch follows sandstone bedding; vertical/diagonal cuts describe clefts; sweeping sky lines reinforce descending pressure.
- Cross-hatch density: concentrated in the ravine and undercut architecture, not distributed uniformly.
- Contour behavior: forms dissolve into shared shadow; no digital outline around every object.
- Material differentiation: stone uses broken layered lines; cloth uses gravity-led folds; sky uses long atmospheric systems; human skin receives minimal marks.
- Distant simplification: Zion and deliverers use sparse marks and silhouette, never equal micro-detail.
