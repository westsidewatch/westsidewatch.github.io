# Engraving Studio Learning Log

Status: **APPEND-ORIENTED / LIVING MEMORY**

Record discoveries from corpus study and plate generation here before promoting mature principles into specialist notebooks or Visual Grammar.

## 2026-08 — Foundation lessons

### L001 — Engraving texture is not Doré

Finding: black-and-white output with cross-hatching can still be visually unrelated to Doré.

Promote: evaluate composition, anatomy, gesture, value hierarchy, spatial staging and Scripture scene intelligence before surface texture.

### L002 — Spectacle is not the default

Finding: reducing Doré to monumental crowds and divine light produces repetitive, false results. Many biblical plates begin from intimate narrative detail.

Promote: select viewing distance from Scripture and Doré precedent before generation.

### L003 — Background figures cannot be population filler

Finding: randomly distributed people create AI crowd texture rather than narrative composition.

Promote: every crowd cluster requires role, gesture direction, depth layer and relationship to the principal action.

### L004 — Creatures require Doré visual ancestry

Finding: generic fantasy vocabulary makes biblical beasts culturally and stylistically incoherent.

Promote: Revelation sea/earth beasts must begin from Doré's own dragon and related animal/monster grammar, then be reconciled with the biblical description.

### L005 — Scene choice precedes object list

Finding: prompts built by listing all textual objects tend to become illustrated inventories.

Promote: decide exact narrative instant, viewer position and first/second visual read before enumerating visible elements.

### L006 — Generated plates are revisable training events

Finding: unlike canonical Doré originals, ONE Studio plates need versioning because later Studio intelligence may expose earlier weaknesses.

Promote: retain revision history and feed review findings back into Living Intelligence.

### L007 — Scripture-image relation is a first-class research object

Finding: studying only how Doré draws misses why his plate works as biblical illustration.

Promote: each canonical plate study records passage context, selected instant, omissions, theological hierarchy and relationship between text and pictorial decision.

### L008 — Prophetic reversal can be staged before catastrophe

Finding: Obadiah 1 became more Scripture-specific when judgment was expressed through low viewer position, false height, descending cliff geometry and a distant counter-path toward Zion rather than through an exploding city.

Evidence: `031-001` revision cycle; comparison with *Isaiah's Vision of the Destruction of Babylon*, *Nehemiah Views the Ruins of Jerusalem's Walls* and *The Walls of Jericho Falling Down*.

Retain: for prophetic oracles, test whether spatial hierarchy and the instant before reversal can carry judgment more truthfully than visible destruction.

### L009 — Plural path instructions trigger generative repetition

Finding: requesting a “thin ascending line of deliverers” produced mechanically spaced miniature figures, while requesting a “broken path” produced excessive unsupported masonry. Both were recognizable AI population/detail habits.

Correction: state a small exact figure count with irregular spacing and atmospheric loss; require fewer, larger, weight-bearing planes for carved paths and stairs.

Retain: crowd and architecture prompts must specify narrative count, irregularity, support and omission—not merely mood or direction.

### L010 — Preserve a passed component without approving the plate

Finding: Philemon r7 was rejected as a complete image but its engraved stroke language was explicitly approved by human review.

Evidence: `work/candidates/057-001-philemon-r7.png`, SHA-256 `1bb5391476ed7896b166cdc7d55e666d7f7bfdee119c0b42ae3eab29c5407342`.

Approved: directional/material-specific hatch, breathable midtones, non-looping wall texture and natural chain gravity.

Rejected: oversized Paul, compressed depth, rigid room grid, missing lived detail and generic AI exterior.

Retain: evaluation memory must be component-specific. “Plate rejected” must not erase a verified stroke advance; “stroke passed” must never imply that composition or publication passed.

### L011 — Monumentality must remain restrained

Finding: a Doré-like large scene derives grandeur from scale contrast, depth, mass, gesture, directional movement and tonal order. Generated images often substitute a sun hidden in clouds, a bright cloud aperture, radiating beams or a supernatural spotlight.

Evidence: the approved Jude 9 r2 achieved dramatic force through the vast ravine, low shrouded body, separated adversaries and restrained angelic gesture. Human review identified this restraint as a major Doré characteristic. The plate's brighter cloud center is tolerated as a retained limitation, not promoted as a pattern.

Retain: prompts for monumental or sacred scenes must explicitly forbid automatic solar openings, light shafts and radiant cloud portals unless the text and Doré ancestry positively require them. Build awe structurally before adding any luminous event.

### L012 — A local sky correction can corrupt passed engraving language

Finding: the Jude 12–13 sky-only experiment corrected an abrupt star band and repeated parallel cloud ribbons, but introduced more swirling, texture-led marks. Semantic improvement in one region does not guarantee stroke improvement.

Retain: preserve the original candidate beside every experiment; never overwrite it. If an edited region changes the surrounding stroke grammar, reject the edit. A passed final cover should not be reopened merely to remove a tolerable flaw.
