# Visual Ancestry

Status: **LIVING NOTEBOOK**

New Westside Watch plates must grow from actual Doré precedents rather than from a generic style label.

## Rule

For every subject Doré did not directly illustrate, identify 3–8 canonical plates that solve related visual problems. Record the inheritance separately:

- scene choice;
- figure anatomy / gesture;
- animal / creature anatomy;
- composition;
- viewing distance;
- crowd behavior;
- landscape / architecture;
- light / value;
- engraving line system.

No single reference needs to provide all traits. The objective is coherent inheritance, not collage.

## Ancestry tree model

`Scripture subject`
→ `biblical textual ancestors`
→ `Doré plates of those ancestors or related visual problems`
→ `specific inherited grammar`
→ `new plate decision`

## Initial Revelation examples

### Sea beast / earth beast

Primary visual ancestor: Doré's Revelation dragon plate.

Then compare:
- Doré animal anatomy and predatory mass;
- Danielic monster imagery where represented or visually analogous;
- crowd reaction to supernatural terror;
- sea / wilderness staging;
- Doré horns, claws, serpentine mass and shadow behavior.

Reject starting from Chinese-dragon vocabulary, videogame bosses, modern creature concept art or generic medieval heraldic beasts.

### White-horse rider

Begin with:
- Doré equestrian anatomy;
- rider/horse single-gesture compositions;
- cavalry and battle compression;
- Revelation Pale Horse;
- cloud/light systems used for divine or catastrophic arrival.

### Heavenly throne

Begin with:
- celestial hierarchies;
- enthroned figures;
- worship crowds;
- architectural/negative-space hierarchy;
- concentrated supernatural light.

## Anti-collage rule

Do not copy a dragon head from one plate, a horse from another and clouds from a third as disconnected pieces. Ancestry must be reconciled into one scene logic, one value plan and one Doré-like pictorial decision.
