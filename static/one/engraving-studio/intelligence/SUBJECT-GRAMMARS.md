### Eyes / gaze — first promoted specialist rule

Evidence: Doré's *Christ in the Synagogue*, *Jesus Healing the Sick* and *Jesus Healing the Lunatic*, cross-checked against the Mark 3 r4/r5 human-review cycle.

- The eye belongs to the brow, lid, socket, nose and head turn; it is not an independent symbol pasted onto the face.
- At plate scale, the pupil/iris is normally a short dark accent absorbed under the upper lid rather than a round black mark.
- Lower lids and sclera remain incomplete and understated.
- Three-quarter faces require unequal visible eye widths but one shared gaze direction.
- Whole-image approval is forbidden until principal eyes are inspected enlarged.
- A failed enlarged eye check must be recorded as a generative artifact and returned to revision.
