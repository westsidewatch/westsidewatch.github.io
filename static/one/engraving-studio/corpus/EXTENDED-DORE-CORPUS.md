# Extended Doré Corpus

Status: **LIVING RESEARCH CORPUS / NON-CANONICAL ASSET SOURCE**

The Westside Watch Engraving Studio may study Gustave Doré's works beyond the canonical Bible illustrations in order to understand his visual intelligence more completely.

This corpus is **research material**, not an automatic ONE illustration asset library. Non-Bible Doré works must not be inserted into the canonical 241 Bible Original Library or assigned to Scripture chapters merely because they are stylistically useful.

## Why expand beyond the Bible

The Bible corpus alone does not expose the full range of Doré's solutions for every visual problem. His wider illustrated work can provide stronger evidence for:

- horses, cavalry and movement;
- monsters, demons and hybrid creatures;
- hell, abyss, smoke, fire and supernatural terror;
- angels, ascent, celestial scale and paradise;
- forests, mountains, seas, storms and extreme landscape;
- medieval and ancient architecture;
- crowds, armies, processions and urban masses;
- intimate human encounters;
- grotesque faces and bodily expression;
- death, grief, fear, violence and judgment;
- comic, satirical and ordinary-life observation;
- how his composition changes across narrative genres;
- how engravers translate Doré's drawings into different line systems.

## Priority research bodies

Study may include, among other verified Doré bodies of work:

- Dante, *Divine Comedy* — Inferno, Purgatorio, Paradiso;
- Milton, *Paradise Lost*;
- Cervantes, *Don Quixote*;
- Tennyson, *Idylls of the King*;
- Ariosto, *Orlando Furioso*;
- La Fontaine, *Fables*;
- Perrault / fairy-tale illustrations;
- *London: A Pilgrimage*;
- *The Rime of the Ancient Mariner*;
- Crusades and historical illustration cycles;
- landscapes, paintings, drawings and other authenticated Doré works where they clarify his visual grammar.

This list is not closed. New bodies may be added after authorship/source verification.

## Research hierarchy

For Scripture continuation work:

1. **Bible Doré plate of the same passage** — canonical and controlling where it exists.
2. **Bible Doré plate solving the same biblical/visual problem** — primary visual ancestry.
3. **Non-Bible Doré work solving the same artistic problem** — extended visual ancestry.
4. Other nineteenth-century material — contextual research only, never treated as Doré evidence.

Thus non-Bible Doré expands the Studio's vocabulary but does not overrule a relevant Bible precedent.

## Cross-corpus study record

For each non-Bible Doré work used as research, record:

- work/cycle;
- title or scene;
- date/edition where known;
- verified Doré authorship/source;
- narrative context in the source text;
- visual problem it solves;
- composition type;
- figure/animal/creature observations;
- light/value observations;
- engraving/drawing observations;
- what may transfer to Scripture illustration;
- what is genre-specific and must NOT transfer;
- related Bible plates for comparison.

## Genre contamination rule

Studying a work does not authorize importing its entire genre vocabulary into Scripture.

Examples:

- Dante's demons may teach anatomy, mass, terror and darkness, but Revelation beasts must still arise from Revelation/Daniel Scripture and Doré's biblical dragon grammar.
- *Paradise Lost* may teach celestial scale and angelic conflict, but Miltonic details not present in Scripture must not silently become biblical facts.
- *Don Quixote* may teach horses, gesture, dust, travel and small-group staging without importing Spanish costume into ancient Israel.
- *London* may teach crowd density, fog, poverty, street depth and observational detail without importing Victorian architecture into biblical cities.

The Studio learns **artistic solutions**, not uncontrolled iconography.

## Long-term purpose

The Extended Doré Corpus lets the Living Studio learn Doré as an artist rather than only as the illustrator of one publication. Combined with close Scripture study, this should make Westside Watch plates less dependent on superficial Bible-engraving imitation and more capable of making new pictorial decisions with a coherent Doré-derived visual intelligence.
