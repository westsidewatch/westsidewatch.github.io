# ONE — Doré Canonical Cover Frame

Status: **LOCKED / CANONICAL FRAME AMENDMENT**

Scope: all ONE chapter covers, historical engravings and Westside Watch Engraving Studio · Doré continuations.

This amendment refines the frame language in `ONE-VISUAL-STANDARD.md`. Where the earlier standard says “floral/baroque corner ornaments,” this document defines the approved interpretation. It is a project-wide master rule, not a Revelation-only patch.

## 1. Doré review

The frame must behave like engraved bookbinding around a nineteenth-century biblical plate. It gives the image gravity and a printed-book threshold, but must never compete with the narrative engraving.

A restrained classical / nineteenth-century book-ornament vocabulary is compatible with Doré-style covers only when treated as **flat gilt linework**, not carved decoration.

> **Engraved book ornament, not modern iconography; binding structure, not a picture frame.**

## 2. Canonical frame construction

- 5:8 portrait book-cover proportion.
- Westside Watch / ONE brand-gold double rule.
- Very fine outer and inner lines.
- Flat aged hot-stamp effect rather than shine or relief.
- Tiny restrained foliate/acanthus corner turns.
- No metallic gradient, bevel, glow, 3D foil simulation, plaster relief or carved moulding.
- Ornament line weight remains subordinate to Doré's etched linework.

## 3. Four-corner motif

The four-corner gold-star treatment is retired.

Replace it with a minimal inward-turning foliate/acanthus gesture:

- one or two leaves at most;
- narrow footprint close to the double rule;
- connected visually to the binding line;
- no rosette, heraldic badge, jewel, starburst or icon-like center;
- no Rococo shell or dense scroll mass;
- slight foil wear and discontinuity are permitted.

The corner should almost disappear at normal reading distance.

## 4. Morning Star separation

The Morning Star remains an independent Westside Watch / ONE theological symbol. It is not a corner ornament and must never be repeated four times around the frame.

## 5. Relationship to Doré imagery

Dense Doré plates require the quietest possible corners. Studio · Doré continuations use exactly the same frame grammar so the binding does not reveal whether the central image is historical or newly produced.

## 6. Prohibited drift

Do not substitute modern UI stars, Art Deco geometry, Celtic knots, Gothic fantasy emblems, Victorian clip-art bouquets, heavy Baroque/Rococo ornament, metallic gradients, embossed effects or book-specific corner inventions.

## 7. Implementation gate

One shared reusable frame asset/component is used across ONE. No book-specific CSS ornament patches.

Representative QA must include a historical Doré plate, Revelation 2 Studio · Doré FINAL, desktop 5:8 and mobile 5:8.
