## Design principle

**One artwork identity, two inscription states, selective appearances.** The seal is composed from **mixed Chinese/English letterpress typography**: a pair of opposing yin/yang impressions (negative/positive), both derived from the **same optical register**. Neither decorative frames nor compulsory tiny legends. Complex mixed composition is allowed in the artwork; everyday Folio removes mixing and retains typography, dimensions, rhythm, inscription polarity and citation grammar. Text remains verified native text in the actual web implementation.

The Seal is not a replacement for every thematic verse or a required endcap to each of the seven realms. One primary, two emphasized artwork placements, other uses conditional. Do not duplicate a full two-stamp animation within a single continuous scroll journey.

## Experience density rules

1. **Homepage:** the full yin/yang animated two-stamp reveal is reserved as the signature encounter. If the sevenfold home and P2 flow are effectively one visitor journey, don't repeat the entire assembly animation again in P2.
2. **Magazine and Emmaus:** each can use a static/reduced single-state or brief inherited transition, with a different purpose: magazine = editorial cover; Emmaus = reading threshold. Not two additional full-length shows.
3. **Olive, Cinema:** refrain from full artwork by default; Folio should carry the DNA. This protects poster / movie identities from visual crowding.
4. **Church, Library, Café:** only programmatic/event/issue-triggered artwork when content justifies it, with one at a time per view.
5. **No decorative fillers:** empty space does not justify another impression; no repeated `THE WORD`, artificial frames, cross ornaments, seven random ticks, foil noise, or gold-on-black flooding.

## Two seal states (identity, not mandatory pair everywhere)

- **Yang / 陽文:** type carries positive mark; its shape is determined by merged type mass, not an enclosing border.
- **Yin / 陰文:** reversal of paper/ink and text/space from the SAME grid and proportions. Do not design it as a second unrelated poster.
- Cross-language directions may reverse between impressions when artistically justified; exact language layout must be verified as genuine related master geometry.
- **Both modes share:** Chinese/Latin type pairing, optical register, dominant/subordinate size ratios, citation treatment, negative-space rhythm, stamping polarity and compositional reduction principles.
- The artistic mixed reading experiments may be difficult to read at a glance. That's permitted for the artwork but **not** for real Bible reading. Canonical scripture can remain selectable via the documented text alternative and readable Folio.

## First production sequence / dependencies

**Phase A – placement audit:** identify actual home scripture node, Magazine cover node, Emmaus threshold DOM and current scroll owners; measure desktop/tablet/390/320 geometry. If no stable slot exists, document the conflict rather than inserting a new overlay.

**Phase B – pure artwork:** make the *static* paired yin/yang inscription on the SAME modular type grid. Use one verified verse first (Romans 10:17 recommended because already known in Olive; alternative Isaiah 55:9 needs canonical text verification). No ornamental rims, badges, paper effects or extraneous labels. A monochrome test must be compelling.

**Phase C – kinetic proof:** one reversible kinetic script, forward/reverse hand-controlled in an isolated page; include hold and reduced-motion static. Show type mixing and impression, not arbitrary animated text clouds.

**Phase D – DNA extraction:** derive Folio variations for masthead, pull quote, speaker-side, card and scroll. Freeze typographic ratios and citation syntax from the accepted artwork, not V1/V2.

**Phase E – limited placement:** first homepage proof only; second magazine cover proof; third Emmaus threshold proof; each behind Draft/opt-in preview and independently reviewed. Keep P2 released 01–03 and P2 04 Draft unchanged unless explicitly approved.

**Phase F – conditional programming:** Church, Library and Café artworks only when real editorial content warrants them; don't implement empty slots.

## Acceptance and governance

- The artistic Seal must pass a static *black-and-white* arrangement review before motion, colors or materials.
- Visual evidence at desktop/768/390/320; no overflow, no accidental single-character hangs, no English type deformation; preserve source scriptures and translations.
- Exact forward/reverse scroll-state equivalence, no racing independent clocks, no unnecessary reintroduction of the whole pair.
- The final system must feel like one coherent typographic identity in Seal and Folio without repeating decorative widgets.
- Current first-layer implementation is under construction; repo architecture and Draft previews establish potential slots but do not prove that every realm's final DOM is implemented. This planning document authorizes **no live rollout**.
