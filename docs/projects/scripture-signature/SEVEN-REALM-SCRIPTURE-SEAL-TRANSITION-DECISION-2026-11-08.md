# Decision — Every realm begins with its Scripture Seal / 七版塊經文印記引章

**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Status:** latest user-approved design direction; planning only, not a deployed implementation  
**Scope:** Main site/P2 first-layer seven realms, without changes to site architecture.

## 1. Supersession of sparse-placement plan

This decision **supersedes the earlier selective-appearance recommendation** in `SEAL-PLACEMENT-PLAN-FIRST-LAYER-2026-11-08.md` where it treated Olive and Cinema as Folio-only and limited signature artwork to a few major locations. The user has approved a **Scripture Seal at the chapter entrance of every one of the seven realms**.

The trigger/transition is the artwork: the primary scripture mark enters, reaches a stable impression/reading moment, and yields the composition to the realm's existing scene. One shared design DNA for all seven, but not seven mechanically identical performances or seven unrelated logos.

The homepage may introduce the full positive/negative master pair. Each realm then uses an economical, meaningful single signature impression or short paired variation appropriate to its scene. Full twin-seal performance is not compulsory per realm.

## 2. Theological concept and editorial care

Motif connects **Ephesians 1:13** (Holy Spirit as seal), **2 Corinthians 3:3** (written with the Spirit on human hearts), and **Jeremiah 31:33** (God's law written on hearts). Maintain theological distinction: the Holy Spirit is the seal; the inscription motif expresses Scripture's internalization. Do not write slogans falsely equating the Holy Spirit with ink or type.

Each realm has its *own verified theme verse* in native Traditional Chinese + KJV, exactly cited. Do not invent specific verses from section labels. Preserve previously approved theme selections (e.g. Olive Romans 10:17, Emmaus Luke 24:27) until a separate verified editorial decision.

## 4. Temporal grammar

**One scroll-progress authority:** `seal arrives → type registers/interweaves → impression settles/holds → chapter art takes over`. Every step must be attached to existing directional progress, not a second time-based autonomous animation. Reverse scrolling restores exact prior states; stopping stops the transition. A shorter act or completed state may be reused where fitting.

**Meaningful differentiation:** vary entering direction or hand-off geometry by each existing scene only when the scene's established motion supports it; do not replay seven full independent copy-pasted seal animations. No sudden opacity crossfade, unexplained overlays, or blind transition blocks. Each print has a stationary complete frame; reader can inspect/reduce motion.

**Typography first:** a typographic positive/negative seal derived from one shared optical grid and mixed Chinese-vertical/English-horizontal composition, without superfluous frame, cross, seven ticks, slogan `THE WORD`, fake antique textures, or new decoration. The first accepted physical impression supplies DNA to all later 7 and Scripture Folio derivatives.

## 5. Real engineering prerequisites

2. Audit actual chapter entrances, current scroll ranges, geometry and DOM at 1648/768/390/320px; the current architecture alone does not certify that all seven are implemented.
3. Assign one correct, bilingual source-verified theme verse to each realm in a separate scripture registry; avoid overwriting approved verse subjects.
4. Prototype one transition in a standalone sandbox with deterministic forward/reverse scroll and a stable hold.
5. Only then stage each chapter entrance in a Draft copy, preserving all prior approved animation and card/poster/cover assets. Compare old/new captures on each breakpoint.
6. Main remains untouched without explicit approval; CI passing is not artistic acceptance.

## 6. Scripture Folio stays separate

The new rule mandates **Seal at the realm entrance**, not the seal on every non-body quotation. Within scenes, readable editorial theme verses, pull quotes, speaker-side citations, cards and subheads use **Folio derived from the finished Seal DNA**, without experimental mixing or forced square frames.
