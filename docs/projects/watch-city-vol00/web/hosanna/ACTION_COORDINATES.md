## Selection rule

A later film is not valuable merely because it is newer, and an early film is not valuable merely because it is old.

A shot earns its place by contributing at least one of:

1. a stronger continuation of physical motion;
2. a decisive visual change of era;
3. a new crowd scale or spatial reading;
4. a stronger threshold / city relation;
5. an audible Hosanna layer;
6. a historically important representation;
7. a publishable rights state.

The final montage should therefore be **dense in time but economical in shots**.

## Hard evidence boundary

This matrix is an editorial cutting hypothesis built from currently verified scene descriptions, transcripts and official clip metadata. A ● does not itself authorize publication. Before a source contributes actual frames, its action coordinate must be checked against the moving image and its rights state must independently pass publication admission.
