# Dawn Shared Resource Layer — Editorial Projection Canon

Status: CANONICAL CONCEPT UPDATE
Date: 2026-09-25
Scope: Dawn shared canonical resource substrate and all site consumers

## Why this update exists

This document supersedes narrower memories or earlier descriptions that treated Curated Collections, Cinema, Spectrum, and Three Morning Stars mainly as overlapping recommendation or topic products.

They are four reusable editorial projections over the same shared canonical resource layer. They do **not** own separate copies of Works and they do **not** create separate identity systems. Their distinction is primarily the dominant medium and editorial mode used to organize a subject.

## Shared substrate first

All admitted resources remain in the shared Dawn canonical substrate / Resource Fabric. A resource is admitted according to the existing authority, rights, relevance, source-policy, and canonical-identity rules. Projection membership is downstream editorial organization, never an admission gate.

The same canonical Work or resource may participate in more than one projection when useful. Products such as Dawn Library, Cinema, ONE, Journal, Search, Multiwrite / 成書, Jerusalem 3000, or future consumers may freely call these projections without duplicating the underlying resource.

## Four editorial projections

### 1. Curated Collections / 策展集 — image-led synthesis

A Curated Collection is a **primarily image-led synthesis of resources around a subject**.

Its dominant evidence and navigation may include photographs, maps, manuscripts, paintings, engravings, diagrams, architecture, archaeology, objects, visual timelines, scanned pages, and other image-rich material. Text and video may be included when they deepen the route, but the visual corpus is the leading editorial grammar.

A Curated Collection is not merely a folder of images. It must organize visual evidence into a coherent subject world, route, comparison, chronology, atlas, or argument. Jerusalem 3000 / Jerusalem Map Atlas is the first production reference case.

### 2. Cinema / 天堂電影院 — video-led synthesis

Cinema is a **primarily video-led synthesis of resources around a subject**.

Its dominant material may include films, documentaries, sermons, lectures, interviews, archival footage, performances, recorded teaching, and other moving-image sources. Text, images, maps, timelines, Scripture coordinates, and research Works may support the viewing route, but video is the leading medium.

Cinema is a projection and display surface over the shared resource layer, not a separate video database.

### 3. Spectrum / 光譜 — text-knowledge-led thematic synthesis

Spectrum is a **primarily text-knowledge-led synthesis that reveals a subject direction, continuum, contrast, school, historical development, conceptual relationship, or knowledge topology**.

Its dominant material is textual knowledge: books, articles, primary texts, reference works, scholarship, notes, textual evidence, and relationships among them. Images and video may be called where useful, but the organizing intelligence is the knowledge structure and editorial question.

Spectrum should emerge from corpus evidence rather than merely renaming existing taxonomy categories.

### 4. Three Morning Stars / 三晨星 — book-led durable selection

Three Morning Stars is **primarily book-oriented**. It identifies books that deserve durable editorial attention and explains why they matter.

It is not a star-rating system and not a generic recommendation list. Its core objects are books / book-like Works, selected through authority, influence, usefulness, distinctiveness, evidence quality, relationship density, and long-term editorial value. Supporting images, video, essays, or tools may be linked, but the book remains the central object.

## Medium dominance is not exclusivity

The four projections are deliberately porous:

- Curated Collection: image-led, may call text + video.
- Cinema: video-led, may call images + text.
- Spectrum: text-knowledge-led, may call images + video.
- Three Morning Stars: book-led, may call supporting media.

A single subject can therefore produce several complementary projections without duplicating resources. Example: Jerusalem may have an image-led Curated Collection, a video-led Cinema route, a text-led Spectrum, and selected Jerusalem books in Three Morning Stars, all pointing to the same canonical resource identities.

## Consumer freedom

These projections are reusable editorial capabilities, not locked pages. Any site product may call one projection or combine several according to its own experience. A future page may, for example, embed a Curated Collection visual sequence beside a Spectrum reading path and a Cinema viewing path while preserving one canonical resource identity underneath.

## Scan implication

The Full-Library Editorial Scan must therefore measure and discover resources by medium as well as topic, language, period, author, authority, and relationships. Gap acquisition must include at minimum:

- image / visual-resource gaps for Curated Collections;
- video-resource gaps for Cinema;
- textual-knowledge gaps for Spectrum;
- book / edition gaps for Three Morning Stars;
- Chinese entry and Chinese depth gaps across all four projections.

Chinese acquisition remains prioritized without lowering rights, authority, relevance, or source-policy gates. Wikisource remains forbidden.
