# Chinese Gap Acquisition — Batch 02 Large Source Expansion

Status: ACTIVE DISCOVERY / RECONCILIATION QUEUE
Date: 2026-09-25
Parent: `BATCH-01-DISCOVERY.md`

## Purpose

Continue Chinese acquisition at collection scale rather than title-by-title browsing. This batch broadens the source pool across books, scholarship, Scripture, manuscripts, images and audiovisual material while preserving the existing Dawn admission chain.

Hard rules remain unchanged: Wikisource forbidden; political/ethnic/war controversy excluded; discovery authority != access permission != rehost permission; Chinese priority never lowers rights/relevance/authority gates.

## Source families added in Batch 02

### A. Chinese theological bibliography / books

1. Logos Evangelical Seminary / 正道神學院 Library
- ~28,000 Chinese physical books.
- 500+ Chinese/Western periodical backfiles.
- audiovisual media, Sunday-school material, Bible editions, hymnals, newsletters and commemorative publications.
- discovery role: large Chinese theological bibliography authority.
- projection targets: Three Morning Stars + Spectrum; AV candidates -> Cinema.
- rights posture: bibliography/discovery first; no assumption of full-text or rehost rights.

2. China Evangelical Seminary / 中華福音神學研究學院
- Chinese e-book discovery through HyRead / iRead and Chinese scholarly resources through Airiti; theological databases and theses also exposed through library resource guides.
- explicit recent Chinese title lists provide high-quality contemporary acquisition candidates.
- projection targets: Three Morning Stars + Spectrum.
- rights posture: provider pointer / institutional access only unless independent public rights are verified.

3. GETS Theological Seminary / 創欣神學院
- 34,000+ Chinese/English Christian and theological volumes.
- 500+ theological theses.
- 6,000+ Chinese literature/history/philosophy special-collection titles supporting Chinese Christianity contextual research.
- projection targets: Spectrum + Three Morning Stars.
- rights posture: bibliographic authority / discovery.

### B. Chinese Scripture / primary text

4. eBible / Biblica Chinese Mandarin Open Resource
- Chinese Mandarin Biblica Bible open-resource edition exposed in browser, HTML, EPUB, PDF, text and other formats.
- projection targets: Spectrum + Three Morning Stars + Scripture semantic index + ONE consumer.
- admission action: verify exact license/redistribution terms before any rehost capability; stable provider pointer may be admitted separately.

5. Academia Sinica Digital Archives — 基督要理 / Doctrina Christiana en letra y lengua china
- ca. 1605 Chinese Christian catechetical text; 33-leaf woodblock work; original held by Vatican Library; digital record managed by Academia Sinica Institute of Linguistics.
- projection targets: Curated Collection + Spectrum + Three Morning Stars.
- semantic coordinates: early Chinese Christianity, translation, catechism, Manila Chinese Christianity, early printed Christian text.
- rights posture: provider pointer and metadata; contact/permission required for reuse beyond allowed access.

6. Archivum Romanum Societatis Iesu / ARSI Chinese Materials
- digital access to parts of the Japonica-Sinica Chinese-book corpus.
- catalogues and Chinese Christian Texts database cover important Ming-Qing Christian texts; around 100 texts also represented in a published facsimile corpus.
- includes digitized early works such as Matteo Ricci-related Chinese materials.
- projection targets: Curated Collection + Spectrum + Three Morning Stars.
- rights posture: item/provider-specific; do not infer rehost rights from digital availability.

### C. Image-led Chinese Christianity corpus

7. Whitworth University — SAM China Mission Photograph Collection
- 26 historical photo albums plus 25 boxes of loose photographs/religious images, with additional albums and hundreds of loose photographs associated with Raymond de Jaegher.
- primary date range includes mission activity in China 1926–1952.
- projection target: Curated Collections first.
- rights posture: SAM retains proprietary rights; pointer/metadata only unless permission exists.

8. Whitworth — China Mission History Image Collection / Archive
- historical photographs and prints from multiple missionary/religious archives and collections.
- broader repository also holds Chinese/Western books and original/reproduction research materials concerning Christianity in China.
- projection targets: Curated Collections + Spectrum.
- rights posture: permission varies by holding repository; image use must remain rights-aware.

9. Historical Photographs of China
- large item-level historical-photo discovery surface with identifiers, dates, locations, tags, material/media fields and downloadable digital representations where offered.
- projection target: Curated Collections; semantic joins through place/date/person/topic.
- acquisition rule: filter out excluded political/ethnic/war-controversy material before admission even when photographs are otherwise historically valuable.

### D. Additional rights-friendly visual discovery

10. Library of Congress item-level Chinese-Christianity visual records

## Batch-scale rule

Do not stop at individual-title discoveries. A provider with a coherent, relevant collection should enter as a source family and be processed through provider-level harvesting/reconciliation where technically and legally possible. Individual Works are admitted only after canonical reconciliation and policy gates.

## Measurement

For each source family report:
- provider-scale candidate count when known;
- machine-enumerable count;
- reconciled existing Works;
- newly admitted Works;
- pointer-only resources;
- deferred/rejected resources with reasons;
- medium distribution: book / text / image / video;
- Chinese semantic coordinates strengthened.

Canonical Work growth is reported only after admission; discovery-scale counts must never be presented as collection growth.
