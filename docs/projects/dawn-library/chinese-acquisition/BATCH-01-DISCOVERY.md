# Chinese Gap Acquisition — Batch 01

Status: ACTIVE DISCOVERY
Date: 2026-09-25
Goal: materially expand Chinese resources in the shared Dawn canonical substrate without creating a parallel pipeline.

## Admission boundaries

- Existing Dawn discovery → reconciliation → rights → relevance → authority → canonical admission remains the only growth path.
- Chinese discovery and promotion run before English discovery, without lowering any gate.
- Wikisource is permanently forbidden at discovery and admission.
- Political / ethnic / war controversy material remains excluded under existing Dawn policy.
- Discovery authority, access permission and rehost permission are separate claims.
- A source being digitized or publicly viewable does not imply rehost rights.

## Batch 01 strategy

Do not fill only book counts. Discover Chinese resources across the dual index:

### Material / experience axis
- image-led: photographs, manuscripts, maps, scanned pages, architecture, archaeology and visual archives → Curated Collections
- video-led: sermons, lectures, documentaries, interviews and archival moving image → Cinema
- text-knowledge-led: primary texts, periodicals, reference works, research and textual evidence → Spectrum
- book-led: monographs, translations, editions and durable reading works → Three Morning Stars

### Semantic axis
Priority coordinates: Bible, exegesis, theology, biblical world, prayer/spiritual formation, church history, preaching/ministry, Chinese Christianity, missions, Scripture translation, people, places and periods.

## High-value discovery source — Yale Documentation of Chinese Christianity

Provider: Yale University Library / Divinity Library, Documentation of Chinese Christianity.
Role: discovery authority + bibliographic/archival evidence; access/rehost evaluated item by item.

Verified collection-scale candidates from provider documentation:

1. Chinese Christian Literature Council / 廣學會 + 基督教輔僑出版社
   - 1,300+ Christian tracts and books.
   - 692 titles from Christian Literature Society for China (1906–1962).
   - 616 titles from Council on Christian Literature for Overseas Chinese (1947–1993).
   - Projection value: Spectrum + Three Morning Stars; scanned covers/pages may support Curated Collections when rights permit.

2. Christian Weekly / 基督教週報
   - Digitized no. 1 (1964) through no. 2572 (2013).
   - Projection value: Spectrum; church history / theology / Christian life / education semantic routes.

3. China Free Methodist Church / 台灣循理會 materials
   - 35 monographs plus serial runs including 循理週報, 循理會刊, 循理報.
   - Projection value: Spectrum + church-history semantic index.

4. Chinese Baptist Convention / 中華基督教浸信會聯會
   - 252 issues across 守望, 浸會之聲, 中華浸信會聯會會刊, plus commemorative publication.
   - Projection value: Spectrum + church history / ministry.

5. Holy Light Theological Seminary / 聖光神學院
   - 45 monographic items and five serial titles with substantial issue runs.
   - Projection value: Spectrum + theology / theological education / ministry.

6. Reichelt Collection / 道風山 related materials
   - 101 monographs, 31 manuscripts, 7 serial titles; multilingual Christianity / Chinese religion / philosophy corpus.
   - Projection value: Curated Collections + Spectrum + Three Morning Stars.

7. Studium Biblicum OFM / 思高聖經學會 translation documentation
   - 32 manuscript volumes including translation notes and 31 books of the Bible in Chinese.
   - 29 Chinese-language books of the Bible published 1946–1964.
   - Projection value: Curated Collections + Spectrum + Three Morning Stars; high-value Scripture translation semantic route.

8. Taiwan Lutheran Church / 基督教台灣信義會
   - 43 local church publication volumes, 5 anniversary volumes, 27 annual reports, 60 newsletter issues, 345 issues of 播種者.
   - Projection value: Spectrum + church history / ministry.

9. Lin Collection of Lantern Slides and Photographs
   - circa 900 original photographs, printed materials, rare books and artifacts documenting Chinese society, cross-cultural encounter, Christianity in China and indigenous Chinese church growth.
   - Projection value: Curated Collections first; supporting visual layer for Spectrum and historical routes.

10. United Board for Christian Higher Education in Asia archives
   - China college files across major Christian universities; portions of China College Files and Audio-Visual Materials digitized.
   - Projection value: Curated Collections + Cinema/AV discovery + Spectrum, subject to item-level policy and exclusion rules.
