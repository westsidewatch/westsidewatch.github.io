# Dawn Chinese Gap Acquisition v1

Status: ACTIVE
Started: 2026-09-25
Depends on: canonical Work identity + Living semantic projection + shared-resource join

## Objective

Move from structural cleanup to material Chinese growth. The goal is not parity by raw count. The goal is to make the shared Dawn resource layer genuinely usable from Chinese entry points across the dual index:

- material / experience: image-led Curated Collections, video-led Cinema, text-led Spectrum, book-led Three Morning Stars;
- semantic / ministry / knowledge: Scripture, Bible study, prayer, exegesis, theology, biblical world, church history, Christian life, ministry, preaching, worship, mission, people, places, periods and finer coordinates.

## Existing baseline

- canonical Works: 203,448
- normalized Chinese Works discovered by canonical scan: about 2,997
- semantic projection coverage: full canonical corpus by Work ID
- Resource Fabric: partial/legacy enrichment layer; it must not cap acquisition

## Acquisition principle

Chinese-first means discovery and promotion order, not lower standards.

Every candidate still passes the existing Source Policy / rights / relevance / authority / canonical admission chain. Wikisource remains forbidden. Existing exclusion of political / ethnic / war controversy material remains unchanged.

No parallel Chinese database is created. Accepted resources become canonical Works/resources in the shared substrate and may then be called by any consumer.

## Priority lanes

### A. Chinese bibliography / discovery authority
Use institutional theological-library catalogues and public bibliographic datasets to discover titles, authors, editions, subject vocabulary and collection gaps. Catalogue discovery does not imply rehosting rights.

Initial authority pool:
- Logos Evangelical Seminary library: large Chinese theological collection and audiovisual holdings.
- China Evangelical Seminary / theological research institute library catalogues and electronic-resource guides.
- China Evangelical Seminary North America library new-book / ebook lists.
- public Chinese Christian bibliography datasets where provenance and metadata quality can be independently checked.

### B. Rights-explicit Chinese full-text
Prioritize sources that explicitly authorize public reading/download/distribution. Store source rights separately from bibliographic authority; never infer rehost permission merely because a file is downloadable.

### C. Chinese visual corpus
Acquire rights-safe maps, manuscripts, archaeology, architecture, historical photographs, diagrams and other visual resources for image-led Curated Collections. Prefer institutional museums, libraries, archives and other first-party/public-domain sources.

### D. Chinese video corpus
Acquire first-party/authorized Chinese sermons, lectures, courses, documentaries, interviews and archival video for Cinema. Preserve provider pointer and rights; do not silently rehost.

### E. Chinese text-knowledge bridges
For large English clusters with little Chinese material, prioritize Chinese introductions, translations, reference works, scholarship and terminology bridges so Spectrum can be entered in Chinese without hiding the deeper English corpus.

### F. Chinese book depth
For Three Morning Stars and durable reading, prioritize important Chinese originals, translations, editions and bibliographic authority. Selection remains downstream editorial judgment, not admission.

## Admission roles

For every semantic cluster, acquisition should seek a balanced Chinese set rather than only books:

- ENTRY — accessible Chinese introduction / orientation
- PRIMARY — primary text / source where applicable
- REFERENCE — dictionary, handbook, atlas, index, chronology
- SCHOLARSHIP — research / commentary / monograph
- VISUAL — maps, images, manuscripts, diagrams, objects
- VIDEO — lecture, sermon, documentary, course, interview
- BOOK — durable book / edition candidates
- BRIDGE — Chinese-English terminology and cross-language pointer
