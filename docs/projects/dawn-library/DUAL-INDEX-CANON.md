# Dawn Shared Resource Layer — Dual Index Canon

Status: CANONICAL CONCEPT
Date: 2026-09-25
Scope: shared Dawn resource substrate, editorial scan, retrieval, and public guidance
Related authority: `EDITORIAL-PROJECTION-CANON.md`

## Core model

The shared resource layer has **two simultaneous index axes**. They must not be collapsed into one taxonomy.

### Axis A — Material / experience index（材質索引線）

This is the **primary public-facing guidance layer**. Its purpose is to help a visitor decide how to enter the corpus and what kind of experience to have.

Current leading projections:

- **Curated Collections / 策展集** — image-led synthesis: 看圖、地圖、手稿、攝影、考古、建築、物件與其他視覺證據。
- **Cinema / 天堂電影院** — video-led synthesis: 看視頻、電影、紀錄片、講道、課程、訪談與歷史影像。
- **Spectrum / 光譜** — text-knowledge-led thematic synthesis: 看主題、知識脈絡、文本、研究與關係。
- **Three Morning Stars / 三晨星** — book-led durable selection: 看書，進入值得長期閱讀與保存的作品。

Axis A is intentionally coarse and legible. It is not the full ontology. It exists to invite reading, viewing and exploration.

### Axis B — Semantic / ministry / knowledge index（語義／事工／知識索引線）

This is the **fine-grained underlying retrieval and composition layer**. Existing examples include Scripture / 經文, Bible study / 查經, Prayer / 禱告, theology, exegesis, biblical world, church history, Christian life, ministry, preaching, worship, mission, discipleship, spiritual formation, people, places, periods, books of the Bible, doctrines, practices, events, and other increasingly fine coordinates.

Axis B is expected to become very deep and very granular. It exists primarily for retrieval, relationships, recommendation, composition, tools, and product calls. It is **not appropriate to dump the complete taxonomy onto the public page**.

## Public guidance rule

Public pages should expose only a **small, editorially selected window** into Axis B at any one time.

Examples:

- a homepage may surface `經文 / 查經 / 禱告 / 教會歷史 / 聖經世界` as five useful doors while hundreds or thousands of deeper coordinates remain hidden;
- a Jerusalem Curated Collection may surface `耶路撒冷 / 聖殿 / 城門 / 第二聖殿 / 耶穌進城`, while the underlying graph retains much finer place, period, Scripture, archaeology and source coordinates;
- a Cinema route may expose `講道 / 聖經課程 / 紀錄片`, while retrieval underneath can use speaker, Scripture passage, timestamp, ministry, topic, place and period.

Selection of visible Axis-B guides should be contextual, evidence-based and limited enough to remain readable.

## Relationship between the axes

The two axes cross rather than compete.

A resource can be addressed simultaneously by:

`material / experience × semantic coordinate`

Examples:

- image-led × Jerusalem × Second Temple;
- video-led × Matthew 21 × Triumphal Entry;
- text-led × Prayer × Lord's Prayer;
- book-led × Church History × Reformation.

This crossing is the basis for reusable retrieval. A product can request a medium experience and a semantic coordinate without owning a separate database.

## Consumer rule

Products may call either axis or combine them freely:

- Dawn Library may use Axis A to invite exploration and selected Axis B doors to guide subject entry.
- ONE may rely heavily on Axis B Scripture and Bible-study coordinates while calling image/video/text/book projections from Axis A.
- Cinema uses video-led presentation but may retrieve by Scripture, speaker, topic, period or place.
- Journal and Multiwrite / 成書 may retrieve resources by semantic coordinates and then choose the appropriate material projection for publication.
- Search may traverse both axes.

Underlying canonical resource identity remains unchanged.

## Scan requirement

The Full-Library Editorial Scan must now produce **both**:

1. a material/medium topology suitable for Axis-A projections;
2. a fine semantic topology suitable for Axis-B retrieval.

For Axis B the scanner should preserve and deepen fine coordinates rather than flattening them into a small set of homepage categories. Public guide candidates should be generated separately from the full semantic index.

## Public-guide candidate model

A semantic coordinate may be surfaced publicly when it has one or more of the following:

- enough high-quality resources to sustain exploration;
- strong current relevance to a page or route;
- strong bridge value between several resource clusters;
- a useful Chinese entry point;
- an important gap that deserves an explicit guided route;
- high Scripture, study, prayer, historical, theological or ministry usefulness.

The visible set should remain deliberately small. The underlying index may continue to grow without corresponding navigation clutter.

## Canon rule

This dual-index model governs current work. Earlier descriptions that treat Curated Collections / Cinema / Spectrum / Three Morning Stars as the whole classification system are incomplete. They are the coarse **material/experience index**. The fine semantic/ministry/knowledge index remains active underneath and is a first-class part of the shared resource layer.
