# Dawn Library — Full-Library Editorial Scan v1

Status: ACTIVE
Started: 2026-09-24
Canonical substrate: `static/dawn-library/living/root.json` + 21 Living Library shards
Canonical Works at start: **203,448**

## Purpose

Turn the existing collection into an editorially navigable library without creating a second identity system. The scan produces three projection layers over canonical Dawn Works:

1. **Spectrum / 光譜** — reveal knowledge continuities, contrasts, eras, schools, media and evidence density.
2. **Curated Collections / 策展集** — turn meaningful clusters into guided, visual, readable routes. `Jerusalem 3000` is the first production reference.
3. **Three Morning Stars / 三晨星** — identify works that deserve durable editorial attention and explain why; this is not a rating system.

Chinese acquisition runs in parallel and is driven by gaps exposed by the scan.

## Hard boundaries

- Dawn remains the only Work identity authority.
- Scan outputs are projections; they do not create duplicate Works.
- Chinese discovery/admission is prioritized, but authority / rights / relevance gates are not lowered.
- Wikisource remains permanently forbidden.
- Political / ethnic / war controversy material remains outside Dawn canonical admission under the existing policy.
- Existing engineering projects are not pulled into a curated collection merely because their research overlaps. Mature engineering outputs may be called later.

## Scan passes

### P0 — Corpus integrity + language normalization — ACTIVE
Measure all 21 shards, deduplicate language aliases, identify missing/unknown metadata, and establish a trustworthy denominator for later editorial work.

### P1 — Topic / period / author / medium topology — QUEUED
Build distributions and co-occurrence maps from the actual Works. Detect oversized pools, thin but important areas, isolated islands and bridge Works.

### P2 — Chinese gap map — QUEUED
For every meaningful cluster, calculate whether a Chinese reader has: entry work, reference work, classic work, modern research, visual/map material, and legal access pointer. Missing roles become acquisition targets.

### P3 — Spectrum candidates — QUEUED
Generate candidate spectra only where the corpus shows a real continuum or productive contrast. Each candidate must cite its supporting Works and expose Chinese gaps.

### P4 — Curated-collection candidates — QUEUED
Select clusters that can sustain a guided editorial route. Require a thesis, route, visual/evidence layer, Chinese entry, English depth layer and rights-safe access. Jerusalem 3000 is the reference implementation, not a template to copy mechanically.

### P5 — Three Morning Stars candidates — QUEUED
Identify durable works through authority, historical influence, usefulness, distinctiveness, evidence quality and relationship density. Store reasons and evidence; never collapse this into a numeric star rating.

### P6 — Website guidance — QUEUED
Publish only mature examples. Homepage guidance should let a Chinese reader enter by question / theme / route rather than confronting a 200k-item wall.

## Chinese acquisition queue v0

Acquisition priority is not “more Chinese books” in the abstract. For each confirmed spectrum/collection, fill these roles in order:

1. 中文入口：普通讀者能開始的可靠作品。
2. 中文經典：有長期影響力的譯著／原著。
3. 中文工具：辭典、索引、地圖、年表、文本工具。
4. 中文研究：大學、研究機構、博物館、考古／歷史出版物。
5. 中文視覺資料：合法圖集、地圖、手稿、攝影與展覽資源。
6. 中英橋接：同一 Work / subject 的中文入口與英文深層研究相互指向。
