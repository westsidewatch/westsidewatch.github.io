## 1. Problem

The current P2 layout puts an approved Jerusalem city-gate issue cover on the right and a separate typographic animation on the left. Browser and build checks pass, but the editor rejected the actual screenshot (2026-10-27, around 79% scroll).

Observed problems from that real screenshot:
- The left side has a featureless gold-paper gap, an isolated star, and an unrelated heavy grey “西望” object far below it; there is no directed reading sequence.
- The fake deep extrusion looks like concrete rather than engraving or refined printing; its apparent shadows compete with the carved sandstone and golden gate light.
- Seven faint “西望” repeats look like residue or dirt rather than purposeful ink registration.
- Horizontal hairlines cross the large Chinese characters; an arbitrary dark-blue top strip does not share a meaningful edge or hierarchy with the crenellated right cover.
- The left page reads as a motion-effects test while the right page reads as a complete publication cover.

Passing 41 browser scenes is *not evidence of art direction*. Several original tests explicitly require 21 depth layers and seven visible impressions. Those checks are implementation-preserving, not visual-acceptance criteria; they need reconsideration when the design changes.

## 5. Publication composition (design proposal for review)

### 5.1 Page roles

- **Right** is the architectural climax: a complete 5:8 cover, midnight-blue title field, intact sandstone skyline/portal and the gate's existing printed golden light. Its geometry, copy and render pipeline are immutable for this task.
- **Left** is a quiet title/insignia opening. It supports and leads into the right cover. Reference meaning is *stone-paper folio and engraved emblem*, not a literal second building. Prefer a continuous authentic warm Temple Stone paper surface; do not assume that pasting the cover's dark-blue strip onto its top 13% makes a unified spread.
- The **Morning Star** and **西望** should form a single vertical title group rather than one emblem at 40% of the page and a disconnected name at the bottom-left. Its pendant/engraving vocabulary is borrowed from the reference; not its exact typography.
- Visual texture should come from the already approved stone grain and real emblem vectors, not 84 stamped words or random divider lines. Tiny register marks, if any, must serve the print narrative without becoming visible wallpaper.
- Hierarchy at the hold frame: (1) right cover masthead/gate, (2) left Star + 西望 one identity group, (3) small editorial/folio metadata if genuinely needed. No equally strong second architectural spectacle on the left.

### 5.2 Candidate proportions, subject to genuine image review

**All normalized dimensions below describe the left page's actual bounding box after layout; not percentages of the whole browser window.** They are review targets, not mandatory geometry copied blindly from the book.

Allow modest optical offsets when the original artwork's visual center differs from its SVG viewBox center. The final title scale, star location and margins must be tuned on real desktop/mobile images by the editor; do not overfit a single 1648-pixel screenshot.

### 5.3 Anti-collision design checks

- Large Chinese title cannot intersect any rule, border, imprint, shadow or the Morning Star. At 68%, 79%, 82% check actual transformed bounding boxes, not source SVG viewBox alone.
- A line is permitted only where its beginning/ending and purpose are readable. Remove the current ~74% horizontal hairline rather than let it pass behind/through the title.
- The original source silhouette must be recognizably two Chinese characters; avoid edge extrusion/alpha layering that fills counterforms or yields a cement effect.
- No partial cover of the gate by left-page visuals; ensure the spine/gutter has a clean edge and that z-index isolation is real.
- The blue/sandstone transition should be visually purposeful, not just a match of individual RGB values or a straight line pasted against a jagged skyline.
- Background repeated marks are not an acceptance requirement and may be removed entirely at the held spread; the seven editorial colors stay in navigation.

## 7. Alternatives and their failure conditions

1. **Continue iterating 21-layer letterpress and seven shadow stamps** — rejected. Even with subtler gold or softer shadows, the left page stays a typographic object test, not a publisher-designed chapter opener. This failed visibly on the actual screenshot.
2. **Mirror the right architectural gate or add a second gate image on the left** — rejected. It produces competing buildings, discards original negative-space logic, and risks unauthorized cover manipulation. It also confuses where the actual golden gate light originates.
3. **Quiet stone-paper chapter opener with a single Morning Star/西望 group** — current candidate. Better matches the inspected Portály spread's quiet-left/full-architecture-right relationship and minimizes new decorative material. Risk: left might appear too empty or the wordmark too small, especially at 320px; prove hierarchy at p44 and p79 before signoff.
4. **Use only the Star and remove 西望** — rejected against the editor's identity requirement and the site’s earlier formal SVG typography decisions.

**Counterargument to #3:** A literary two-column book chapter opening is not an animated cover. Simply copying its whitespace could make the left slide inert. The implementation must keep a purposeful moving title at p44 and an integrated star/title hold at p79; the independent critic should be encouraged to reject #3 if this cannot be demonstrated in genuine screenshots.

## 8. Accessibility, performance and implementation risks

- In normal-motion mode, **preserve the approved Morning Star v5.3 iframe/WebGL artwork and its original internal rotation, rays and rendering cadence**. That independently authored interior rendering is *exempt* from the publication-page's single `progress` clock. The outer star container's position, visibility, entrance and departure are **not** exempt: all must remain tied to the one reversible `progress` value. Check both WebGL and no-WebGL rendering; a blank/starless result is a failure.
- The only clock for **publication transitions** is existing `progress`; one-way CSS entrance animations or time-based page layers that break reverse scrolling are disallowed. Do not modify the iframe's protected internal animation engine merely to collapse separate renderer motion into that outer clock.
- For `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, eliminate transitional slides, fades, 3D spin and moving rays **without removing content**. The original approved **static Morning Star SVG** replaces the autoplay/WebGL iframe at the *host page* level; the v5.3 source files remain immutable. Snap to the following **meaningful states**, directly controlled by requested `progress`: p≤30 = intact cover arrival (no unreadable intermediate clip); p31–55 = large legible original 西望 identity; p56–86 = final star + 西望 publication spread with safe separation; p≥87 = readable canonical Contents with its 12 entries and controls. Keyboard, scroll reversal and direct `?p=` navigation must reach every meaningful state. Do not let the old `const stone=ease((progress-.865)/.125)` override this snap behavior; adjust that transition in implementation only after signoff.
- On narrow viewports ≤820px, the original cover/left folio stacks; re-evaluate vertical Star/title safe space in the *page* box, not desktop offsets.
- At 200% browser zoom, text must remain legible and not cut off within fixed-height tiles.
- Preserve `data-ws-scope` and font/weight tokens; verify fonts actually resolve, rather than relying on fallback.
- Do not introduce network-hosted image assets or new runtime bundles. Reuse original SVG, existing paper texture and local CSS.
- A stock photograph, unverified image reference, star substitute, or programmatic pixel sampling does not count as visual evidence.
- Existing unrelated failing Core Contract items must be disclosed separately, not hidden or bypassed.
