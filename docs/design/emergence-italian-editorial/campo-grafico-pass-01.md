# Campo Grafico — Issue Study Pass 01

Status: evidence extraction in progress
Scope: establish issue-level method before completing all 66 issues.

## Corpus fact

Campo Grafico ran 66 issues from 1933 to 1939. The archive and later Campo Grafico historical material explicitly describe each issue as capable of changing completely from the previous one, cover through interior. The corpus spans minimal typography, futurist/constructivist saturation, photomontage, bichrome, four-colour, abstract composition and material experiments.

This is the direct historical precedent for Doré Journal's rule: identity is editorial intelligence, not a repeated template.

## 1933 — Year I: range itself becomes identity

The documented cover spectrum already moves between extreme poles: constructivist intensity, bichrome restraint, four-colour richness and complex photomontage. Historical summaries identify work ranging from typographic collage and pure typography to abstract composition, woodcut and photomontage.

Extraction:
- issue identity may be generated from the current editorial problem;
- consistency can live one level above appearance;
- contrast across issues is part of the publication's identity;
- a Journal system must permit radical local grammar without losing canonical publication identity.

Material evidence recorded for Year I includes aluminized papers and experimental binding.

Emergence translation:
- metallic/material shift -> light-dependent state change;
- photomontage -> concurrent visual memory states;
- radical issue-to-issue change -> work-conditioned grammar selection.

## 1934-01 — velvet cover

24 pages. Cover by Carlo Dradi and Attilio Rossi. The archive records an Isard velvet-paper cover.

Extraction:
`surface is semantic`.

A cover may establish meaning before image or typography through tactility/material expectation.

Digital translation:
- do not fake velvet visually as decoration;
- translate tactile distinction into interaction/response only when subject warrants it;
- material grammar belongs beside image grammar, not below it as finishing.

## 1934-02 — oversized demonstration insert

The archive records a 297 x 420 mm Linotype advertising/demonstration insert plus another demonstration insert.

Extraction:
`format can temporarily exceed the normal page`.

Digital translation:
- normal reading width can be deliberately exceeded for a semantic specimen;
- an insert is not merely another block: it interrupts the publication's spatial contract and then returns.

Emergence grammar:
`stable field -> expansion beyond field -> inspection -> return`.

## 1934-03 — out-of-text demonstration table

The issue records advertising/demonstrative inserts from printing-ink/colour producers.

Extraction:
`the magazine can contain demonstrations of its own means of production`.

For Doré Journal this means research/process artefacts may enter the publication when the material process itself is part of the thought. The publication need not hide how it was made.

## 1934-05 — glued printed covers as evidence

The archive records an illustrative table with book covers physically glued onto the page.

Extraction:
`citation can become material evidence`.

Instead of reproducing an example as a neutral illustration, an editorial surface may preserve the example's objecthood and make the act of attachment visible.

Digital translation:
- source artefact should sometimes remain visibly distinct from the host page;
- provenance can be spatial, not only footnoted;
- attachment can be a grammar: host field + foreign object + visible boundary.

## 1934-06 — glued quartino / multilingual type specimen

The archive records a glued quartino from a German printer/foundry containing Oriental and Asian characters.

Extraction:
`a specimen can enter as a foreign typographic body`.

Digital translation:
- preserve typographic otherness instead of normalizing all content into the Journal's house type;
- imported evidence may carry its own local grammar while remaining inside the issue.

## 1934-09 — experimental editions inside the periodical ecology

The archive notes an announcement for Campo Grafico experimental editions, including Lucio Fontana.

Extraction:
`publication can be a laboratory that produces adjacent experimental artefacts`.

This supports Doré Design / Journal / publishing experiments living around the canonical issue without all becoming public-site navigation.

## First grammar cluster

Pass 01 yields seven reusable Campo Grafico grammars:

1. `issue-reinvention` — every issue may regenerate its visual constitution.
2. `material-first` — paper/surface/production can carry meaning before layout.
3. `format-excursion` — an insert may exceed the normal spatial contract.
4. `production-as-content` — demonstrate the means of making inside the publication.
5. `attached-evidence` — cited objects can retain visible objecthood/boundaries.
6. `foreign-local-grammar` — imported material may preserve its own typography/visual system.
7. `publication-as-laboratory` — the periodical can generate experimental side artefacts.

These are not Campo Grafico style presets. They are composable editorial operations.
