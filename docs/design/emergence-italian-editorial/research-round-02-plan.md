# Italian Editorial DNA — Research Round 02

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: ACTIVE
Parent: Emergence / publishing.dimensional-writing

## Objective

Move from magazine-level style labels to reusable editorial intelligence. Journal is explicitly an independent-magazine surface: the main site keeps a stable navigation and interaction architecture, while every Journal issue may invent a complete visual world from its own theme and content.

Canonical rule:

> Main site identity is stable architecture. Journal identity is stable editorial intelligence with issue-specific visual invention.

## Research sequence

1. Campo Grafico — inspect the 66-issue corpus as experiments, extracting cover, spread, insert, transparency, photomontage, colour, paper and reveal grammars.
2. Stile Industria — inspect all 41 issues for image/text/object/material strategies.
3. Domus — split by editor and art-direction era rather than treating the brand as one style.
4. Casabella — split by editorial/graphic eras.
5. Abitare — split by editorial/graphic eras and study dwelling/travel/photography relations.
6. Studio Boggeri — study typography + photography + industrial communication synthesis.
7. Industrial photography — Aldo Ballo and related object/document traditions.
8. Olivetti visual ecology — identity across graphics, objects, exhibitions, advertising, architecture and editorial culture.
9. Physical semantics — paper, ink, overprint, binding, inserts, folds, transparency and die-cutting.
10. Digital translation — map physical/page grammar to Emergence temporal grammar.

## Atlas model upgrade

Replace flat `magazine -> style` with:

`publication -> era -> grammar modules`

Composable modules:

- layout grammar
- image grammar
- typography grammar
- material grammar
- density grammar
- sequence grammar
- irony grammar
- emergence grammar

The goal is composition, not cosplay. A future Doré result may combine, for example, one lineage's spatial judgement with another lineage's photographic grammar and a Westside-native temporal grammar.

## Same-source-image benchmark

This is the central experimental protocol.

- Freeze one neutral source image.
- Preserve subject identity/content.
- Run every editorial DNA family against that exact source.
- Hide family names/logos during evaluation.
- Judge whether each output expresses a genuinely different editorial intelligence rather than colour/font/crop variation.
- Repeat with a second, semantically different source image.
- A family passes only if its grammar transfers across both sources.
- Record failed or generic outputs in Rejection Memory.

PASS means the viewer can perceive distinct editorial judgement without relying on copied mastheads, logos or one-to-one historical cover imitation.

## Vol.00 transfer benchmark

After neutral-image validation, apply the learned grammar to The Gate / `一萬年的建城禮`.

Required semantic mapping:

- many ruins -> repetition + accumulation
- ten thousand years -> temporal depth
- archaeological tell -> stratification
- desolation -> density without living presence
- human building ambition -> visual excess
- final question -> sudden reduction / silence

The target is not an Italian-looking page. The target is a new layout that historical magazines never made, but which could only be produced because Doré has learned their editorial intelligence.

## Success criterion

Italian editorial DNA has entered Doré when learning no longer produces imitation. It produces new issue-specific visual language from subject, theme, editorial judgement and time.
