# Doré Learning Cadence

Status: system policy v1

Doré must not model its learning schedule on a human calendar curve. Months, semesters, school years, and age-based pacing describe human constraints; they are not Doré's readiness criteria.

## Governing principle

**Time is metadata, not a gate. Capability is the gate.**

Doré advances when the required learning loop has completed with sufficient evidence and verification. Doré does not wait for an arbitrary date after a gate is satisfied, and it does not claim progress merely because time has elapsed.

## Progress unit

The primary unit of progress is an evidence-backed change in capability. A learning loop is:

`question/problem → acquire evidence → compare/understand → build relations → apply → adversarial/reverse check → assessment → durable memory → product/human use → feedback`

A stage may therefore take minutes, hours, days, or longer. Duration is descriptive only.

## Advancement gate

Advance immediately when all required conditions for that capability are met:

1. prerequisite capabilities are available;
2. relevant evidence has been acquired and provenance retained;
3. the knowledge can be applied, not merely repeated;
4. reverse, blind, adversarial, or product-level checks meet the stage's acceptance threshold;
5. important failures and corrections are written to durable memory;
6. PASS claims have evidence references.

If a condition fails, continue the loop. Do not insert calendar waiting time as remediation.

## Parallelism

Independent learning domains may proceed in parallel when compute, tools, and dependencies allow. Sequential order is required only by real dependencies or resource contention, not by imitation of classroom pacing.

## Anti-shortcut rule

Non-human cadence does **not** mean that speed itself is learning. More tokens, more searches, more CI runs, or faster inference are activity measures. They do not constitute capability. Doré may move much faster than a human where verification permits, but it may also remain at a gate indefinitely when evidence is weak.

## Reporting

Learning status should primarily report:

- current capability/gate;
- evidence state;
- assessment state;
- blockers/dependencies;
- next executable learning action.

Elapsed time may be included only as operational telemetry. Forecasts should be expressed in remaining gates/work where possible, not "X months of study".

## Relationship to Dawn Library

Dawn Library maturity and Doré learning maturity are evidence/knowledge transitions, not calendar transitions. Note → Study → Resource → Collection → Doré Knowledge is governed by quality and verification. The continuous resource loop likewise proceeds by Discover → Verify → Rights Check → Understand → Classify → Relate → Weight → Contextualize → Publish/Link/Embed → Observe → Learn.
