# DORÉ FILM — Experimental Film 01

Status: ACTIVE FILM PRODUCTION

This is the first experiment judged as a film rather than a capability demo.

## Question

When engineering traces recede, does the Doré Biblical Long Scroll begin to feel like cinema?

## Passage

Patriarchal passage: Abraham → Isaac → Jacob.

## Runtime target

20–30 seconds, one uninterrupted camera consciousness.

## Film grammar

Doré original canvas → camera enters/approaches the world → meaningful micro-life → camera leaves the frame → world/time bridge → next Doré original → Scripture quietly emerges.

Light may enter only if it can obey the canonical rule: engraved golden line, not glow/particles/fill; it must not imply that canonical Genesis Light begins in the patriarchal passage.

## Non-negotiables

- one camera consciousness;
- no slideshow reset;
- Doré originals remain recognizable anchors;
- black-and-white main world;
- no sound in Edition One;
- bilingual visible text only; English Scripture is KJV;
- no invented miracle;
- macro composition stays Doré; micro-action carries life;
- generation-minimal: do not regenerate the world when camera/geometry/compositing can carry the shot.

## Acceptance

Do not judge this by CI success. Judge the rendered MP4 as a film fragment:

1. Does it have cinematic attention rather than engineering camera motion?
2. Does at least one Doré image become a living world without losing Doré identity?
3. Does leaving the frame feel like entering continuing biblical space/time?
4. Does the next anchor arrive as discovery rather than a slide change?
5. Does the 20–30 second fragment create a coherent emotional/narrative movement?

The output is evidence for the next production scale, not a polished final sequence.
