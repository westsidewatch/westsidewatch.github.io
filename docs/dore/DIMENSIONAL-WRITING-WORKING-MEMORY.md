# Doré Dimensional Writing — Working Memory

Status: shared writing grammar working memory. This is not reader-facing manuscript. It records reusable writing discoveries from real writing work and should be consulted by future dimensional-writing consumers.

## 2026-09-25 — From bone / flesh / texture to skin / pores / hair

Writing quality is not complete when research has supplied structure, facts, links, and historical texture. The prose must continue through progressively finer levels:

- **Bone** — thesis, chronology, causal structure, chapter architecture.
- **Flesh** — facts, people, geography, dates, material culture, biblical and historical evidence.
- **Texture / mortar** — cross-links, small knowledge hooks, echoes, surprising but relevant digressions, transitions that bind large factual blocks instead of stacking them.
- **Skin** — scene, atmosphere, spatial presence, physical surfaces, weather, movement, scale, sound, light; nonfiction written with novelistic immediacy.
- **Pores** — felt experience and minute sensory or human perception that makes the historical scene inhabitable.
- **Hair** — extremely fine observed/researched detail: e.g. begin Lions' Gate not from an encyclopedia label but from what the four worn stone animals look like now, a crack, abrasion, missing edge, discoloration, the centuries visible on the surface.

The finer levels do **not** authorize invented facts. Reconstructed scene must be anchored in evidence. When a source gives a concrete scene/detail, prefer it over abstract summary. Where a detail is tradition, report the existence and provenance of the tradition as a fact rather than silently converting it into archaeology.

## Journalism grammar: factual arrangement carries the argument

A major conclusion should often be produced by **selection, sequence, juxtaposition, recurrence and stopping point**, not by the narrator announcing the conclusion.

Example grammar:

Baghdad destroyed -> Mongol advance -> Ain Jalut -> Baybars -> three years later Baybars inspects repairs and waqf accounts in Jerusalem and builds a hostel.

Do not automatically append: “therefore Baybars was God's instrument” or “therefore destruction became construction.” If the factual sequence has already led the reader there, stop before the explanatory sentence. The reader completes the inference.

This is not absence of editorial intelligence. The writer's intelligence operates in research, verification, selection, order, proportion and juxtaposition. Avoid repeatedly interrupting the text with disclaimers, interpretive signposts, “this means,” “interestingly,” “it is worth noting,” or AI-style thesis announcements.

Biblical parallels can work the same way: place Cyrus, Assyria as the rod, Babylon raised for judgment, and then the historical event beside one another. Do not force a fourth declarative theological verdict when Scripture plus facts already create the question or inference.

## Literary / New Journalism layer

Doré exploration of literary journalism / New Journalism confirms reusable techniques associated especially with Tom Wolfe and long-form reportage:

1. **Scene-by-scene construction** rather than continuous historical summary.
2. **Reported dialogue / exact words** where sources preserve them.
3. **Point of view / position in the scene** — let the reader experience what a participant could see or face, without inventing unrecorded thoughts.
4. **Status and incidental detail** — clothing, objects, rooms, gestures, surfaces, possessions, institutional routines, ordinary logistics. These small facts disclose the larger social world.
5. **Saturation reporting** — depth of research precedes apparent ease. A simple paragraph may rest on extensive research.

For historical writing, translate these methods ethically: reconstruct only from sourced evidence; do not invent dialogue, thoughts, weather, gestures or sensory facts merely to make prose cinematic. Historical chronicles, archaeology, geography, architecture, itineraries and material remains can supply the scene.

## Free writing / dimensional travel

The manuscript may travel far from the apparent local subject if the detour deepens the main line and returns with added meaning. Jerusalem may be written beginning from Rus' before the Mongol invasion; a gate may open Baghdad, Haran, the Euphrates, Ain Jalut and then return to a crack in one stone lion. “Off-topic” is permitted when the excursion advances the book's structural, theological, geographic, human or material argument.

Rule: **able to be simple -> keep simple; worth becoming complex -> do not flatten it.** Do not expand every allusion. Do not compress a genuine time tunnel into an encyclopedia label.

## Anti-AI prose gate

Avoid formulaic narrator labels such as:
- “《聖經》裡有一些很奇怪的王。”
- “事情後來走到很有意思的一步。”
- “這裡值得停一下。”
- “這個名字也要稍停一下。”
- “更重要的是……” when it merely announces importance.

Prefer entering directly through the fact, scene, quotation, object or movement.

During full revision remove unnecessary discourse particles, adverbs, conjunctions, transitions and filler, including habitual 「了」「呢」 where deletion strengthens the sentence. Preserve author-supplied material unless explicitly told to delete it; full revision is not permission to silently shorten or discard established material.

## Current Lions' Gate training example

Do not begin Baybars as “a thirteenth-century Mamluk sultan” and move on. Ask what the name opens. The four stone animals can open the Mamluk layer; Baybars can open Ain Jalut; Ain Jalut can open Mongol destruction from Rus' and Baghdad; the Mongol route can be compared geographically with Abraham's Ur-Haran-Canaan route; Baybars's 1263 Jerusalem visit can open the Dome of the Rock and its underground traditions; the stone lion can open biblical lions; Revelation 5 can turn lion into slain Lamb and carry the reader naturally toward the Sheep Gate.

The objective is not maximal information. It is a continuous spatial-temporal reading experience in which every detour returns with pressure on the main line.
