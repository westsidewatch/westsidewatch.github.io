# Doré Folio / 多寫 — Content Boundary

Status: LOCKED

The governing policy is [本站及所有產品：資源選取原則](resource-selection-policy.md).
It applies across all products, not just Folio. Controversial resources are excluded;
book shelves additionally require reviewed complete-work and edition evidence.
Public-domain rights, a source root page, search relevance, or EPUB export do not
establish that a resource is a book. Unknown items fail closed into a non-public
review queue. No minimum catalogue count overrides either gate.

The previously withdrawn two political/news items remain regression fixtures in
the shared policy tests. They cannot be adopted by any product under another format.
