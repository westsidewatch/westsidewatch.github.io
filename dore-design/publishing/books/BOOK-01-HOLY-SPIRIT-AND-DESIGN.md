## 1. Product identity

This is simultaneously:

1. a serious design textbook/resource;
2. a theological reflection on creativity, discernment, making, beauty, order, technology/AI and seeking the Holy Spirit's leading;
3. a gospel-bearing book;
4. a Westside Watch Journal special issue;
5. the documentary record of building the New Westside visual system.

The book is not commentary added after the website. The website, design system, Doré learning, Storybook experiments, failed directions, critique and manuscript are one research process.

## 2. Primary display form: the open book / the opening gate / the city of light

The principal digital expression should feel like **an open book that is also a sequence of opening gates and a city being entered**.

Core experiential thesis:

**HTML book = open book + opening doors/gates + a luminous city + page-turning progression.**

The user should be able to read it as a book, traverse it as architecture, and eventually print it as a physical publication.

This is not permission for literal castle decoration. The experience must derive from the New Westside grammar:

- page / spread = readable plane;
- gate / threshold = chapter transition or conceptual entry;
- city = accumulated editorial architecture;
- light = revelation/orientation/arrival, not generic gold effect;
- wall/bookmark/battlement = structural rhythm;
- Huarongdao / flow = editorial reconfiguration where justified;
- 5:8 / Editorial Gravity = hierarchy;
- Living Paper / Sacred Surface = material contexts;
- Doré engraving = editorial/art-direction language, not repeated wallpaper.

## 3. Web-to-print requirement

The HTML edition must be conceived so the same source can become a print edition.

Required architecture:

**structured manuscript → semantic chapters/sections/figures/specimens → interactive HTML rendering → print CSS/paged-media rendering → proofed PDF/physical print.**

Interactive behaviors require print equivalents. A gate-opening transition, for example, may become a chapter gate/spread in print. A moving editorial wall may become a static sequenced plate or foldout logic.

The digital book should not depend on effects that destroy reading order when printed.

## 4. Journal special issue: 《守望，一座光明的城》

The current Journal concept **《守望，一座光明的城》** becomes an important public exhibition/entry point for this book.

It should not merely advertise the book. It should demonstrate the book’s thesis through actual editorial experience.

The special issue can introduce and deepen the Journal’s twelve-column system because the column names, functions, narrative order and editorial purposes have rich theological content and can be further developed.

The twelve columns are not navigation labels only. The book should study:

- biblical/geographic name;
- Scripture and narrative associations;
- why the column was named that way;
- editorial function;
- spiritual/theological intention;
- rhythm within the issue;
- relationship to WATCH / WALK / WITNESS / WORSHIP or later refined structure;
- visual grammar and bookmark/battlement rhythm;
- what new theology/editorial meaning can responsibly be developed.

The Journal special therefore becomes both:

**a book chapter in public form** and **the launch/explanation of the twelve-column editorial world**.

## 5. Origin layer

Preserve the actual source order:

**prayer/seeking → recorded reception → Scripture → discernment → naming/annotation → editorial intention → design translation → experiment → critique → renewed discernment.**

Do not rewrite later design-token explanations as if they were the original spiritual source.

The Sacred Palette, column names, Westside Watch, Watch for the Dawn, Living Water, ONE, The Gate, Journal and other names must retain provenance.

## 6. Design textbook method

Every major design claim should teach:

**Observation → Principle → Reason → Rule → Counterexample → Experiment → Evidence → Revision → Transfer.**

Doré must continue studying mature design systems, Brand Bibles, CI/VI manuals, editorial systems and publication design. External sources teach system construction and pedagogy; they do not become Westside’s identity.

Study must feed:

**source dossier → Storybook experiment → browser evidence → manuscript passage → theory delta.**

No study batch is complete without durable writing.

## 7. Theological integrity

Canonical source boundary:

`dore-design/projects/new-westside-visual-construction/SOURCE-BOUNDARIES.md`

Do not mix idol-centered or non-Christian sacred symbolism into the design as visual atmosphere. Avoid syncretism and decorative spirituality.

Christian theological language must also remain disciplined. Doré/AI cannot certify that an aesthetic choice is divine revelation. It can preserve the author’s recorded reception, compare it with Scripture/source material, translate it into design hypotheses, and submit outcomes for discernment.

## 8. Visual research requirements

The book itself must become a high-level piece of publication design. The Brand Bible product loop should study:

- world-class Brand Bibles / VI / CI manuals;
- editorial books;
- cultural-institution guidelines;
- page/spread rhythm;
- grid systems;
- indexes and marginalia;
- diagrams and specimens;
- typography and bilingual composition;
- image/caption systems;
- front/back matter;
- physical binding/printing implications;
- digital book interactions;
- print/digital continuity.

Those findings should continuously return to Storybook as original New Westside book/spread/page experiments.

## 9. Required final visual proof

The final design theory must generate many mature, materially different New Westside website/book templates while maintaining one core identity.

The book should visually document this diversity rather than conceal it.

A useful final sequence may include:
- source/origin pages;
- theory pages;
- annotated design-system specimens;
- failed experiments;
- style families;
- closed-book identity test;
- complete homepage variants;
- Journal/ONE/Library/Gate examples;
- web interaction plates;
- final convergence.

## 10. Relationship to production site

The book and Storybook may explore freely. Production homepage/site remains protected until separate user aesthetic approval.

Book completion ≠ production promotion.
