# Book 02 Language Style Guide — v0.1

Status: ACTIVE / DESCRIPTIVE-FIRST STYLE SYSTEM
Book: 《神很遠 神很近：天國語言極簡史》
Established: 2026-09-04

## 1. Purpose

This guide is not a request to imitate Huang Renyu sentence by sentence. It studies transferable narrative methods associated with *1587, A Year of No Significance / 萬曆十五年* and combines them with the existing manuscript's own voice.

The target is a calm, literate, human Chinese prose style that does not read like generic AI output.

## 2. What to learn from 《萬曆十五年》

Transferable methods:

- begin with concrete events, people, places, decisions and small facts before announcing the abstract thesis;
- let large structures emerge from apparently small incidents;
- keep an analytical distance even when the material is dramatic;
- use people as lenses into a larger system, rather than turning every chapter into pure biography;
- combine readable narrative with serious argument;
- prefer controlled observation over emotional instruction to the reader;
- allow irony or contradiction to arise from facts rather than from mocking language;
- when reconstructing motives, mark inference carefully rather than converting speculation into fact;
- move between close detail and long historical/theological arcs.

Do not copy Huang Renyu's distinctive wording, sentence patterns, historical idioms, or recognizable passages.

## 3. Existing manuscript voice to preserve

The current manuscript already has a strong native pattern:

**scene → small detail → key word → lexical/theological opening → larger biblical arc → personal reflection.**

Examples of recurring strengths to preserve:

- chapter openings that enter a scene before explaining it;
- short paragraphs that create breathing room;
- Hebrew/Greek terms introduced as turning points rather than academic ornaments;
- repeated images such as wind, waiting, speech, silence, light and nearness;
- concrete physical actions that carry theological weight;
- sudden short sentences after longer explanatory passages;
- movement from ancient text to ordinary contemporary experience;
- a final reflective turn that remains simple rather than sermonizing.

## 4. Sentence-level rules

### Prefer nouns and verbs over adverbial inflation

Use the action itself when it is strong enough.

Prefer:
- 他坐在黑暗裡，沒有再說話。

Avoid:
- 他極其痛苦地、深深地陷入一種無法言喻的沉默之中。

### Adverbs

Use fewer adverbs, especially intensifiers such as:
- 非常
- 極其
- 深深地
- 真正地
- 顯然地
- 其實
- 無疑地

Keep them only when they change meaning rather than add emotional pressure.

### Adjectives

Adjectives are allowed, but they should name a visible, historical, material or conceptual quality. Prefer one exact adjective to three vague ones.

### Sentence rhythm

Default rhythm:
- medium sentence for information;
- longer sentence for relation/cause/arc;
- short sentence for weight.

Do not make every paragraph one sentence. Do not make every important sentence a standalone slogan.

### Paragraph length

Short paragraphs are part of the manuscript voice, but over-fragmentation becomes AI-like dramatic writing. Use paragraph breaks when the thought, scene, speaker, evidence level or rhetorical function changes.

## 5. Narrative temperature

Default: **cool surface, warm depth**.

The narrator should usually sound calm, observant and patient. Emotional weight comes from the event or image itself.

Avoid telling the reader what to feel:
- 令人震撼
- 令人感動
- 不禁讓人深思
- 這是何等奇妙
- 我們必須深刻認識到

Let the scene carry the force.

## 6. Exposition pattern

Preferred sequence:

1. What happened?
2. What exact word/text matters?
3. What does the word/text demonstrably mean?
4. What inference is being proposed?
5. Where does it sit in the larger biblical arc?
6. What does that change in how we read the story?

Do not begin every section with the conclusion.

## 7. Claim discipline in prose

Different claims require different verbal temperature.

### Textual fact
Use direct wording.

### Lexical fact
Use direct wording only when lexicon/source support is strong.

### Interpretive inference
Use phrases such as:
- 可以看見
- 這裡可能暗示
- 在這個框架下
- 至少可以這樣理解

### Author hypothesis
State explicitly that this is the book's proposed reading.

### Devotional reflection
Allow first-person or direct address, but keep it distinct from textual proof.

This prevents the popular book from sounding falsely certain.

## 8. Rhetorical questions

Use fewer.

A rhetorical question is useful when it genuinely opens an unresolved problem. It is weak when it merely forces agreement.

Avoid chains such as:
- 為什麼？因為……
- 這意味著什麼？意味著……
- 難道不是嗎？

Prefer direct development when the answer is already known.

## 9. Repetition

Keep motif repetition; remove explanatory repetition.

Good repetition:
- wind returns in a scene;
- waiting returns across chapters;
- a word returns with changed meaning;
- “神很遠，神很近” returns at structural thresholds.

Bad repetition:
- restating the thesis three times with synonyms;
- summary sentence after every paragraph;
- repeated “換句話說”.

## 10. AI-smell blacklist

Use sparingly or remove unless genuinely necessary:

- 值得注意的是
- 不難發現
- 顯而易見
- 這不僅僅是……更是……
- 從某種意義上說
- 可以說
- 深刻揭示了
- 賦予了……新的意義
- 形成了一個完整的閉環
- 在這個層面上
- 某種程度上
- 讓我們回到
- 真正重要的是
- 這正是……的核心

Also avoid excessive em dashes, excessive three-part parallelism, and repeated concluding aphorisms.

## 11. Historical/narrative restraint

When narrating biblical or historical scenes:

- distinguish text from reconstruction;
- use concrete sensory detail sparingly;
- do not invent psychological certainty;
- if adding atmosphere, keep it plausible and non-doctrinal;
- never let invented scenic detail become evidence for a theological claim.

## 12. Humor and irony

Low dosage. Dry, observational, never cute.

The book may notice contradiction, human limitation or institutional absurdity, but should not sound sarcastic or perform cleverness.

## 13. Theological prose

Prefer:
- image anchored in Scripture;
- argument anchored in text;
- reflection anchored in life.

Avoid stacking abstractions such as:
- 奧秘、維度、能量、頻率、層次、場域
unless the word is technically necessary and defined.

Do not borrow non-Christian mystical vocabulary to make theology sound profound.

## 14. Chapter architecture

A strong chapter should usually contain several of these movements, not necessarily in fixed order:

- scene;
- textual problem;
- lexical hinge;
- historical/biblical bridge;
- counter-reading;
- synthesis;
- quiet application;
- closing image.

The chapter should feel discovered, not mechanically outlined.

## 15. Relationship to the academic paper

The paper supplies claim maps, source burden and argument discipline.

The popular manuscript supplies story, cadence, scene and human readability.

Do not copy academic sentences directly into the book unless rewritten for narrative register.

## 16. English target

English should reproduce narrative temperature, not Chinese syntax.

Target qualities:
- plain but literary;
- concrete verbs;
- restrained modifiers;
- limited rhetorical questions;
- theological precision;
- no evangelical marketing language;
- no generic AI cadence.

Chinese remains the primary authored text.

## 17. Editing test

Before accepting an AI-written paragraph, ask:

1. Does it add a fact, image, argument or transition?
2. Could 20% of the modifiers be removed?
3. Does it tell the reader what to feel?
4. Does it sound like a summary of a summary?
5. Is a claim stronger than its evidence?
6. Is there an unnecessary rhetorical question?
7. Does the paragraph contain a phrase common to generic AI prose?
8. Does it still sound like this manuscript after removing the headline/theme words?

If several answers are unfavorable, rewrite.

## 18. Canonical formula

**冷靜敘述，具體名詞，準確動詞，節制修飾；先讓事情發生，再讓意義出現。**

And:

**大問題從小地方長出來，不靠大聲說出來。**
