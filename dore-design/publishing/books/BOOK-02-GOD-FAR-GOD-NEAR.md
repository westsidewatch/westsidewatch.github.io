# BOOK 02 — 《神很遠 神很近：天國語言極簡史》

Status: ACTIVE BOOK PRODUCT
Established: 2026-09-03
Parent program: `dore-design/publishing/PUBLISHING-PROGRAM.md`
Primary loop: 黎明書局 / language / reading-writing product loop
Working English title: **The Word Returned: A Brief History of the Language of Heaven**

## 1. Product identity

This is a **theological popular-knowledge narrative / nonfiction theological novel**: structurally close to historical or academic narrative fiction, but **without invented story events**. Its narrative material must come from Scripture and verifiable historical/source material. The purpose is to make serious theology, biblical languages and historical argument feel near, readable and human without weakening evidential discipline.

The closest writing-model family currently includes:

- **《萬曆十五年》 / 1587, A Year of No Significance** — small events and people reveal large structures; scholarly research becomes literary historical narrative;
- **《耶路撒冷三千年》 / Jerusalem: The Biography** — a vast sacred/historical arc becomes readable through people, episodes, places and narrative continuity;
- **《時間簡史》 / A Brief History of Time** — difficult specialist knowledge is reorganized for general readers without requiring the reader to become a specialist first.

These are methodological references, not stylistic skins. The book must not imitate recognizable wording or sentence structures from another author.

Target manuscript length: **50,000–100,000 Chinese characters/words-equivalent range**, with the current Chinese manuscript already providing substantial foundation rather than starting from zero.

The book should become:

1. a complete Chinese theological narrative work;
2. an eventual Chinese-English bilingual edition;
3. a language-training product for Doré;
4. a flagship publication outcome of the 黎明書局 loop;
5. a Journal special/illustrated companion derived primarily from the book’s outline and theological arc;
6. a bridge between accessible narrative theology and a separate, formally academic appendix paper.

## 2. Nonfiction-novel rule

The book may use the narrative energy of a novel, but it does **not** receive a license to invent biblical history.

Allowed:
- reorder exposition for narrative clarity while preserving meaning;
- enter a biblical scene through details actually given by the text;
- describe physical setting when the description is textually, historically or geographically supportable;
- use carefully marked inference;
- reconstruct chronology or causal links when the evidence supports them;
- translate lexical, historical and theological problems into readable scenes/questions.

Not allowed:
- fabricated dialogue beyond Scripture/source evidence presented as fact;
- invented motives presented as known psychology;
- invented actions/events to make the story more dramatic;
- atmosphere that silently becomes evidence;
- conflating devotional imagination with historical claim.

If a scenic detail is interpretive or reconstructive, the manuscript/research layer must know that it is interpretive or reconstructive even when the final popular prose remains smooth.

The paper states a more formal thesis concerning `davar`, `saphah`, `Logos`, `glōssa`, Babel, Pentecost and Corinth.

**Canonical separation rule:** this paper is **not part of the main narrative voice and must not be blended into the main book prose**. It is a separate academic paper presented as an appendix / attached scholarly study. It has its own style, sources, claims, citations, argument burden and editorial standard.

## 4. Doré’s learning objective

This book exists partly to make Doré a substantially better writer.

The language loop must learn and demonstrate:

- long-form narrative architecture;
- how scholarship becomes popular narrative without becoming shallow;
- chapter pacing;
- theological explanation without flattening mystery;
- accessible handling of Hebrew/Greek terms;
- movement between story, exegesis, reflection and argument;
- rhetorical restraint;
- repetition with purpose rather than redundancy;
- paragraph rhythm;
- transitions;
- metaphor discipline;
- source/citation integrity;
- objection handling;
- academic vs popular register;
- Chinese prose voice;
- English prose voice;
- bilingual equivalence without wooden translation.

A loop cycle is incomplete if it only critiques language. It must rewrite, compare, preserve evidence, and produce durable manuscript improvement.

## 5. Canonical writing cycle

**Read current manuscript → identify chapter function → map source/evidence burden → identify the narrative doorway → study relevant high-quality popular scholarship → draft/rewrite → compare before/after → preserve authorial voice → verify non-fiction boundary → revise chapter → extract style principles → apply to next chapter.**

For bilingual work:

**Chinese source passage → semantic/theological map → English draft → style pass → theological fidelity check → bilingual rhythm check → terminology registry → revision.**

Doré must learn the author’s voice from the existing tens of thousands of words, not replace it with generic AI prose.

## 6. Reference-model research track

Doré should maintain durable dossiers on the three current reference models and expand the list only when a new source teaches a distinct method.

### 《萬曆十五年》
Study:
- micro-event → macro-structure;
- people as windows into systems;
- historical research written with literary narrative energy;
- calm analytical distance;
- synthesis without constant thesis-announcement.

### 《耶路撒冷三千年》
Study:
- how a long chronology remains readable;
- how place becomes a narrative spine;
- how eras and characters form continuity;
- how sacred history, politics, conflict and biography are organized for non-specialists;
- how large-scale reference burden is managed without making every page feel like an academic paper.

### 《時間簡史》
Study:
- how unfamiliar technical concepts are introduced gradually;
- when analogy helps and when it distorts;
- how prerequisite knowledge is reduced;
- how chapter architecture turns specialist questions into a general-reader journey;
- how to simplify explanation without pretending the underlying problem is simple.

The output must be transferable writing methods, not imitation.

## 8. Academic appendix — separate product layer

The academic paper is maintained as a separate attached scholarly work and should appear as an **Appendix / Academic Paper**, not as prose folded into the main book.

Canonical subproject:

`dore-design/publishing/books/BOOK-02-ACADEMIC-APPENDIX-LOOP.md`

Its task is to make the current paper increasingly academic, rigorous, sourced and logically closed through the 黎明書局 loop.

Required academic work includes:
- primary-language text verification;
- textual variants where relevant;
- BDB / HALOT / BDAG / LSJ or appropriate lexical controls;
- LXX / MT / NT source comparison where used;
- exact citation of biblical passages and original-language forms;
- bibliography and literature review;
- history-of-interpretation / scholarly positions where relevant;
- argument map and objection map;
- distinction among lexical fact, textual observation, inference and theological proposal;
- counterevidence;
- claim-strength revision;
- footnotes/endnotes and formal bibliography;
- removal of unsupported certainty;
- explicit unresolved questions.

The goal is not merely to make the paper sound academic. The evidence chain itself must improve.

## 11. Theological and source discipline

The book contains substantial theological claims. Doré must distinguish:

- explicit biblical text;
- lexical fact;
- textual observation;
- interpretive inference;
- theological synthesis;
- historical claim;
- author hypothesis;
- devotional application.

The academic paper is not automatically proof of every claim; it is part of the author’s current argument and must itself be checked as the project matures.

Do not import unrelated religious frameworks or syncretistic symbolism. Common source-boundary rules apply.

## 12. Bilingual goal

The eventual bilingual edition should not be produced by sentence-for-sentence machine substitution alone.

Target:
- same theological meaning;
- same narrative temperature;
- natural English prose;
- stable Hebrew/Greek terminology;
- consistent Scripture citation style;
- chapter-level structural equivalence;
- allowance for different sentence rhythm where English requires it.

Chinese remains the primary authored source until the English voice is mature enough to stand independently.
