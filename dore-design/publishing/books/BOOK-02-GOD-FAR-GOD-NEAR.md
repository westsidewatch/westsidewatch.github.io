# BOOK 02 — 《神很遠 神很近：天國語言極簡史》

Status: ACTIVE BOOK PRODUCT
Established: 2026-09-03
Parent program: `dore-design/publishing/PUBLISHING-PROGRAM.md`
Primary loop: 黎明書局 / language / reading-writing product loop
Working English title: **The Word Returned: A Brief History of the Language of Heaven**

## 1. Product identity

This is a text-led theological narrative book intended to train Doré’s language, structure, theological exposition, editorial voice and eventually bilingual Chinese-English writing.

Target manuscript length: **50,000–100,000 Chinese characters/words-equivalent range**, with the current Chinese manuscript already providing substantial foundation rather than starting from zero.

The book should become:

1. a complete Chinese theological narrative work;
2. an eventual Chinese-English bilingual edition;
3. a language-training product for Doré;
4. a flagship publication outcome of the 黎明書局 loop;
5. a Journal special/illustrated companion derived primarily from the book’s outline and theological arc;
6. a bridge between accessible narrative theology and a shorter, more academic argument.

## 2. Current primary sources

Current supplied manuscript:

**《神很遠 神很近：天國語言極簡史》**

The manuscript already has a clear thesis and chapter arc: God gives language, human pride damages/loses it, the Word becomes flesh, and the Holy Spirit returns the gift; the current table of contents moves through creation, Babel, waiting, incarnation, Pentecost, Corinth, tongues, transformation, objections and appendices.

Current supplied academic companion:

**《天國語言極簡史｜論文》**

The paper states a more formal thesis concerning `davar`, `saphah`, `Logos`, `glōssa`, Babel, Pentecost and Corinth, and is a research/argument source for the broader book rather than a replacement for its narrative voice.

## 3. Doré’s learning objective

This book exists partly to make Doré a substantially better writer.

The language loop must learn and demonstrate:

- long-form narrative architecture;
- chapter pacing;
- theological explanation without flattening mystery;
- accessible handling of Hebrew/Greek terms;
- movement between story, exegesis, reflection and argument;
- rhetorical restraint;
- repetition with purpose rather than redundancy;
- paragraph rhythm;
- transitions;
- metaphor discipline;
- source/citation integrity;
- objection handling;
- academic vs popular register;
- Chinese prose voice;
- English prose voice;
- bilingual equivalence without wooden translation.

A loop cycle is incomplete if it only critiques language. It must rewrite, compare, preserve evidence, and produce durable manuscript improvement.

## 4. Canonical writing cycle

**Read current manuscript → identify chapter function → verify argument/source burden → diagnose language/structure → study high-quality relevant writing where permitted → rewrite sample → compare before/after → preserve authorial voice → revise chapter → extract style principles → apply to next chapter.**

For bilingual work:

**Chinese source passage → semantic/theological map → English draft → style pass → theological fidelity check → bilingual rhythm check → terminology registry → revision.**

Doré must learn the author’s voice from the existing tens of thousands of words, not replace it with generic AI prose.

## 5. Relationship to 黎明書局 loop

This is the primary publication product of the 黎明書局 loop.

The loop should contribute:

- reading lists;
- theology/language references;
- lexicon work;
- source verification;
- writing models;
- chapter editing;
- bilingual terminology;
- manuscript notes;
- publishing metadata;
- Journal adaptation material.

The book, in return, gives the loop a product target: reading/research must produce a better manuscript, not merely a larger resource library.

## 6. Academic companion role

The academic paper should be maintained as a separate but linked layer.

Functions:
- formalize the central thesis;
- expose evidence/argument chains more explicitly;
- identify claims requiring stronger lexical, exegetical, historical or theological support;
- provide notes/appendix material for advanced readers;
- supply diagrams/tables for the Journal special;
- help distinguish hypothesis, interpretation and established lexical fact.

Do not force the popular book to read like the paper. Do not let the popular book make a stronger academic claim than its evidence supports.

## 7. Journal special / illustrated companion

Unlike Book 01, this book is primarily text-led.

Its Journal expression should therefore use the **outline and argument arc as an illustrated special**, rather than turning every paragraph into decorative artwork.

Possible visual spine:

**Creation / Word → Eden / Naming → Babel / Fracture → Waiting → Logos / Incarnation → Pentecost / Return → Corinth / Mishearing → Inner renewal → Hope.**

Possible visual/editorial forms:
- timeline;
- Hebrew/Greek word plates;
- source-text comparison;
- maps where editorially relevant;
- manuscript marginalia;
- chapter-opening symbolic images;
- diagrams of the argument chain;
- selected narrative scenes;
- bilingual quotations;
- short academic inset;
- glossary/lexicon pages.

The Journal issue should invite readers into the full book, not reduce it to an infographic.

## 8. Language/style system to be learned

Doré must produce a durable language/style guide from the existing manuscript.

At minimum track:
- sentence-length tendencies;
- preferred narrative distance;
- use of short paragraphs;
- handling of rhetorical questions;
- Scripture introduction patterns;
- Hebrew/Greek formatting;
- metaphor density;
- recurring theological vocabulary;
- prohibited generic-AI phrases;
- humor/irony level;
- emotional restraint;
- chapter-opening method;
- chapter-closing cadence;
- use of repetition;
- bilingual translation choices.

This guide is descriptive first, prescriptive only after enough manuscript evidence.

## 9. Theological and source discipline

The book contains substantial theological claims. Doré must distinguish:

- explicit biblical text;
- lexical fact;
- interpretive inference;
- theological synthesis;
- historical claim;
- author hypothesis;
- devotional application.

The academic paper is not automatically proof of every claim; it is part of the author’s current argument and must itself be checked as the project matures.

Do not import unrelated religious frameworks or syncretistic symbolism. Common source-boundary rules apply.

## 10. Bilingual goal

The eventual bilingual edition should not be produced by sentence-for-sentence machine substitution alone.

Target:
- same theological meaning;
- same narrative temperature;
- natural English prose;
- stable Hebrew/Greek terminology;
- consistent Scripture citation style;
- chapter-level structural equivalence;
- allowance for different sentence rhythm where English requires it.

Chinese remains the primary authored source until the English voice is mature enough to stand independently.
