# Watchtower Fold — Doré self-critique

**Hypothesis.** Treat Westside as a threshold-city rather than a navigation dashboard: identity occupies one spatial mass, while five destinations become a processional route cut by a single dawn seam.

## Strong
- Clear spatial thesis: identity field versus navigable city field.
- Five destinations have hierarchy and procession without equal-card dashboard treatment.
- Bilingual type is integrated into the spatial structure rather than appended as translation.
- Mobile is recomposed into alternating path segments around a vertical dawn seam rather than merely shrinking desktop.
- Material strategy is architectural and light-based, with no dependency on stock imagery.

## Risks
- The monumental serif language can still drift toward contemporary editorial-site familiarity.
- The abstract architectural field may need real photography or archival material in later experiments to deepen Westside specificity.
- The city/path metaphor is stronger than prior card grids, but it still needs user judgment before becoming brand language.

## Provisional learning
Accept: threshold-as-spatial-structure, destination-procession-not-equal-cards, mobile-recomposition-around-one-visual-axis. Reject: generic-equal-card-navigation, desktop-only-composition-thinking.

Next: Can real Westside documentary imagery or stone/print material be fused into the threshold-city structure without weakening orientation?
