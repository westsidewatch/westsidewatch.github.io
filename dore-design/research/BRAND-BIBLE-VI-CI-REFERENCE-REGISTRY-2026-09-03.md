# Brand Bible / VI / CI Reference Registry

Status: ACTIVE DURABLE RESEARCH REGISTRY
Established: 2026-09-03
Project: New Westside Visual Construction / 《聖靈也會參與設計嗎？》

## Purpose

This file exists so external design references do not disappear after one conversation. Doré must be able to rediscover and revisit the exact sources later.

This registry is not a style-shopping list. Every source is assigned a learning role: structure, theory, teaching method, brand architecture, page sequencing, typography, color logic, application system, publication design, motion, accessibility, governance, or Storybook visual transfer.

All external sources are references, not authorities over the New Westside Visual Constitution.

## Permanent research rule

For each source Doré studies, preserve:

1. URL and source name;
2. source class: official brand guide / design system / historical manual / case study / independent DESIGN.md analysis / gallery;
3. what problem it solves;
4. how the document/site is structured;
5. how it teaches rather than merely lists rules;
6. visual/layout/typographic observations;
7. what is reusable as a method;
8. what must not be copied;
9. one manuscript idea;
10. one Storybook experiment or visual-study transfer, when applicable;
11. date reviewed and any version/date exposed by the source.

A source is not considered “learned” until the resulting notes are written and, when visually relevant, at least one experiment or comparative study has been produced.

# A. Modern design-system / technical teaching references

## Apple Human Interface Guidelines
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/
Design principles: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles
Class: official design system / teaching system
Why study: principles → foundations → patterns → components → inputs → technologies. Strong example of a system teaching judgment rather than only prescribing values.
New Westside question: how can principles such as purpose, responsibility, familiarity, flexibility, simplicity, craft and delight be translated into evaluative questions without importing Apple’s surface style?
Storybook transfer: build variants that test one principle at a time while preserving Westside core identity.

## Material Design
URL: https://m3.material.io/
Class: official design system
Why study: coherent world model; material/surface/light/motion relationships; components emerge from a theory rather than isolated visual rules.
New Westside question: can Light / Paper / Engraving / Architecture operate with equally coherent internal logic without imitating Material surfaces?
Storybook transfer: material-behavior experiments rather than palette imitation.

## Atlassian Design System
URL: https://atlassian.design/
Foundations: https://atlassian.design/foundations
Class: official design system
Why study: foundations, tokens, accessibility, content, spacing, grid, color, typography, iconography, illustration, logos, elevation, borders and radius presented as a connected operating system.
New Westside question: how should theological/origin meanings remain distinct from semantic and component tokens?
Storybook transfer: trace principle → semantic role → component → complete page.

## IBM Design Language
URL: https://www.ibm.com/design/language/
Principles: https://www.ibm.com/design/language/philosophy/principles/
Layout: https://www.ibm.com/design/language/layout/overview/
Class: official brand design language
Why study: unusually valuable bridge between brand philosophy and production design. IBM explicitly frames layout as the place where hierarchy, scale, proportion, contrast, harmony, rhythm and repetition combine.
New Westside question: how can a recognizable point-of-view live in the *way elements come together*, not merely in assets?
Storybook transfer: composition-only tests where the same assets are rearranged according to different governing rules.

## Carbon Design System
URL: https://carbondesignsystem.com/
Class: official product design system
Why study: design system as implementable documentation, code, contribution model and content standard; useful for writing the textbook as an operational resource rather than a manifesto only.
Storybook transfer: component states, coded tokens, documentation-as-product.

## Fluent 2
URL: https://fluent2.microsoft.design/
Class: official design system
Why study: semantic/alias color roles, component/system layers and multi-context application.
Storybook transfer: context/mode experiments rather than raw-palette experiments.

## USWDS
URL: https://designsystem.digital.gov/
Design principles: https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/
Design tokens: https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/
Class: official public design system
Why study: tokens as curated choices that reduce arbitrary design decisions; principles used as an evaluative lens; strong connection between accessibility, usability and implementation.
Storybook transfer: constrained token-scale tests and real-content stress.

## GOV.UK Brand Guidelines
URL: https://brand.design-system.service.gov.uk/
Design System: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/
Class: official brand + product design system
Why study: one brand across many contexts; explicit distinction between brand elements and service components; current guidelines explain how a brand flexes according to audience, content and context.
New Westside question: how should Journal, ONE, Church, Library and Gate vary in expression without fragmenting the identity?
Storybook transfer: one identity across informative → expressive contexts.

## BBC GEL
URL: https://bbc.github.io/gel/
Class: official technical experience language
Why study: framework-agnostic, code-centric guidance that ties patterns to accessibility and implementation.
Storybook transfer: component behavior, accessibility and interaction evidence.

## The Guardian Design System
URL: https://guardian.github.io/theguardian.design/
Alternative presentation: https://design.theguardian.com/
Class: editorial design system
Why study: especially relevant to Westside because it blends brand, editorial and UX thinking; color supports editorial navigation; typography is role-based across headline/body/meta; grids and spacing connect print and digital.
Storybook transfer: editorial hierarchy, section-color metadata, dense content, print/digital continuity.

## W3C WCAG 2.2 / WAI
URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/
What’s new in 2.2: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/
Class: accessibility standard
Why study: objective interaction/reflow/focus constraints. Accessibility must enter Westside visual theory as an expressive constraint, not post-production repair.
Storybook transfer: keyboard focus, focus-not-obscured, reflow, reduced motion, target states.

## Design Tokens Community Group
URL: https://www.designtokens.org/
Stable technical reports: https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/
Format module: https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/format/
Class: community-group technical specification
Why study: stable interoperable token source-of-truth; groups, aliases/references and technical exchange. Use as implementation architecture, not as brand theory.
New Westside question: how to preserve Origin / Theological Meaning above primitive/semantic/component token layers?
Storybook transfer: generated variants consuming named semantic/context tokens rather than raw values.

# C. Mature live brand-guideline systems

## Dropbox Brand Guidelines
URL: https://brand.dropbox.com/
Framework: https://brand.dropbox.com/framework
Logo: https://brand.dropbox.com/logo
Imagery: https://brand.dropbox.com/imagery
Class: official brand bible / living web guideline
Why study: excellent example of a brand guideline as an expressive website, not a dry PDF. Structure includes framework, voice/tone, logo, typography, iconography, color, imagery and motion. The “Tab” and “Planes” show how one generative visual rule can organize layout across contexts.
New Westside question: what is Westside’s equivalent of a few generative rules that remain recognizable even when specific motifs are absent?
Storybook transfer: create one-variable generative-system tests, not Dropbox-like layouts.

## MIT Brand Guide

Color: https://brand.mit.edu/color
Typography: https://brand.mit.edu/typography

Why study: clear brand architecture; core vs expanded palette; color combination principles; typography tied to history and legibility; galleries, do/don’t, templates and applications.

# D. Historic CI / VI manuals and standards — study sequencing, systems thinking and book design

## New York City Transit Authority Graphics Standards Manual (1970)
Reference edition/source page: https://standardsmanual.com/products/nyctacompactedition
Accessible reference listing: https://buddhistuniversity.net/content/reference/graphic-standards-manual_nycta
Class: historic CI/wayfinding manual
Why study: systematic typography, signs, grid/spacing, information hierarchy and consistency across a huge physical network. Also study the *book itself* as a designed teaching artifact: sequencing, diagrams, repeated rule pages, specimen logic.
Caution: reproduction rights apply; learn structure and method, do not reproduce copyrighted pages into Westside publication without permission.
Storybook transfer: grid, hierarchy, signposting, repeated-system discipline.

## U.S. EPA Graphic Standards System (1977)
Official archive text: https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P101HJQ2.TXT
Class: historic federal CI manual
Why study: strong example of a manual explaining why a coherent identity improves public service, consistency and production economy; system-oriented rather than logo-only.
Storybook transfer: system economy, information presentation, disciplined applications.

## NASA historic Graphics Standards tradition
Current official access: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/
Class: historic/current institutional CI
Why study: visual identity across marks, typography, supporting graphics and application constraints; famous example of corporate identity as repeatable system.

## National Park Service / Unigrid tradition
Current official identity guidance: https://home.nps.gov/subjects/hfc/nps-graphic-identity-and-style-guides.htm
Class: public-information / publication system
Why study: brand recognized not by a single decorative style but by recurring structural grammar across publications and environments.

# H. Mandatory output from this registry

Doré must turn this registry into products, not a bookmark list.

For each study batch produce:

**1. Source dossier**
- source architecture map;
- 5–10 governing principles;
- visual/typographic/layout observations;
- teaching/writing methods;
- what is transferable;
- what is rejected.

**2. Manuscript passage**
At least one durable section written in book-quality prose using the cycle:
Observation → Principle → Reason → Rule → Counterexample → Experiment → Evidence → Revision → Transfer.

**3. Storybook visual study**
When source content is visually relevant, build an original experiment testing the learned method with Westside content. It must not copy the source brand surface.

**4. Cross-source synthesis**
After a batch, compare systems: what do mature guides consistently do, where do they disagree, and what distinctive New Westside theory emerges?

**5. Book / Journal transfer**
Record whether the learning belongs in the design textbook, theological reflection, evangelistic argument, Journal art direction, or more than one layer.

## Core anti-copy test

> If its logo, colors, typefaces, illustrations and signature motifs are removed, what reasoning system remains?

Only that remaining reasoning is eligible to enter Doré’s durable learning.
