# New Westside Living Water Homepage — Candidate 01

Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE — visual direction first, not a technology benchmark.

## Correction to the motion programme

P1 is retained only as the Motion Technology Playground. It may continuously test native scroll, CSS scroll timelines, View Transitions, Motion, GSAP/Flip/ScrollTrigger, Lenis, SVG/Canvas/WebGL, depth, masking, object persistence and mobile/reduced-motion implementations. P1 is not a homepage candidate and must not be promoted as Living Water design.

The main design line now starts here.

## Design thesis

The homepage is not a row of cards. The whole page is a Living Water editorial space. Scripture Cinema is the source/current at entry; Journal, reporting, theology, Dawn Library and other editorial worlds become currents within one continuous spatial field.

The design must visibly carry the exploration work before optimization begins.

## Candidate 01 visual obligations

A candidate fails before technical review if these are not visible:

1. Whole-page flow — the viewport feels inside one continuous spatial river, not beside a carousel.
2. Scripture Cinema — the opening has immersive Doré engraving / paper / light-dark / depth behavior and a clear cinematic field.
3. 8:5 as world — each 8:5 is an enterable editorial world/cover, not a component card.
4. Continuity — neighboring worlds overlap, anticipate and recede; movement crosses object boundaries.
5. Temporal hierarchy — reporting can move fast, editorial medium, theology slow, reading settles.
6. Viewport conductor — one current leads while adjacent currents support and distant currents become quiet.
7. Depth — foreground/midground/background, scale, crop, masking, typography and light establish camera-like attention without requiring constant 3D.
8. Confluence — related worlds can meet spatially rather than appear as recommendation cards.
9. Enter / settle / return — entering depth decelerates browsing into reading; return restores river position and orientation.
10. Contemporary editorial identity — shared spatial grammar, independent art direction. The result must not resemble a church-site carousel or generic AI landing page.

## Motion grammar

Website motion can be concentrated into Flow → Focus → Open → Settle → Return, but Candidate 01 is allowed to overbuild first. Reduction happens only after a rich working composition exists.

Scripture Cinema has a separate narrative grammar. It may use 2D engraving → selective spatial/cinematic transition → 2D engraving at book/chapter/salvation-history boundaries. It must remain visually Doré/New Westside rather than becoming generic WebGL spectacle.

## Technology policy

No pre-emptive dependency austerity. Mature animation technology is available because Doré Exploration already removed that threshold. Candidate 01 may use the strongest appropriate combination of CSS scroll-driven animation, sticky, IntersectionObserver, View Transitions, Motion, GSAP/Flip/ScrollTrigger, Lenis, SVG/Canvas/WebGL or other mature primitives.

Technology is evaluated after the visual/spatial hypothesis is working. Then remove duplication, replace unnecessary dependencies, reduce frame work and retain the smallest stack that preserves the design.

## Human-visible learning loop

Exploration principle → visible candidate behavior → human critique → revision → retained design primitive / rejected effect → Doré Design capability.

Every major exploration conclusion used by Candidate 01 must have a visible counterpart. A hidden engineering claim does not count as design progress.

## Quality gate

The internal benchmark remains Red Dot-level digital art direction: not a claim of award affiliation, but a rejection threshold. Early candidates may be incomplete, but they must already show distinctive spatial genes, art direction, attention choreography and a memorable New Westside identity. Rough is acceptable. Ordinary is not.
