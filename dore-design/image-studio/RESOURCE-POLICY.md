# Search and resource policy

Doré may search for references and reusable material, but discovery is not
permission. Every selected item must be verified on its original item page.

## Preferred order

1. Existing project-owned assets.
2. Public-domain or CC0 museum/library collections.
3. CC BY assets with feasible attribution.
4. CC BY-SA only when share-alike is compatible with the intended derivative.
5. Generated original artwork.

Do not use a source marked `unknown`, `editorial use only`, `non-commercial`,
`no derivatives`, or `no known restrictions` as production material without a
specific human rights review.

## Allowlisted discovery sources

| Source | Use | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Openverse | discovery across open collections | verify license again at original provider |
| Wikimedia Commons | scans, engravings, maps, textures | inspect each file page, attribution and share-alike |
| Smithsonian Open Access | CC0 collection material | require CC0 marker; preserve title/creator/source |
| Rijksmuseum Data | public-domain/CC0 collection data | require item rights statement |
| NYPL Digital Collections | prints, maps, manuscripts | enable public-domain filter and verify item record |

## Required manifest fields

`source_url`, `provider`, `creator`, `title`, `date`, `license`,
`license_url`, `rights_verified_at`, `credit_line`, `source_sha256`,
`downloaded_at`, `transform_notes`, and `reviewer`.

Search thumbnails, Pinterest posts, social screenshots, blogs, and commercial
stock previews are reference-only. They may inspire a search for the original,
but cannot enter the asset directory.

CC0 removes copyright restrictions but not necessarily trademark, privacy,
publicity, cultural, or moral-rights concerns. People, sacred objects, community
materials, and institutional marks require an additional context review.
