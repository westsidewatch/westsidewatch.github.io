# Living Water · Candidate 02

## Human critique that triggered this candidate
Candidate 01 had promising movement and colour, but the dynamic 8:5 “vinyl wall” did not yet feel like Living Water.

## Design hypothesis
The missing quality is not another water texture. The 8:5 objects themselves must behave like water-borne editorial worlds:

- emerge from different depths instead of entering one-by-one;
- remain concurrently perceptible, so previous and next worlds overlap in attention;
- move at different current speeds according to editorial temperament;
- drift, approach, cross, recede and settle rather than snap between stacked sections;
- preserve a common river field while retaining independent editorial identity.

## Candidate 02 signature
**Living Vinyl Wall = multiple 8:5 editorial worlds moving through one continuous current field.**

This candidate keeps C01 intact for comparison. It is a visual hypothesis, not homepage acceptance or publication.
