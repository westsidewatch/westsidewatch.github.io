# New Westside Visual Constitution v1.1

Status: CANONICAL
Established: 2026-09-03
Updated: 2026-09-03
Scope: New Westside / Westside Watch permanent visual grammar and convergence core
Authority: canonical convergence core; historical notes, experiments and PDFs are provenance, not substitutes.

## 0. Governing method — Expand first, converge later
**World → Learning → Experiment → Expansion → Mature templates → Comparison → Discernment → Convergence → New Westside.** During EXPANSION, Doré enters materially different traditions without asking whether they already look like Westside. During CONVERGENCE, this core returns as questions and constraints. **White + gold line + large serif ≠ Westside style.**

## 1. World and audience
**Watch / 守望** — attention, discernment, waiting, seeing, witnessing and faithful presence.
**Dawn / 黎明** — **Night → Watching → First Light → Dawn → Day**; temporal and structural before chromatic.
**City / Gate / Road / 城 · 門 · 道路** — Journal, ONE, Living Water, 黎明書局 and The Gate are different places/thresholds, not equal SaaS cards.
**Living Water / 活水** — flow, reflection, life, gathering and renewal.
**Scripture / 聖經** — not “old book + serif + gold + stone”; it contributes time, memory, road, wilderness, river, mountain, gate, feast, lamp, fire, star, witness, gathering, waiting and proclamation.
**People / 人** — real church/community life must be able to enter through documentary material.
**Audience / 受眾** — the future website addresses the **Toronto Chinese-speaking community / 多倫多華人**. This is an audience and communication context, **not a visual-locality motif**. Toronto need not appear as skyline, map or civic aesthetic unless content specifically requires it. **Vaughan is not part of the New Westside visual core and must not be inferred as a design locality.**

## 2. Permanent Visual DNA — 光 · 線 · 紙 · 刻 · 築
**LIGHT · LINE · PAPER · ENGRAVING · ARCHITECTURE / 光 · 線 · 紙 · 刻 · 築**
> **Inherit the grammar, not the material. / 繼承語法，不繼承素材。**
Light ≠ everything gold; line ≠ decorative frames; paper ≠ fake antique paper; engraving ≠ Doré on every page; architecture ≠ literal castles.

## 3. Permanent grammar — 磚 · 垛 · 流
**BRICK · BATTLEMENT · FLOW / 磚 · 垛 · 流.** Brick = content unit. Battlement = layout itself forms the wall. Flow = content rises, yields, retreats and returns to light as editorial judgment changes.

## 4. Black-vinyl-wall → Huarongdao → battlement skyline
The black-vinyl-wall reference contributes many visual items coexisting in one field, not a literal record-store aesthetic. **Editorial Gravity:** Brick size = editorial weight at this moment.

**5:8 = highest editorial-gravity mother proportion:** 5×8 Lead/Cover/Tower → 3×5 Major → 2×3 Feature → 2×2/1×2 Supporting → 1×1 Stream/Archive/Discovery.

**Huarongdao motion:** surrounding units slide and yield instead of hard-refreshing. Content can rise **1×1 → 2×3 → 3×5 → 5×8** and recede **5×8 → 3×5 → 2×3 → archive**. Motion is quiet and consequential.

> **From afar, a city/wall extends across the horizon. Up close, every brick is a piece of information being watched.**

The wall is architecture; mobile is a recomposition, not a shrunk desktop wall.

## 5. Website and Journal
**The website is the city; each Journal issue is an exhibition within the city.** Issue art direction never silently becomes permanent Westside style.

## 6. Surface and material system
**Living Paper** — clean reading paper; base `#FAF9F5`.
**Papyric Texture / Sacred Surface** — **Temple Stone source material × pale-gold overlay → subtle papyric/stone-paper quality**, recalling biblical scroll, inscription, parchment and papyrus without fake-antique effect.
> **Living Paper is the paper color; Papyric Texture is the paper character.**
**Temple Stone is material, not a single brand color** and not a universal background.
**Classical, not retro:** New Classicism combines biblical/material associations and classical typography with contemporary IA, interaction, accessibility and responsive behavior.

## 7. Doré engraving system
Doré is a line, atmosphere, light, scale and material language, not merely pasted illustration. Traces can include cross-hatching, line fragments, cropped figures, silhouettes, line-density changes, engraved light edges and partial city/cloud/water/earth traces.
Intensity: **0 Pure Instrument → Trace → Presence → Image → Immersive**.
Asset families: **LIGHT / SKY / STAR / WATER / CITY / EARTH / TRACE**, with **Full / Fragment / Trace** strengths.
Original Doré works are **Editorial Content**, not anonymous UI texture; keep **Doré as design language** separate from **Doré as curated artist**.

## 8. Typography — classical serif foundation, not a font fetish
The established typography deliberately uses **free serif fonts in both English and Chinese** to create a classical editorial temperament without paid-font dependency.
Current practical pair:
- **Cormorant Garamond** — free English serif; principal display/editorial voice.
- **Noto Serif TC** — free Traditional Chinese serif; principal Chinese editorial voice.
The constitutional rule is **serif + classical temperament + bilingual coherence + practical free licensing**, not permanent loyalty to these exact names.
There is explicit room for future typographic elevation. A premium face such as **Didot** may be studied later, but paid licensing is currently a reason not to make it a dependency. Future replacements must materially improve the system and be tested for bilingual pairing, reading, display, responsive behavior and licensing.
Expansion experiments may use other typographic traditions fully; convergence re-evaluates the serif/classical foundation deliberately.

## 10. Color philosophy
> **紙承載，墨成文，夜守望，金顯現。 / Living Paper carries. Ink Black prints. Watch Night watches. First Light Gold reveals.**
> **Gold is not the sky. Gold is the first light caught by the edge.**
First Light Gold is Highlight & Trace, not a generic primary accent or full-page theme. **Color is metadata, not architecture.** Hierarchy is carried first by scale, position, whitespace, composition and flow.

## 11. Modern Color System architecture
The 2019 PDF 《设计系统中颜色规范的建立方法》 is historical methodology. Its lesson — brand color requires system, validation and tokens — remains useful; its HSB/HSV manual scale generation is not canonical implementation.
1. **Brand primitives** — preserve Sacred Palette provenance.
2. **Perceptual color families** — modern perceptual reasoning, OKLCH preferred where supported.
3. **Semantic tokens** — e.g. `surface.reading`, `surface.sacred`, `surface.watch`, `text.primary`, `light.first`, `identity.water`.
4. **Context/modes** — Reading, Sacred, Watch/Night, Editorial/Journal, accessibility/high-contrast contexts.
5. **Component tokens** — Brick, wall, navigation, scripture, ONE, Gate, Journal, Doré.
6. **Accessibility/gamut gates** — validate real combinations in browsers.
7. **Portable token source** — prefer DTCG-compatible machine-readable authority shared by design tools, Doré Design and HTML/CSS.

## 12. Mature-template standard
A concept sketch is not a mature template. Mature experiments demonstrate complete experience, real imagery/material strategy, typography system, navigation, section rhythm/transitions, content components, hover/focus/interaction, meaningful and reduced motion, footer/end state, long-content stress, real Traditional Chinese + English stress, desktop/tablet/mobile, responsive transformation, accessibility/contrast/keyboard checks, real HTML/browser evidence, and design critique.
**Technical PASS is not design maturity. A screenshot is not a system.**

## 13. Expansion breadth × maturity depth
Doré develops on two independent axes: **Breadth** (many genuinely different design worlds) and **Depth** (selected experiments carried to mature systems). Do not mature only currently preferred directions. The design museum should cover Swiss/International, Bauhaus/Modernism, editorial/newspaper, book/print, image-led documentary, Japanese editorial, New Wave/Postmodern, brutalism, neo-brutalism, maximalism, extreme minimalism, fashion/luxury, museum/cultural, collage/zine/punk, Y2K/early web, skeuomorphic, spatial, cinema, illustration, vernacular/community, data/information, sacred architecture and generative/experimental work.

## 14. Convergence questions
Only after sufficient expansion ask how Watch works spatially/temporally; whether Dawn is color/timing/reveal/rhythm/transition; how City and Living Water coexist; how Light·Line·Paper·Engraving·Architecture become contemporary; how Brick·Battlement·Flow become IA rather than decoration; how the wall stays alive without becoming a dashboard; and what the broader design world has taught us that changes expression of the core.

## 15. Explicit anti-patterns
White+gold+large-serif ≠ Westside; brand gold ≠ gold rules everywhere; Dawn ≠ gold gradient; Watch ≠ dark background; Scripture ≠ fake antique paper; Temple Stone ≠ stone everywhere; Architecture ≠ castle illustration; Doré ≠ engraving on every screen; staggered cards alone ≠ city; Journal issue ≠ permanent website; technical PASS ≠ aesthetic acceptance; Doré self-judgment ≠ user acceptance; experimental candidate ≠ production style; **Toronto audience ≠ Toronto-themed visual decoration; Vaughan ≠ design locality.**

## 16. Authority, acceptance and versioning
Only explicit user aesthetic acceptance can establish `style_acceptance=true` or promote an experiment into New Westside identity. Every revision records what changed and why. Convergence tasks record the constitution version/hash consumed; expansion tasks know it exists but do not use it as a similarity filter.
