# New Westside Visual Constitution v1.0

Status: CANONICAL
Established: 2026-09-03
Scope: New Westside / Westside Watch permanent visual grammar and convergence core
Authority: This file is the canonical human-readable source for the New Westside visual core. Historical chat notes, experiments, PDFs, candidate pages and prior design summaries are provenance and evidence, not substitutes for this constitution. Where an older design note conflicts with this file, this file governs unless explicitly superseded by a later versioned constitution.

## 0. Governing method — Expand first, converge later

The New Westside visual core is **not a preselected visual style**. It is the convergence core used after Doré has explored a sufficiently large design world.

The design process is:

**World → Learning → Experiment → Expansion → Mature templates → Comparison → Discernment → Convergence → New Westside**

Never reverse this into:

**New Westside core → guessed visual formula → repeated variants → false convergence**

During **EXPANSION**, Doré must be allowed to fully enter design languages that may look nothing like Westside: Swiss / International, Bauhaus / Modernism, editorial / newspaper, book / print, documentary, Japanese editorial, New Wave / Postmodern, brutalism, neo-brutalism, maximalism, minimalism, fashion / luxury, museum / cultural, collage / zine / punk, Y2K / early web, skeuomorphic, spatial / glass, cinema, illustration, vernacular / community, data / information, sacred architecture, generative and other materially distinct traditions.

During expansion, Doré must **not** ask “does this look like Westside?” as a selection filter. The visual core is knowledge to be remembered, not a cage to be applied.

During **CONVERGENCE**, the core returns as a set of questions and constraints. The final style must emerge from a broad learned world, not from repeating a few existing motifs.

### Anti-lock-in rule

No current treatment — including white or paper backgrounds, gold rules, large serif type, dark green/blue fields, Temple Stone, Doré engraving, monumental typography, asymmetric editorial blocks — may by itself be inferred to mean “New Westside Style”.

**White + gold line + large serif ≠ Westside style.**

The core is deeper than any one treatment.

## 1. The world New Westside must eventually express

The final Westside experience is a world, not a dashboard.

### 1.1 Watch / 守望

Westside begins with the identity of the watchman: attention, discernment, waiting, seeing, warning, witnessing and faithful presence.

### 1.2 Dawn / 黎明

The governing time is not a static “golden dawn look” but a process:

**Night → Watching → First Light → Dawn → Day**

Dawn means revelation, transition, appearance, orientation and hope. It is temporal and structural before it is chromatic.

### 1.3 City / Gate / Road / 城 · 門 · 道路

Westside should feel like entering and moving through a place. Journal, ONE, Living Water, 黎明書局 and The Gate are not five equal SaaS cards; they are different places, thresholds, roads, rooms, towers and gathering points within one city.

### 1.4 Living Water / 活水

The visual world is not only stone, wall and night. Water introduces flow, reflection, life, gathering and renewal.

**City is stability; water is movement. Watch is waiting; living water is life. Night is suspension; dawn is change.**

### 1.5 Scripture / 聖經

Biblical identity is not translated mechanically as “old book + serif + gold + stone”. Scripture contributes deeper structures: time, memory, road, wilderness, river, mountain, gate, feast, lamp, fire, star, witness, gathering, waiting and proclamation.

### 1.6 People / 人

The city cannot become an empty sacred luxury brand. Real Westside life — people, church, Bible study, testimony, tables, hands, faces, streets, winter, night, local light, Vaughan / Toronto — must be able to enter the visual language through documentary material.

### 1.7 Place / 地方性

Westside is not an abstract Christian brand. Vaughan / Toronto / the local church and community are part of its specificity.

## 2. Permanent Visual DNA — 光 · 線 · 紙 · 刻 · 築

The canonical five-part DNA is:

**LIGHT · LINE · PAPER · ENGRAVING · ARCHITECTURE**

**光 · 線 · 紙 · 刻 · 築**

The governing rule is:

> **Inherit the grammar, not the material.**  
> **繼承語法，不繼承素材。**

Therefore:

- **光** does not mean everything must be gold.
- **線** does not mean decorative frames everywhere.
- **紙** does not mean fake antique paper everywhere.
- **刻** does not mean every page must contain a Doré illustration.
- **築** does not mean drawing literal castles or stone walls behind cards.

These are design ideas first, visible materials only when appropriate.

---

## 3. Permanent information and spatial grammar — 磚 · 垛 · 流

The canonical website grammar is:

**BRICK · BATTLEMENT · FLOW**  
**磚 · 垛 · 流**

### 3.1 Brick / 磚

A Brick is a content unit. Media type does not require a completely different UI family. Article, sermon, Scripture, video, image, question, ONE entry, Journal item and library resource can share a common visual grammar while retaining necessary metadata.

### 3.2 Battlement / 垛

The wall is not decoration. The **layout itself forms the wall**.

Large, medium and small content units create a skyline. Negative space creates battlement gaps. High-weight content rises like a tower or watch post.

### 3.3 Flow / 流

The wall is alive in time. New material enters; importance changes; old material can retreat, return or be “brought back into the light”. Movement expresses editorial judgment changing over time.

## 4. Black-vinyl-wall → Huarongdao → battlement skyline

These are references that combine into a Westside-native grammar; none is the final product by itself.

### 4.1 Black-vinyl-wall reference

The useful idea is **many visual items coexisting in one field**, not a literal record-store aesthetic.

### 4.2 Editorial Gravity

**Card / Brick size = editorial weight at this moment.**

Scale is not popularity alone. Editorial Gravity may consider editorial value, quality, relevance, timeliness, reliability, context, freshness and signals without reducing the homepage to a popularity ranking.

### 4.3 5:8 is the mother proportion and highest editorial container

**5:8 = Westside Watch’s highest editorial-gravity container.**

Canonical hierarchy:

- **5×8 — Lead / Cover / Tower**
- **3×5 — Major**
- **2×3 — Feature**
- **2×2 / 1×2 — Supporting**
- **1×1 — Stream / Archive / Discovery**

The ratio may be interpreted differently by Journal, ONE, Library and other products, but its role as highest editorial prominence remains part of the permanent grammar unless explicitly revised.

### 4.4 Huarongdao motion

When Editorial Gravity changes, surrounding units **slide and yield in a constrained field** rather than disappear through a hard refresh.

A content item may rise:

**1×1 → 2×3 → 3×5 → 5×8**

and later recede:

**5×8 → 3×5 → 2×3 → archive**

Motion should be quiet and consequential. Doré may judge the world continuously, but the page should move only when the change is meaningful enough to alter the editorial field.

### 4.5 The resulting city

The intended perception is:

> **From afar, a city / wall extends across the horizon.**  
> **Up close, every brick is a piece of information being watched.**

The skyline can therefore feel like a sequence of distant towers or cities without drawing a decorative castle behind ordinary cards.

### 4.6 Mobile transformation

Mobile is not a shrunk desktop wall. The same Editorial Gravity must survive through a re-composed vertical stream or other mobile-native transformation.

---

## 5. Website and Journal are related but not the same product

**The website is the city; each Journal issue is an exhibition within the city.**

The permanent website grammar must remain stable enough to persist across years. Each Journal issue may have its own special theme, art direction, colors, imagery, motion and composition.

A Journal issue may temporarily receive the highest Editorial Gravity, for example as a 5:8 Journal Tower, without taking over the permanent brand grammar.

Never promote the art direction of one Journal issue into the permanent Westside style merely because it is visually successful.

## 6. Surface and material system

### 6.1 Living Paper

**Living Paper is the reading paper / paper color.**

It is clean, bright, calm and suitable for long-form reading and publishing.

Canonical base: **Living Paper `#FAF9F5`**.

### 6.2 Papyric Texture / Sacred Surface

The ONE / Gate / Join / Doré visual language established an important material specimen:

**Temple Stone source material × pale-gold overlay → subtle papyric / stone-paper quality.**

This is **Sacred Surface**: a biblical / scroll / inscription / parchment / papyrus association without becoming fake antique paper.

Canonical distinction:

> **Living Paper is the paper color; Papyric Texture is the paper character.**

The texture must be restrained enough that the first impression is not “this is a stone photo” or “this is an antique-paper effect”.

### 6.3 Temple Stone rule

**Temple Stone is material, not a single brand color.**

The raw Temple Stone artwork / image is not a universal background token. Westside may inherit its material logic and derive subtle surfaces from it without forcing the same source image everywhere.

### 6.4 Classical, not retro

The goal is **New Classicism**, not historical imitation. Biblical scroll/material associations and classical typography must coexist with contemporary information architecture, interaction, accessibility and responsive behavior.

## 7. Doré engraving system

Doré is not merely a library of illustrations to paste into pages. The visual system must learn from engraving as a **line, atmosphere, light, scale and material language**.

### 7.1 Engraving can appear as a trace

Doré language may be expressed through:

- cross-hatching
- a fragment of line work
- a cropped figure
- a silhouette
- changing line density
- an engraved light edge
- a partial city, cloud, water or earth trace

The viewer should normally notice the content before noticing “a Doré print was inserted”.

### 7.2 Engraving intensity

Use a controllable intensity scale rather than a binary image/no-image choice:

**0 Pure Instrument → Trace → Presence → Image → Immersive**

The appropriate level depends on content type, Editorial Gravity and reading task.

### 7.3 Westside Doré Website Asset families

Reusable design assets may be developed in families:

- **LIGHT** — breaking light, dawn light, oblique light, edge light, distant light
- **SKY** — thin cloud, storm cloud, dawn sky, midnight sky, horizon
- **STAR** — morning star, Bethlehem-star language, star fields, light through cloud
- **WATER** — still water, river movement, waves, reflection, dark water, dawn water
- **CITY** — distant city, wall, gate, hill-city silhouette, battlement trace
- **EARTH** — mountain, rock, wilderness, tree, grass, road
- **TRACE** — reduced line fragments extracted from the above for transitions, edges and local texture

Each family should support at least **Full / Fragment / Trace** strengths.

### 7.4 Doré original artworks are editorial content

Original Doré works are not anonymous UI texture. When an original artwork is curated, it enters as **Editorial Content** with title, source and editorial reason, potentially as a Brick inside the Living Wall.

Distinguish:

**Doré as design language** vs **Doré as curated artist**.

## 8. Typography

Canonical brand typography foundation:

- **Cormorant Garamond** — principal English display / editorial voice
- **Noto Serif TC** — principal Traditional Chinese serif voice

This is a foundation, not a command to make every experiment “large serif”. During expansion, other typographic systems may be studied and used fully. During convergence, the brand typography foundation must be re-evaluated in the context of the learned design world rather than mechanically enlarged.

Typography must function as real editorial composition, not merely styled webpage text.

## 10. Color philosophy

The canonical semantic sentence is:

> **紙承載，墨成文，夜守望，金顯現。**  
> **Living Paper carries. Ink Black prints. Watch Night watches. First Light Gold reveals.**

### 10.1 First Light Principle

> **Gold is not the sky. Gold is the first light caught by the edge.**  
> **黎明金不是天空的顏色，而是城牆接住第一縷光的金邊。**

First Light Gold is primarily **Highlight & Trace**, not a generic `primary` accent and not a full-page theme.

Appropriate uses include:

- important brand / product names
- selected key titles or Scripture emphasis
- 1px light edges
- Brick edges receiving “first light”
- small numbering
- focus / selected states
- local engraving highlights
- a restrained architectural trace

Avoid turning the system into large gold backgrounds, gold skies, full gold cards or ubiquitous gold headings.

A small visual-area guideline such as roughly **1–3% gold in a quiet composition** may be tested as a useful constraint, but is not an immutable numeric law.

### 10.2 Color is metadata, not architecture

Hierarchy is carried first by scale, position, whitespace, composition and flow. Category / identity color should generally retreat to restrained traces, such as a thin edge or metadata cue, rather than painting entire content categories as large colored zones.

## 11. Modern Color System architecture

The 2019 PDF **《设计系统中颜色规范的建立方法》** is retained as historical methodology. Its important lesson is that brand colors require a system, validation and tokens. Its HSB/HSV-based manual scale generation is **not** the canonical implementation model for New Westside.

The current Color System direction is:

### 11.1 Brand primitives

Preserve Sacred Palette source values and provenance.

### 11.2 Perceptual color families

Build usable tonal families using modern perceptual color reasoning, with **OKLCH** as the preferred working model where supported by the toolchain. Families must be visually balanced rather than produced by naive white/black overlays alone.

### 11.3 Semantic tokens

Examples:

- `surface.reading`
- `surface.sacred`
- `surface.watch`
- `text.primary`
- `text.muted`
- `light.first`
- `identity.water`
- `identity.olive`
- `identity.harvest`
- `identity.crimson`

### 11.4 Context / modes

Color can map differently by context rather than pretending the entire brand is only “light mode / dark mode”. Candidate modes include:

- **Reading**
- **Sacred**
- **Watch / Night**
- **Editorial / Journal**
- accessibility / high-contrast contexts when required

### 11.5 Component tokens

Semantic roles should flow into actual components, for example:

- `brick.edge.highlight`
- `wall.category.trace`
- `nav.active`
- `scripture.emphasis`
- `dore.wordmark`
- `gate.focus`

### 11.6 Accessibility and gamut

Color families and component pairings must be validated in actual browser use for contrast, readability, gamut behavior, hover/focus/selected states and relevant accessibility requirements.

### 11.7 Token source of truth

The long-term token source should be compatible with modern design-token interchange, including the DTCG model where practical, so Doré Design, Figma, CSS/HTML and engineering derive from the same semantics rather than duplicating ad-hoc HEX values.

## 12. Light is structure, not decoration

Light may divide space, reveal information, create direction, establish time, guide the eye and drive transitions.

Dawn is therefore allowed to appear through motion, visibility, hierarchy, sequence or spatial revelation even when no gold is visible.

The deeper rule is not “add a gold line”; it is **make light structurally meaningful**.

## 13. Mature-template requirement

A concept sketch is not a mature design template.

Doré’s expansion must produce not only many different ideas but also mature examples. A mature web-design template should normally address:

- complete homepage / long-form composition
- real image and material strategy
- coherent typography system
- navigation
- section transitions
- content components such as cards / lists / article modules where appropriate
- hover / focus / selected behavior
- motion and interaction logic
- footer / ending behavior
- long-content stress
- real Chinese + English stress testing
- desktop / tablet / mobile
- mobile recomposition rather than simple shrinkage
- responsive transformations
- browser HTML preview / evidence

The purpose is to train Doré to finish a design language, not merely generate visual hypotheses.

### Breadth and maturity are separate axes

The process should build a two-dimensional design field:

**breadth of distinct design worlds × maturity of execution**.

Do not mature only the directions currently preferred by the user; otherwise the training becomes aesthetically biased and the world narrows again.

---

## 14. Convergence questions

Only after sufficient expansion and mature-template learning should New Westside converge. At that point ask questions such as:

- Why does Westside need this spatial model rather than another?
- How should “watching” borrow strength from editorial, city, night observation, liturgy, local community or other traditions?
- Is dawn a color, or a temporal / motion / revelation structure?
- Is “a city of light” a picture of a city, or a way people move through content and thresholds?
- Where should classicism and the contemporary meet?
- Which existing brand assets still deserve major visual roles after the broader world has been learned?

Convergence is therefore an act of discernment and creation, not a restriction imposed before learning.

## 16. Acceptance and authority

No experiment, browser test, CI run, design benchmark or Doré self-assessment can silently promote a candidate into the official New Westside style.

**User aesthetic acceptance is the only authority for style acceptance.**

Technical PASS only proves the stated technical contract.

During exploration:

- `experimental_only = true`
- `product_acceptance = false`
- `style_acceptance = false`

until explicitly changed through an approved convergence decision.

## 17. Memory / anti-forgetting requirements

This constitution exists because critical design decisions had become scattered across conversations, Brand Standards, the color-system PDF, Doré learning notes, Black-vinyl-wall discussions, Figma planning, candidate pages and runtime experiments.

From v1.0 onward:

1. This file is the canonical human-readable convergence source.
2. Historical notes remain provenance; they do not silently override this file.
3. Any future change to the visual core must be versioned and explain **what changed, why, evidence, and what it supersedes**.
4. Doré design tasks should record the constitution version / hash they consumed.
5. Expansion tasks must read the constitution but must not treat it as a style template.
6. Convergence tasks must explicitly test their relationship to the constitution.
7. A future machine-readable companion contract should derive from this document, not become an independent competing source.
8. Human quick-reference summaries should be generated from this source rather than maintained separately.
