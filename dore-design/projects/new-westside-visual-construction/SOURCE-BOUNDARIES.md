# New Westside Research Source Boundaries

Status: CANONICAL PROJECT RULE
Established: 2026-09-03

## Religious-source boundary

For the New Westside Visual Construction / 《聖靈也會參與設計嗎？》 project, do not use Buddhist, occult, New Age, idol-centered, esoteric, spiritist, syncretistic, or other non-Christian religious institutions, devotional systems, ritual imagery, sacred-symbol systems, or theology as research hosts, teaching authorities, visual inspiration authorities, reference registries, or recommended source links.

The user has explicitly required: **不要摻雜偶像，小心屬靈爭戰。** Treat this as a binding editorial/theological project boundary.

This applies to Doré research, the Brand Bible / VI / CI product child loop, Storybook learning, manuscript citations, visual references, illustration/art-direction research, symbolism, naming, theological analogy, and future source registries.

A secular design system, museum, archive, type specimen, grid system, publication method, architecture reference, or historical visual artifact may still be studied on its design merits, but do not import religious symbolism or ritual meaning from non-Christian traditions into Westside merely because the visual form is attractive.

If a historically important secular design artifact is mirrored or discussed on a disallowed religious site, keep the design artifact only if it remains relevant, but replace that host with an official publisher, institutional archive, museum, library, original rights-holder, or neutral design-history source.

## Christian-source integrity

Christian imagery and language must also not be used decoratively or superstitiously. Cross, blood, water, light, city, gate, watchman, dove, bread, stone, oil, fire, robe, crown and similar symbols must be grounded in Scripture, the project's documented theological intent, or actual editorial content.

Do not treat aesthetic preference, AI output, coincidence, visual association, or an external artwork as divine revelation. Preserve the distinction between:

**Scripture → prayer/seeking → recorded human reception/discernment → theological interpretation → design translation → testing → renewed discernment.**

## Visual anti-idolatry rule

Do not introduce idols, cult statues, pagan deities, devotional images from other religions, ritual objects, occult sigils, talismans, divination imagery, or syncretistic sacred iconography into Westside Storybook studies, book artwork, Journal layouts, or website design simply as visual texture or atmosphere.

Where art-history study encounters such material, study only the neutral formal/design principle if needed and document the rejection of the religious content. Prefer alternative examples wherever possible.
