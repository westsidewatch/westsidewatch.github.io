# 《十字架的影子》〈義人的血〉C1 Grammar Findings v1

Status: PROJECT GRAMMAR / TRANSFER CANDIDATES — NOT UNIVERSAL
Project: 《十字架的影子》
Comparison lineage: A baseline → B1/B2 Grammar Core transfer → C1 Post-Huang experiment
Purpose: record the C1 failure mode without contaminating transferable Grammar with project identity.

## 1. Project-local findings

C1 remained materially controlled through the Abel / land / Deuteronomy material. Two short passages are admitted as legitimate for different reasons:

- 「從一具屍體到一座城，要先量一段路。」 — earned compression. The preceding legal material earns the compression and the sentence opens scale rather than closing meaning.
- 「許多年以後，另一個義人的血流在聖殿裡。」 — structural break. Time, scene, person and narrative unit change; the break has contextual work even without exceptional rhetorical brilliance.

The first clear WATCH point is near:

- 「他的兒子沒有被葬在列王的墳墓裡。」

The sentence is not automatically rejected. It uses an established father/son and burial contrast and may be locally earned. Its importance is diagnostic: after this point C1 begins to acquire its own successful rhetorical device as a local style pattern. Later short landings, isolated contrasts and climax beats accelerate until the Christological convergence is increasingly shaped by model rhetoric rather than the whole-project Grammar.

B1 and B2 did not show this accelerating AI-style trajectory. Therefore C1 is not evidence that long prose inevitably deteriorates. It is evidence of an authority drift introduced or exposed under the Post-Huang writing state, especially when genuine emotional/theological salience rises.

Project rule: emotional and theological pressure must remain. Do not flatten Zechariah, the murdered righteous, sacrificial blood, Hebrews or the Cross in order to avoid AI rhetoric. The material is allowed to become painful and intense. What is denied is automatic rhetorical escalation.

## 2. Transferable candidates — identity isolated

The following candidates contain no Cross-specific thesis, motif, vocabulary, cadence, theology or signature sentence.

### A. Persistent Grammar State

Grammar is not a one-time preflight. It remains a recoverable writing state throughout generation.

Control loop:

`Grammar State → Material → Salience Detection → Expression Candidate → Grammar Return → Continue`

At a high-salience point the model must return to Grammar before expression becomes local style.

### B. Salience is signal, not style instruction

The model's ability to detect that a moment is important, moving, surprising, beautiful, tragic or structurally powerful must be preserved. Salience detection has discovery authority, not style authority.

A high-salience judgment may result in a short sentence, a longer scene, slower factual residence, reordered material, one added detail, or no rhetorical increase at all. The judgment `this matters` does not itself authorize `write more dramatically`.

### C. Rhetorical permission does not propagate

A short sentence, contrast, isolated paragraph, repetition, parallelism or compression that is admitted once does not increase the authority of the same device later.

`earned once ≠ earned again`

Every recurrence must be re-admitted by the current material and whole-text Grammar.

### D. Local success must not become automatic local grammar

A successful expression can alter the model's recent-context distribution. The model may begin imitating its own immediately preceding success.

Failure trajectory:

`earned device → local pattern acquisition → self-imitation → frequency acceleration → stylistic takeover`

The writing system must detect the trajectory, not merely judge each sentence in isolation. Several individually defensible sentences can still form a deteriorating whole.

### E. Emotional pressure and rhetorical escalation have separate authority

Material pressure may rise sharply. Emotional, intellectual or theological importance does not automatically authorize shorter sentences, more whitespace, denser parallelism, repetition or landing sentences.

`material/emotional pressure ≠ rhetorical escalation permission`

The model must preserve its sensitivity to emotional peaks while returning expression control to Grammar.

### F. Restore normal state after an admitted strong move

After an earned high-voltage sentence or structural break, generation returns to the normal Grammar state. A successful strong move must not become the new local baseline.

## 3. Trajectory admission candidate

Future acceptance should inspect prose temporally, not only sentence-by-sentence or paragraph-by-paragraph.

Watch for:

`device occurrence → recurrence → frequency acceleration → self-imitation → stylistic takeover`

Especially trigger Grammar Return when:

1. emotional / conceptual salience rises sharply;
2. a strong sentence has just been admitted;
3. the same rhetorical device is about to recur;
4. the text approaches a known thesis or climax and the model can predict the intended conclusion.

This is a candidate mechanism, not a lexical blacklist and not a ban on short sentences or strong writing.

## 4. Promotion boundary

Keep two layers isolated:

1. Project Grammar — may contain 《十字架的影子》 examples, theological movement and local acceptance evidence.
2. Transferable candidates — mechanism only; no project identity.

Promotion remains:

`project discovery → candidate extraction → heterogeneous validation → promote`

Do not promote to Universal Grammar from C1 alone. 《天國語言極簡史》 has independently shown a related `AI style increases as generation continues` failure and can serve as heterogeneous validation, but its project Grammar and identity remain separate.

## 5. Next writing acceptance

Write a new 〈義人的血〉 version from the same fixed material field. Do not mechanically merge short paragraphs or suppress salience. Preserve legitimate earned compression and structural breaks. The principal test is whether Grammar remains recoverable after the first high-salience point and through the Christological convergence, without flattening the material.
