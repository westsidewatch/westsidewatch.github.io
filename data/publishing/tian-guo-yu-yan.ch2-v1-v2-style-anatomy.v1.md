# 《天國語言極簡史》第二章 v1 / v2 文體解剖 v1

Status: WRITING TRAINING / COMPARATIVE ACCEPTANCE

## 0. Core rule

> **Narrator pressure must be earned by accumulated material. Facts carry the passage by default; authorial pressure enters only where the book's own inner action requires it.**

Chapter 2 inner action: **嘆息 / 用力**.

## 1. Material carries thought

Prefer `specific object → human action → next specific object` over `specific object → abstract conclusion`.

Brick-world model: `取土 → 加水 → 模具 → 曬 → 燒 → 搬 → 吃飯`. The verbs perform effort. Scale can move from monument to worker to food without announcing a camera move.

Strong sentences are not banned; scheduled strong sentences are. A compressed sentence is admitted only after enough concrete material has earned it, and it must not be explained afterward.

## 2. Submerged recurrence

For an underground-waterway motif:
- first occurrence: material only;
- second occurrence: altered material;
- later crossing: juxtaposition may become visible;
- theological naming waits until accumulated returns require it.

The author may know the waterway before the reader. The prose must not put up a sign naming it.

## 3. AI-trace taxonomy

1. **表演式發現** — `等等 / 有意思的是 / 更驚人的是 / 注意這裡`. HARD BAN.
2. **密集落地句** — every paragraph receives a punch-line. Suppress.
3. **自動金句** — compression generated because a paragraph seems to require quotability. Suppress unless earned.
4. **解釋回聲** — narrator names a motif immediately after juxtaposing it. Delay.
5. **假懸念** — question or withheld answer used only to keep reader attention. Ban unless the research problem is real.
6. **機械反轉句** — repetitive `不是A，而是B`. Frequency gate.
7. **摘要式人物** — major emotional events compressed into synopsis while minor facts expand. Correct through scene magnification.
8. **小論文封口** — every digression receives a concluding thesis. Replace with re-entry.
9. **研究主持人語氣 / research-host narration** — narrator interrupts the world to announce what should now be investigated, noticed or followed. Examples rejected in live Chapter 2 acceptance: `其中「以力」尤其值得追`、`再往古代地名裡走`、`這件事值得畫一張地圖`、`問題自然出現了`、`可惜事情沒有這麼省事`、`這個讀法很有意思`、`這大概是……最容易被遮住的一句話`. These are not merely bad phrases; they reveal a narrator standing outside the novel and hosting a research programme. **HARD STRUCTURAL BAN.** Let one material object encounter the next without announcing the investigation.
10. **替讀者宣布價值** — `值得追 / 值得注意 / 很有意思 / 很重要 / 很奇妙 / 很難不注意`. Do not tell readers what deserves attention when arrangement can make them attend.
11. **導遊式尺度切換** — `我們去看 / 再往……走 / 把目光轉向 / 先來看 / 接下來看`. Move scale through factual adjacency.
12. **問題報幕** — narrator explicitly announces the question immediately before supplying the evidence. If the evidence can make the reader form the question, remove the announcement.
13. **版式代替壓力** — one-sentence paragraphs, stacked short lines, isolated quotations and blank-space emphasis used to manufacture weight. Formal book prose uses natural Chinese publishing paragraphs. Short sentences live inside paragraph rhythm unless exceptionally earned.

Live acceptance finding: the Chapter 2 draft remained viable through the material sequence of plain → mud → brick → ration → `來吧` → city/tower → Nimrod. It began to collapse at `其中「以力」尤其值得追`, where the narrator stopped inhabiting the world and became a research host. Future reconstruction should use the pre-collapse passage as a rhythm reference, not copy it mechanically.

## 4. Positive house grammar

### A. Material carries thought
Use brick, mud, bitumen, ration, wall, hand, body, road, breath and wind before abstract nouns when material can perform the thought.

### B. Earned compression
One short strong sentence may remain when factual accumulation earns it. Do not explain it afterward.

### C. Selective magnification
When the chapter's inner action changes, slow the scene rather than announce its importance.

### D. Scale without signposting
Move among person / object / city / century / Scripture by factual adjacency, never camera-direction language.

### E. Submerged recurrence
Motifs return before they are named.

### F. Curiosity before utility
Research may live before its theological use becomes visible.

### G. Re-entry, not mini-conclusion
A digression flows back into host prose rather than closing as an essay.

### H. Narrator pressure is scarce
Interpretation is a scarce resource. Spend it only after material cannot carry the necessary pressure alone.

### I. Human irregularity
Paragraph length and sentence pressure follow material, not a polished alternation template.

### J. Reader discovers without being congratulated
Never announce that a connection is clever, surprising, beautiful or important.

### K. Research itself is plot
Do not split `story in正文 / research in footnote`. A word, place, distance, tablet, map, house or later reading can generate the next narrative event. The narrator does not announce the research; the evidence enters the room.

### L. Sources may become voices
A translator's note, rabbi, archaeologist, historian, lexicographer or clay tablet may briefly speak inside the narrative. This prevents the omniscient narrator from claiming every discovery while preserving novel rhythm. Provenance should be natural rather than thesis-style boundary signage.

### M. Foreground / QA division
正文 lets relations happen. QA may later name mature relations that would damage novel rhythm if explicitly explained: name/name, mouth/mouth, pride/obedience, justice/mercy. **正文埋線，QA 顯線。**

## 5. Cross-project transfer

Transfer to other Doré writing projects:
- research-host narration ban;
- value-announcement ban;
- guide-like scale transition ban;
- layout-as-pressure diagnostic;
- material-carries-thought;
- research-as-plot;
- source-as-voice;
- reader-discovery without congratulation.

Do NOT transfer project identity: Babel/Nimrod/name/mouth motifs, chapter inner actions, author thesis, theological conclusions or signature cadence.
