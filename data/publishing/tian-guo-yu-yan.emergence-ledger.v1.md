## Survey rule

Chapters 1–2 are comparatively mature first-draft / authorial substrate. Survey means **discover before redesign**. Do not expand merely because a possible motif exists. Preserve strong scenes and syntax. Mark the difference between:

- `NATIVE` — already materially present in canonical manuscript;
- `EMERGENT` — latent connection strongly supported by existing manuscript;
- `RESEARCH` — promising but requires textual/historical verification;
- `AUTHORIAL` — theological/literary construction belonging to author interpretation;
- `RISK` — current prose may overstate evidence and must be checked before publication.

No word-count target.

## Chapter 1 — 要有光

Compressed theme: **話語**
Inner action: **聽見**
Maturity: comparatively high; preserve source scenes.

### C1-01 — Canaan wind / Isaac tent

Status: `NATIVE / FIRST-CLASS WATERWAY`
Origin: opening sentence and repeated wind after Isaac's blessing.

Observed manuscript function:
- wind exists before theological explanation;
- surrounds the irreversible blessing scene;
- returns after silence and Esau's crying;
- already behaves as atmospheric bass line rather than symbol label.

Waterway:
`迦南風 → ruach / rachaph → waiting → Pentecost wind → final wind still blowing → eagle opens wings`

Action: preserve opening and recurrence. Do not explain Spirit at first appearance.

### C1-02 — Isaac hears the wrong son / hearing position

Status: `NATIVE → EMERGENT`
Origin: Isaac cannot see, touches disguise, hears voice, later hears real Esau and trembles.

Existing line:
`聲音辨認 → 話語不可收回 → silence`

Emergent long line:
`以撒聽錯 → Eden "神豈是真說" → Adam hears God's voice and fears → Babel cannot understand → Pentecost hears → Corinth cannot understand → interpretation / edification`

Research exit:
- Genesis 27:22 voice/hands wording;
- Genesis 27:39 Hebrew syntactic ambiguity and translation history.

Admission reason: the chapter's inner action is `聽見`; ambiguity can turn the Isaac scene from merely a davar illustration into the first human-language hearing problem.

### C1-03 — davar weight

Status: `NATIVE / LOAD-BEARING`
Origin: Isaac blessing → davar → Psalms → creation.

Existing strength:
- strong scene-to-word transition;
- `話語還是重的` is a load-bearing author line;
- lamp-not-searchlight passage already gives davar temporal behavior.

Risk / research:
- verify frequency and lexical claims;
- avoid equating word and created object as a simple dictionary definition;
- distinguish semantic range from theological construction.

Waterways:
`話的重量`, `燈`, `說話→現實`, later `五句/萬句` as possible distant echo.

### C1-04 — amar / davar distinction

Status: `NATIVE / RESEARCH / RISK`

Manuscript already correctly notices Genesis 1:3 uses `amar`, not `davar`, and attempts to distinguish speaking action from effective word.

Need:
- rigorous lexical/theological verification;
- do not assert `all God's amar becomes davar` as lexical fact if it is author synthesis;
- preserve the useful reader question: what is the relation between speaking and reality?

Waterway:
`God speaks → serpent questions what God said → Jesus temptation → human reception of God's word`.

### C1-05 — rachaph / incubation / silence

Status: `NATIVE / FIRST-CLASS WATERWAY`

Existing manuscript already contains:
- bird/eagle imagery;
- silence before `要有光`;
- waiting as active incubation;
- explicit bridge to Acts 1 waiting;
- `神一直在` language.

This is not a new motif to insert. It is already structural substrate.

Research:
- lexical range and three-occurrence claim;
- Deut 32:11 relation;
- bird incubation metaphor must be distinguished from lexical definition;
- later research Isaiah 40:31 and actual eagle soaring/updraft behavior before final ending.

### C1-06 — `甚好` suspended over history

Status: `NATIVE / FIRST-CLASS WATERWAY`

Existing manuscript explicitly says `甚好` remains suspended over history and awaits realization in Incarnation / Spirit.

Waterway:
`創造 → 甚好 → fall/Babel → Christ → Spirit → new creation`.

This establishes Chapter 8 from Chapter 1. Preserve. Research the theological precision of saying divine evaluation is not withdrawn.

### C1-07 — Adam naming / names

Status: `NATIVE → EMERGENT`

Existing manuscript already makes naming central: animals, woman, Abram/Abraham, Jacob/Israel.

Potential waterway:
`God grants/names → human names creation → Babel "make a name for ourselves" → hero/kingship/name against death → Jesus' name → renewed identity/new creation`.

Research:
- ancient Hebrew naming conventions;
- avoid absolute `name = essence` simplification;
- do not use false Saul→Paul renaming trope.

### C1-08 — serpent speech / `誰的話是真的？`

Status: `NATIVE / LOAD-BEARING → EMERGENT MASTER QUESTION`

Existing manuscript already begins from `神豈是真說`, amar/davar, human evaluation replacing divine word.

Master question now visible:
**誰的話是真的？**

Long line:
`God speaks → serpent questions/deceives → human self-evaluation → Nimrod speech/snare → Babel mobilizing speech → prophets → Word incarnate → Spirit opens human mouths → truthful/new-created speech`.

Risk:
current sentence `蛇說的那些話，嚴格說來，都不是謊言` requires careful textual/theological review; serpent's speech includes deceptive distortion and must not be flattened into factual truth.

### C1-09 — hands / clothing

Status: `NATIVE / AUTHORIAL / RISK`

Existing manuscript writes God `用手` making clothing. Genesis 3:21 says God made garments; it does not explicitly say `with his hands`.

Potential literary waterway:
`Isaac reaches hand and is deceived → Eve reaches/takes → God clothes humans → Incarnation gives the Word actual human hands → Jesus touches / breaks bread / washes feet → cross`.

Rule: if retained, label `用手` as literary embodiment, never false textual quotation.

### C1-10 — Ayekah / sigh / God seeks humans

Status: `NATIVE / MAJOR CROSSING`

Existing manuscript already calls `你在哪裡？` a sigh and extends God's seeking through Elijah/Jonah/human hiding.

Crosses:
- Chapter 2 `嘆息`;
- title resolution `神一直都很近`;
- hearing-position line: same divine presence, human hears differently after hiding.

Research:
- Ayekah/Eichah relation must be philologically checked; literary echo is not automatically etymological identity;
- `first sigh` is authorial/literary, not lexical fact.

### C1-11 — God did not move; humans hid

Status: `NATIVE → TITLE-LEVEL EMERGENCE`

Existing manuscript already states effectively: God remains in the garden; humans hide; `不是神改變了，是他們改變了`.

This is one of the strongest native foundations for final Chapter 9 theme:
**神很遠嗎？神一直都很近。**

Do not over-explain here. Let Chapter 9 reveal that the answer was present in Chapter 1.

## Chapter 2 — 噢，那座塔

Compressed theme: **嘆息**
Inner action: **用力**
Maturity: comparatively high, but several factual/theological claims require research hardening.

### C2-01 — Nimrod as hero before villain

Status: `NATIVE → HIGH-VALUE RECONSTRUCTION`

Existing manuscript already positions Nimrod as mighty hunter, hero, rebel and kingdom founder. The new construction rule is to let his power become genuinely attractive/impressive before judgment.

Research queue:
- Genesis 10:8–12 Hebrew;
- `gibbōr`, `ṣayid`, `lip̄nê YHWH`;
- ancient Jewish interpretations;
- royal hunt / kingship / imperial imagery;
- avoid deriving Nimrod straightforwardly from `marad` unless philologically supportable.

RISK: current manuscript says Hebrews `gave him this name/root marad` and `名字叫叛逆`. This needs serious etymological verification before publication.

### C2-02 — `用口獵人`

Status: `EMERGENT / RESEARCH / FIRST-CLASS CANDIDATE`

Not yet adequately present in canonical prose, but it arises naturally from the existing Nimrod hunter + language architecture.

Research:
- Rashi on Gen 10:9 and Genesis Rabbah tradition (`ensnared minds with his mouth`);
- Ralbag/Ramban/Targum traditions and disagreement;
- distinguish biblical lexical text from later interpretation.

Potential waterway:
`serpent speech → Nimrod hunts/ensnares through speech → Babel "come" → mouth/lips as snare → deception → Jesus calls → Spirit returns speech`.

### C2-03 — hunter → snare → fisher

Status: `EMERGENT / AUTHORIAL / RESEARCH`

Core inversion:
`從獵人到得魚，從人口說謊到神口說話。`

Potential line:
`Nimrod captures people into his kingdom/name → Jesus calls fishermen to gather people into God's kingdom → John 21: human effort all night / Jesus speaks / net fills`.

Need:
- textual research on Matt 4:19 and John 21;
- do not force lexical equivalence between hunting and fishing;
- use as redemptive literary/theological transformation only if the narrative earns it.

### C2-04 — Gilgamesh / heroic melancholy / death

Status: `NATIVE / HIGH-VALUE EXPANSION`

Existing manuscript already contains Gilgamesh, Enkidu, mortality, immortality quest, plant/serpent, return to Uruk wall.

The current paragraph compresses too much of a potentially major world.

Expansion exits:
- Uruk and city wall;
- tablet/story transmission;
- Enkidu relationship and death;
- journey to Utnapishtim;
- flood tradition;
- plant and serpent;
- return to city;
- ancient hero's confrontation with mortality.

RISK:
- `史詩以他看著城牆的那個沉默結束` needs textual verification and likely refinement;
- `parallel to Nimrod` must not imply identity;
- verify chronology and `oldest literature` wording.

Inner-action value: extremely high. It makes `用力 → 死亡 → 嘆息` experiential rather than doctrinal.

### C2-05 — city / wall / tower / name

Status: `NATIVE / FIRST-CLASS WATERWAY`

Existing manuscript already joins hero, wall, tower, name and final emptiness.

Long line:
`Cain builds city → Nimrod kingdom/cities → Uruk wall → Babel city/tower/name → human attempts permanence → later Jesus' world/cities → Ephesus/Corinth → God's city/new creation`.

Research should open material civilization rather than merely add facts: brick, bitumen, labor, ziggurat, kingship, writing, urban attraction, monument/name/memory.

### C2-06 — `來吧` / collective speech becomes action

Status: `EMERGENT / HIGH-VALUE`

Babel's speech can become the first major demonstration after Eden that human language still has powerful social effects even after losing alignment with divine truth.

Important theological correction:
Human language after the fall/Babel is not simply `powerless`. Lies, commands, slogans and collective speech can create social realities. What is lost is truthful alignment with God's creative reality, not all influence.

Waterway:
`蛇的問句 → Nimrod mouth → Babel "來吧" → Jesus "來跟從我" → Spirit-governed human speech`.

Research exact Hebrew wording and narrative repetitions before prose construction.

### C2-07 — Cain → Lamech → Nimrod / violence becomes organized

Status: `NATIVE / STRONG`

Existing manuscript already builds a generational escalation:
`Cain kills/hides → Lamech sings violence → Nimrod organized power → tower`.

This is structurally valuable but must be tested against Genesis chronology/genealogical relationships; do not imply a simple direct genealogical line if the text does not support it.

The `song` matters because violence becomes language, art and boast—not only action. This intersects the mouth/speech waterway.

### C2-08 — God's sigh / human sigh

Status: `NATIVE → CHAPTER INNER ACTION`

Existing manuscript already uses Gen 6:6 `nacham` and transitions Chapter 3 with `神的嘆息，人的嘆息`.

This proves `嘆息` is not an externally imposed new theme; it is already native manuscript material.

Research:
- semantic range of `nacham`;
- distinguish regret/grief/comfort by stem/context;
- avoid dictionary compression `nacham = sigh` without philological support.

Long line:
`God seeks Ayekah → God grieves → human hero sighs before death → waiting → Romans 8 groanings too deep for words → Spirit / hope`.

Writing rule: Chapter 2 should not name the later Spirit resolution prematurely.

### C2-09 — Babel pit / open sky

Status: `NATIVE / MAJOR ENDING IMAGE`

Existing ending is strong:
`Etemenanki pit → human greatest dream now empty → pit open to sky → same sky over Noah/Abraham/Pentecost → pit empty, sky not empty → language from God has not stopped`.

This is already a title-level precursor to `神一直都很近`.

Potential issue: current final explanatory sentences may resolve Chapter 2 too quickly relative to the newly established `嘆息` inner action. During reconstruction, test whether `天空一直都在` can remain while postponing explicit salvation explanation.

Research:
- Etemenanki archaeological dimensions/location/current condition;
- avoid false GPS precision or tourism claims without sources.

### C2-10 — upward / downward

Status: `NATIVE → EMERGENT MASTER SPATIAL LINE`

Babel: humans build upward / seek heaven/name.
Later inversion:
`humanity goes up → YHWH comes down to see → Incarnation comes down/Word dwells → Spirit descends → God dwells within`.

This line is central to Chapter 4 `降臨 / 靠近` and final title resolution.

Do not make simplistic cosmological claims; preserve as narrative spatial grammar.

### C2-11 — satan / obstruction / road

Status: `RESEARCH ONLY — NOT YET ADMITTED TO PROSE`

Research Numbers 22:22 `לְשָׂטָן לוֹ` and broader Hebrew corpus. Verify common-noun role `adversary/opponent` and occurrence history before any `first occurrence` claim.

Potential crossing:
`snare → obstruction → wrong road → apparent opposition as rescue → follow Jesus → freedom`.

Admission depends on whether research produces a genuine connection rather than a clever lexical detour.

### C2-12 — flood / 1 Peter return

Status: `RESEARCH ONLY — LATER CROSS-CHAPTER CANDIDATE`

Chapter 2's flood/ancient world may later return through 1 Peter 3:18–22.

Must distinguish major interpretations of `ἐκήρυξεν`, imprisoned spirits, Noah-era reference and 1 Peter 4 gospel-to-dead language. Do not assert one contested interpretation as explicit text.

Potential structural value: an apparently closed ancient age reappears inside the New Testament Christological horizon.

## First field-survey findings

1. **The architecture is already inside the manuscript.** Wind, hearing, hiding, sigh, naming, human speech, `甚好`, waiting, city/name and open sky are not post-hoc inventions.
2. Chapter 1's newly assigned inner action `聽見` is strongly supported by the existing Isaac/Adam/serpent material.
3. Chapter 2's newly assigned theme `嘆息` is also native: the manuscript already contains divine grief, heroic mortality and the explicit Chapter 3 bridge `神的嘆息，人的嘆息`.
4. The most important new discovery requiring research is not a new topic but a **connection among existing topics**: `serpent speech → Nimrod hunter/speech tradition → Babel collective speech → snare → Jesus' call / fisher → Spirit-returned mouth`.
5. The strongest title-level native evidence already exists in both chapters: Chapter 1 `不是神改變了，是他們改變了`; Chapter 2 `那個坑，空著。那個天空，沒有空著.` These should be allowed to return in Chapter 9 as discoveries, not repeated slogans.
6. The mature opening chapters should therefore receive **selective excavation and factual hardening**, not wholesale rewrite.
