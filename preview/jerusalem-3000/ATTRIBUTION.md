## Terrain and modern vector context

- **Copernicus DEM GLO-30** — preferred terrain authority. Copernicus Data Space documents it as a global 30 m DSM in WGS84 geographic coordinates; current GLO-30 access requires CCM registration.
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-90** — fallback terrain authority when GLO-30 access is unavailable.
- **OpenStreetMap** — modern vector reference only. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

No Google terrain, Google 3D Tiles, or other proprietary map geometry may be copied/traced into the canonical reconstruction without an explicit compatible licence.
