# B31 — readable major-landmark shells

## What the blockiness came from

Several visible landmarks still used solid wing or tower bodies even after their surrounding courts improved. Detail added to a nearby temple did not fix these masses. This package prioritises real interiors, openings and roof profiles within existing envelopes, not generic decoration or arbitrary resizing.

| Landmark group | Previous issue | This package | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| David/Solomon royal courtyard hypotheses | Solid rectangular wings around open yard | Hollow rooms, roof slabs, three real courtyard-facing openings per wing, supported floors | Wing plan/door proportions/stone-timber profile authored; not recovered palaces |
| Independent Persian/Hasmonean sanctuary halls | Unbroken long upper walls | Five actual high openings per side; independent era profiles retained | Count, sill, width/height and lighting reconstruction authored; no excavated facade claim |
| Antonia four towers | Solid tall cubes with flat caps | Hollow tower shells, real narrow openings, recessed roof terraces, low parapets/crenels | Military silhouette is illustrative; not a verified Roman elevation or merlon count |
| First temple / Herodian sanctuary | Previously opened shells and tiered court ensembles | Existing geometry retained | Rear first-temple tier masses, complete interiors and precinct registration remain unfinished |
| Later major religious cores | Existing multi-part church/shrine/mosque models | No generic reskin in this package | Individually audit source-specific plans before replacing any remaining schematic masses |

This is a first pass on the most obvious selected masses, **not certification that all major landmarks or all18eras are now complete**. Generic residential models remain a separate task. Missing landmark candidates are not invented to make the skyline busy.

## Geometry and source distinction

Existing literary/context sources remain: [Kings7](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1kings/7) for royal building context, early rebuilding/rededication source records, and [JosephusWar5.238–247](https://lexundria.com/j_bj/5.238-5.247/wst) for Antonia's ensemble/four towers. These do not supply the newly authored facade/window/crenellation designs. No new ancient facade is claimed verified from them.

Royal wings retain their original positions, footprint and6/7/8m roof-height budgets. Walls0.4m, roof slab0.25m,2m-wide/3.5m-high openings and room subdivision are authored. Each wing uses a full original-DEM checked display datum; floor support faces follow original grid/triangle crossings. Main courtyard access remains clear. No global terrain flattening or enlarged housing reservation.

Early halls retain their independent30×15×12m envelopes and existing roof budget. Each side wall is split into sill/piers/head, giving five real1.2×1.4m openings with authored sill at68% of hall height. The Persian profile does not acquire Hasmonean rooms or Herodian dimensions.

Antonia retains22m body/24m cap tower footprints and the existing selected50/70cubit height convention. Each tower has a floor,1.2m walls, three0.7m slits per face in an authored upper band and a recessed roof. Parapet/crenel geometry stays inside the inherited total tower-height budget; southeast tower remains taller. Count/profile of crenels and all openings are authored, not sourced measurements. This does not resolve the fortress's exact footprint, ancient rock height or temple descent.
