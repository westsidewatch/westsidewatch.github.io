## Boundaries and remaining work

The historical inner-court step relationships are NOT introduced in this package: existing shared display datum remains. Fifteen semicircular steps, Nicanor gate identity, service chambers, Soreg/Hel, laver, north slaughter band and complete colonnades still require the common plan/section. The new closed chamber outlines are not roofs and do not designate a whole precinct as complete. Sanctuary interiors are still solid massing. The first-temple altar remains an explicitly authored placeholder; Chronicles/Kings comparison unresolved. Bethesda remains a separate unbuilt city interface.

All parts inherit the existing temple object IDs, evidence filters, construction/destruction and reversal. They do not assert physical survival between separate temple periods. The first-temple datum envelope includes the water ensemble; terrain itself is unchanged. No new model downloads or text API calls at runtime. Each vessel uses one16-segment lathed mesh with an open top, thick wall and floor; eleven vessel meshes replace separate ring/floor draw calls. Detailed animal/sculptural meshes are deferred.
