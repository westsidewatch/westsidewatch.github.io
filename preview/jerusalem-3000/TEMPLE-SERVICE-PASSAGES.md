# B27 — hollow side chambers and service passages

This package replaces selected solid display masses with inspectable space. It does not complete vertical circulation, certify ancient plans, change whole-city scale or assert surviving fabric between periods.

## First temple

The six north/south side-storey masses become actual floor/roof/outer-wall/end-wall envelopes beside the existing main wall. The inherited outside widths5/6/7cubits and storey heights5cubits remain; these are **outside display envelopes**, not certified clear room dimensions. Slabs0.15m and walls0.18m are authored. Existing rear tier masses remain unresolved solid envelopes, so this is not a continuous U-shaped chamber reconstruction.

[1Kings6:5–10](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1kings/6) describes surrounding chambers, increasing storey widths and an entrance/ascent. An authored1m south ground-storey door is now physically open; exact position, lintel and width are not supplied by that text. No additional upper-floor external doors or speculative staircase is inserted. Upper-tier access, wall setbacks and the conflict between nominal clear textual widths and retained exterior budgets remain explicitly unaccepted. The roof and high windows retain their previous envelopes. A section now exposes cavities rather than cutting through solid blocks.

## Early Second Temple

The selected Hasmonean profile's three priests' chambers on each side now communicate through aligned internal doorways as well as the previously built outward entrances. Both halves of shared dividing walls receive matching openings, so no duplicate wall can secretly block circulation. Door count, direction and1.2×2.2m dimensions are authored; [1Maccabees4:57](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1maccabees/4) names repaired chambers/doors but does not establish this layout. Persian geometry remains independent and receives none of these Hasmonean rooms or doors. Roofs, foundations and existing common display datum stay joined.

## Herodian sanctuary

The two outer5cubit bands formerly filled by solid18cubit-high masses now contain a floor, outer wall and west end wall. Their existing8cubit roof caps, upper heights and70cubit body boundary stay unchanged. At each east end an authored3cubit-wide portal through the porch rear-wing wall connects the floor-level band to the hollow porch wing. Actual rays traverse both the band and this portal; a merely hollow band ending at a solid porch wall is not accepted.

[Middot4:5,7](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Middot.4.5) describes the northern mesibbah, southern water-descent context and the route around the building. These two open bands are only display/service envelopes within the existing model. They are **not** a completed mesibbah reconstruction, an assertion that both sides were symmetric pedestrian corridors, or a model of actual drainage. Exact ramp profile, roof ascent, south upper door and ancient surveying remain unresolved. North/south semantic distinction is retained in the source programme; no unsupported stair or water course is invented here.

Actual geometry rays verify six first-temple chamber cavities and floor levels, the authored south entrance, all six Hasmonean rooms' connected passages, and both Herodian floor-level bands through the porch portals. Existing main-axis doors, roof heights, high windows, ritual equipment, approach clearance, DEM contact, evidence filtering, phase isolation and cached reverse identity remain in regression coverage. Four-stage GPU section/restore, portrait, all18periods and17transitions accompany this change. Historical geometry admission and complete precincts remain false.
