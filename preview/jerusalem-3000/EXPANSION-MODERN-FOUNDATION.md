# Outside-walls / modern paired foundation

This package replaces the two remaining later-era anonymous footprint grids and provisional inheritance overlays with the existing independent skeleton compiler, shared DEM, ledger publisher and comparison map. It does not complete modern Jerusalem or certify historical continuity.

## Paired layout

Both snapshots explicitly register the same six authored Ottoman old-city district envelopes and five route contexts from the preceding pack. This supplies a stable visible background, not proof that all buildings or uses survived. IDs remain phase-scoped; adjacent object fates are unresolved. A model background is not a verified reuse link.

The outside-walls snapshot adds three district contexts (southwest extra-mural housing, westward roadside development, northwest compound context) and three connecting paths. The modern snapshot retains those authored layout contexts and adds two western/northwestern envelopes and a westward connector. Totals: 9 districts / 8 paths and 11 districts / 9 paths. These are design envelopes and terrain-following coarse cells, not historical building counts, measured neighborhood boundaries or reconstructed individual monuments.

Coverage is explicitly a local old-city-and-western-environs study window. Eastern neighborhoods, distant northern/southern/western districts, full transport networks, exact walls and public cores remain gaps. Empty space is unmodeled, not uninhabited. No administrative or political boundaries are encoded. Modern is a composite 20th-century-to-present phase, not a simultaneous reconstruction of all those dates.

## Source context

- [Mishkenot Sha'ananim preservation/cultural project](https://jerusalemfoundation.org/old-project/mishkenot-shaananim-cultural-center-guest-house/) provides the nineteenth-century extra-mural residential context.
- [IAA Russian Compound excavation](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=26618) supplies a local site context, not a full measured compound plan.
- [OpenJerusalem historical letter catalogue](https://archives.openjerusalem.org/index.php/mikhailov-asks-police-to-prohibit-to-cabmen-to-use-jaffa-road-during-explosions-for-construction-of-cistern) supplies a Jaffa Road / Russian Compound historical context. Catalogue search was available; the page fetch was unavailable during implementation. It is corroborative, not the authority for model geometry.
- [Daniel and Render, transport planning study](https://onlinepubs.trb.org/onlinepubs/circulars/ec058/15_04_Daniel.pdf), pp.763–767, describes growth along arterial routes, the western Jaffa corridor, and hilltop/valley constraints. It is a study of its contemporary planning context (2003), not a current transport service or network certification.

Sources constrain direction and relationships only. Polygon coordinates, connector shapes, partitions, road widths and six-metre mass heights are authored illustrations. No windmill, compound building or light-rail route is generated from these citations. The old-city source links remain attached to its copied design context; extra-mural features carry their own context links.

## Comparison and checks

The fixed map window for all 18 periods expands to X[-2000,1100], Z[-1300,1300] metres in the existing canonical frame so the new local envelope is not clipped. The six earlier independent phases keep their prior 3D camera union; the two expansion phases share a separate, larger union camera. Crossing groups refits the camera; returning restores the same group framing. All share the same terrain and metric map, with portrait-aware framing.

The source pack is fetched once through the existing loader. No remote imagery, model or terrain service is added. Geometry is created locally for the selected phase. Token/traffic/FPS savings are not measured.

Generator checks cover eight distinct phase envelopes, exact old-city background coordinates, source role registration, outward extent, road/reservation exclusion, stable rebuild identities, evidence restoration and publication rollback. Browser checks cover all 18 periods plus direct portrait entry, reverse switching through the pair and previous six, group camera restoration, filtering and single pack requests. Historical foundation acceptance remains false; architecture detail stays paused.

Next: audit the full 18-era / 17-adjacent-transition spatial matrix and prioritize missing districts, wall/water/core domains and unregistered historical linework before claiming whole-timeline continuity. These local later snapshots do not close the Hellenistic, early-period or modern coverage gaps.
