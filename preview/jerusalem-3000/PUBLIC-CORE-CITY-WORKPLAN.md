## Construction sequence

1. First/early second/Herodian temple groups: core, courts, exits and main-street/gate interfaces together before interior ornament.
2. Roman/Byzantine/early Islamic groups: civic axes, Sepulchre/Nea and sacred/administrative precincts together. Research eastern and western Cardo separately; do not stretch a local excavation into a citywide colonnade.
3. Crusader/Mamluk/Ottoman/exterior/modern: admit citadel, gate, market, school and service ensembles with phase-specific use, demolition, repair and uncertainty.

Every batch reviews18 completed stages and17 adjacent pairs. Same model count is not a completion criterion. New surveyed forms still need sources, registered plans, dimensions and dating. Missing evidence remains a gap.

Source entry points: [candidate programme](data/landmark-building-programme.v1.json), [six-candidate review](PUBLIC-BUILDING-SOURCE-REVIEW.md), [IAA2021 Eastern Cardo](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=25927&mag_id=133), [IAA2025 Western Cardo](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=27699). The latter two are research entry points only; no colonnade is added in this package.
