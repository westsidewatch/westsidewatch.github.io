# B33 — dated Holy Sepulchre architectural shells

Continues major-landmark block-mass reduction. The round hall and adjoining buildings previously remained solid cylinders/boxes despite having a dome. This package changes the internal construction and roof/entrance readability, not the display site anchor or date authority.

## Distinct states, same source policy

Existing [Custodia history](https://www.custodia.org/en/sanctuaries/basilica-of-the-holy-sepulchre/) remains the date/context authority. The five-aisle early basilica has literary/architectural context, also described in [L'Osservatore Romano](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/en/news/2026-06/ing-006/on-the-rock-of-golgotha.html). Those sources do not authenticate the inherited display positions/dimensions or newly authored door/column/window profiles.

- **Early Constantinian context:** an actual hollow round hall, floor, east/west portals, annular roof and double-surface dome replace the solid cylinder. The eastern rectangle becomes a five-aisle shell with eastern entrances and an authored central western connection. Existing70×45m display envelope,16m maximum hall height and32m rotunda dome budget remain. A complete Martyrium/atrium/Golgotha reconstruction is not claimed.
- **1009fragments:** existing open partial low rings remain. No new intact dome, floor or eastern hall is generated for this state.
- **1048rebuild:** two former solid small chapel boxes become hollow rooms with openings. Authored cardinal portals connect them to the round-hall context. Number/form/precise circulation of these chapels are not source-certified.
- **Post1149/later context:** the adjoining rectangle becomes an interpretive hollow hall, not a claimed surveyed Catholikon plan. The old5m solid overlap into the rotunda is removed while retaining the union's eastern edge. A formerly solid bell mass becomes a lower shell, open upper belfry and shallow hip roof within its previous12×12×25m display budget. Belfry profile/sections are authored.

The rotunda outer radius20m, body15m and dome17m radius remain inherited **display choices**, not recovered ancient measurements. Inner wall radius19.2m, floor0.12m, portal sectors/4m lintel, annular roof and0.2m inner-dome offset are authored. Cardinal portal arrangements differ by selected profile. No statement that all these exact elements survived across eras.
