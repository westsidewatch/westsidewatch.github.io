## Visible change and limits

The existing exterior envelope, relative anchor, maximum roof height and post-543 display offset remain unchanged. Interior aisle widths (17/23/17 m), portal widths and roof elevations are authored distribution within the existing envelope, not excavated measurements. The metadata exposes these limits and retains all unverified registration flags.

The shared hall builder accepts optional portal widths; other churches retain their defaults. The asynchronously generated later city now refreshes its caption and landmark controls after the current phase is published, so a paused newly selected phase is not left with stale explanatory controls.
