Continues the user priority to remove major-landmark box masses across eras. Nea and the selected later Aqsa forms had pitched roofs/domes, but their principal halls were solid five/seven rectangular blocks. The new shared engineering shell does not merge their historical identities or create evidence of original fabric survival.

## Actual architecture instead of filled volumes

- Each main hall now has a floor, external walls, real entrance openings, interior column rows, beams and independent roof slabs. Upper roof steps have open clerestory bays with supporting piers. Longitudinal aisles are physically clear.
- Slab/wall/column sizes, bay spacing, door proportions and clerestory configurations are authored. No exact archaeological facade reconstruction is asserted.
- Nea's three previously solid semicylinders become curved wall/roof envelopes. The main apse is physically reached through the central rear opening; precise side-chapel circulation remains unfinished. Flat apse roof profile is retained rather than inventing conches or decoration.
- The later eastern Aqsa addition formerly overlapped6m inside the last aisle. The overlapping solid is removed; the external part becomes a separate hollow bay inside the same total object envelope. No main-hall resize or relocated mosque. Its exact medieval plan and connection remain unverified.
- Existing ground adaptation, court support, object IDs, evidence/lifecycle and cached world ownership remain. New `Nea近看` / `禮拜堂近看` controls face the north entrances of Aqsa and west front of Nea; the Nea model still appears only in its existing post543context.

No external models/textures or runtime source downloads. All later period variants retain independent records and their original chronology; same geometry grammar is not a surviving-masonry claim.
